FAQ

Good to know.

Do you take walk-ins?

Yes. Walk-ins are welcome during opening hours: Tuesday to Friday 9 AM – 6 PM and Saturday 9 AM – 12 PM.

Can I book an appointment?

Yes. Call (985) 262-0942 to book a time. Customers say an appointment means little or no wait.

When are you open?

Tuesday to Friday from 9 AM to 6 PM and Saturday from 9 AM to 12 PM. The shop is closed on Sunday and Monday. Hours may change on holidays, so call ahead if you're not sure.

Do you cut kids' hair?

Yes. Kids are welcome, and customers often bring their sons in for haircuts at the same visit.

Can I get my beard done too?

Yes. Ask for beard work along with your haircut.

Where do I park, and is the shop accessible?

Barbershop 315 is at 1161 St Charles St in Houma, with wheelchair-accessible parking and a wheelchair-accessible entrance.

How much does a haircut cost?

Prices aren't listed online yet. Call (985) 262-0942 or ask in the shop.
