Info & FAQ

Before youget inked

Deposits, pricing, walk-ins, aftercare and how we keep you safe. Still have a question? Call or text 541-944-5433 or email info@epicink.com.

Cash only

We accept cash only, and there's an ATM in the studio. Any balance above your deposit is due at the time of service.

72-hour notice

Deposits are non-refundable. Reschedule by email at info@epicink.com with a full 72 hours' notice, regardless of emergencies, illness or weather.

18+ with photo ID

ID is required by law. Arriving under the influence or without ID cancels the appointment and forfeits the deposit.

Be on time

No-shows, cancelling with insufficient notice or arriving 15+ minutes late forfeit the deposit, as it disrupts other appointments.

No numbing

Our artists have a “no numbing” policy. Using numbing agents means cancellation or rescheduling and possible loss of your deposit.

Non-transferable

Deposits can't be transferred to another person. Substantial art changes after booking may mean extra art fees or a new date.

Frequently asked

Everything else

Walk-ins

If we have openings, we accept walk-ins for tattoos on a first come, first served basis, usually Tuesday to Saturday, 11:30 am to 6 pm.

You can call, email or text to check availability. The tattoo request form gets the highest priority. Walk-in availability is not guaranteed.

Booking an appointment

The best way to get price quotes, consultations and appointments is the tattoo request form. If we're available, we can also book in the studio with a $100 cash deposit, applied to your tattoo.

We're usually tattooing from noon on, so customer support after that depends on our availability.

What's your soonest opening?

It changes daily. We have multiple artists with completely independent schedules, so tattoo dates can range from “today” to months out depending on your request and your artist's availability. The best way to know is to talk to your artist, fill out the request form, come in, email or text us.

Free consultations

Consultations are free and a great way to meet an artist, discuss your idea and set the time, price and date. They're available by appointment Tuesday to Saturday at 11:30 am or 12:00 pm.

Not every tattoo needs an in-person consultation; many can be discussed by phone or email. Between 12 and 6 pm we're usually tattooing, so walk-in consultations depend on artist availability.

Pricing

We work on a sliding scale: prices are set by time, size and level of complexity, and designs can be made larger, smaller or modified to meet your budget.

“Flat rate” is a set price agreed in advance with your artist, regardless of time. “Hourly rate” is prorated hourly, usually with a minimum of 2 or more hours per session. Lower rates may be available through our apprentice/student artist(s).

Groups & friends

Groups are welcome to book separate appointments with separate artists at the same time, or back to back appointments with the same artist.

Numbing creams

Our artists currently have a “no numbing” policy. Numbing products can make skin rubbery, red and irritated, affect healing, and the FDA has warned consumers about certain topical numbing products.

Use of any numbing agent will result in cancellation or rescheduling of your appointment and possible forfeiture of your full deposit.

Memorial tattoos & ashes

Putting ashes in a tattoo is against the law in Oregon. We'd love to work closely with you on a special design that celebrates the positive memories of your loved one.

Minors

In Oregon you must be 18 with a valid government-issued photo ID to get tattooed, regardless of parental consent. You're still welcome to come in, ask questions and plan your design, and we'll give you free aftercare on your 18th birthday.

Sterilization

We use single-use products and never reuse inks, needles or supplies. Where applicable we sterilize equipment in an autoclave, which is spore tested monthly by an independent third party laboratory as required by Oregon law. Results are posted in the studio, and you're always welcome to see these records.

Licensing

The facility is licensed and inspected by the Oregon Health Authority and the City of Medford, and all licenses are on display in the studio. Artists are licensed and trained as required by Oregon law, with training in First Aid, CPR, OSHA, blood-borne pathogens, universal precautions and sterilization.

Should I shop around on price?

Price shopping is encouraged, but it's a “buyer beware” market. Look closely at sterilization and at the artist's portfolio. Getting tattooed by an amateur at home can be very dangerous.

Gift cards

Every tattoo is different, so we prefer to talk through your ideas before selling a gift card. Call 541-944-5433 to create the perfect gift. Each artist has their own gift card protocol, and gift certificates are non-refundable.

Tattoo aftercare

Heal it right

We recommend Aquaphor Ointment and Dial Antibacterial Soap, both for sale at Epic. Don't use any other products; if you're sensitive to Aquaphor, ask your artist.

Congratulations on your new epic tattoo & thank you!

  1. Remove the bandage in 2–4 hoursWith clean hands. Do not re-bandage.
  2. Wash gently with DialRemove any blood or plasma from the surface.
  3. Blot dry, thin layer of AquaphorA very thin layer is all you need.
  4. Repeat 2–3 times a dayUntil fully healed, about 2–4 weeks.
  • Don't soak it in baths, lakes, rivers, pools or hot tubs until healed. Showering is fine; keep it out of the water as much as possible.
  • No picking or scratching. A “dull & waxy” look and some flaking are normal while new skin regenerates.
  • Ice packs (dry, not wet) help with swelling. Wear loose clothing and avoid sun; use sunblock once healed.
