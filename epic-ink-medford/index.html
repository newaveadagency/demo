Good to know

Questions, answered

Do you take walk-ins?

Yes, on a first come, first served basis when an artist is open, usually Tuesday to Saturday, 11:30 am to 6 pm. You can call, text or email to check availability. The tattoo request form gets the highest priority, and walk-in availability is never guaranteed.

How much will my tattoo cost?

Prices depend on time, size and level of complexity. Your artist will quote either a flat rate (a set price agreed in advance) or an hourly rate (prorated, usually with a 2-hour minimum per session). Tell us the price range you're comfortable with and we'll design to fit your budget.

How do I pay?

Epic Ink is cash only. There's an ATM inside the studio. Any balance above your deposit is due at the time of service.

Is a consultation free?

Yes. Consultations are free and are a pressure-free way to meet an artist, talk through your idea and set the time, price and date. Book online with Mark or Kory, or fill out the tattoo request form.

How old do I have to be?

In Oregon you must be 18 with a valid government-issued photo ID to get tattooed, regardless of parental consent. Younger visitors are welcome to come in, ask questions and plan ahead, and we'll even give you free aftercare on your 18th birthday.
