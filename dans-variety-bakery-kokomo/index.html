Good to know

Before you come in.

Do you take cards?

No. Dan's takes cash and checks only.

How do I make sure my order is ready?

Call in your order at (765) 453-4591 before 1 a.m. to guarantee it.

How much is a dozen?

A dozen standard donuts is $12 and a half dozen is $7.20. Apple fritters, pecan danish and donut holes are not included in the dozen price. Donut holes are $2 per half-dozen or $3.50 per dozen.

When can I get pecan rolls and tiger tails?

Pecan rolls are made on Fridays and Sundays only. Tiger tails are Saturday only.

What are your hours?

Tuesday through Sunday, 1 a.m. to 12 p.m. Closed on Mondays.
