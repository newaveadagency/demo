In the store

Walk the aisles.

Every department here comes from the store's own photos. Stock changes with the seasons, so if you need something specific, call 802-744-2223 before you drive over.

01 · Groceries & pantry

Staples on every shelf.

Aisles of canned goods, pasta, sauces and boxed groceries, so a missing ingredient is a short trip, not a drive out of town.

  • Pasta and pasta sauces
  • Canned goods
  • Boxed groceries

02 · Beer, wine & cold drinks

Something cold, something to share.

A wall of red and white wines and a row of glass-door coolers with canned and bottled drinks.

  • Wine wall with reds and whites
  • Glass-door coolers with cold drinks
  • Beer and seltzers in the coolers

03 · Snacks

For the road or the game.

A full rack of chips and salty snacks.

  • Potato chips
  • Tortilla chips
  • Salty snacks

04 · Household

The things you always run out of.

Paper goods, cleaning supplies, laundry detergent, plates, cups and cutlery.

  • Paper towels and plates
  • Cleaning sprays and laundry
  • Party cups and cutlery

05 · Health & personal care

A small pharmacy shelf, close to home.

Over-the-counter basics on the pegboard, with personal care on the shelves below.

  • Pain relief, cold and allergy
  • Toothbrushes and personal care
  • Batteries and reading glasses

06 · Winter, car & camp

Ready for a Vermont winter.

The hardware-store corner of a general store: rock salt for the steps, washer fluid and oil for the truck, propane cylinders and charcoal for camp, and tape and cords for quick fixes.

  • Rock salt and windshield washer fluid
  • Motor oil, 2-cycle oil and fuel additives
  • Propane cylinders and charcoal
  • Duct tape, electrical tape and extension cords
