Good to know

Questions

What are the store hours?

Monday to Friday 5 AM to 8 PM, Saturday 6 AM to 8 PM and Sunday 7 AM to 8 PM.

Do you take SNAP/EBT?

Yes. The store accepts SNAP/EBT as well as credit cards, debit cards and tap-to-pay mobile payments.

Do you deliver?

Delivery is listed as a service option. Call the store at 802-744-2223 to ask about it.

Where are you and is there parking?

17 North Hill Road, Westfield, VT 05874. There is a wheelchair-accessible parking space, and the store has a restroom.
