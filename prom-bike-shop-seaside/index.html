Our story

Your local, friendly, full-service bike shop.

Located in Seaside, on Oregon's scenic North Coast, the Prom Bike Shop has been proudly serving all bicyclists since the 1930s. We are one of the oldest bike shops in Oregon, and family owned and operated since 1968.

  • A mechanic on staff daily for all your repairs
  • A large selection of bikes and bicycling accessories
  • Rentals for the beach and the streets, kid to adult sizes
  • Open year round, on the corner of 12th Avenue and North Holladay

Good to know

Questions we hear a lot.

Anything else? Give us a call at 503-738-8251 or email prombikeshop@yahoo.com.

Can I ride a rental on the beach?

Yes. Funcycles, single-speed beach cruisers and the beach tandem can go on Seaside's beach. Beach riding depends on high and low tide times and sunset, so check with us for the best riding times. There's easy beach access on 12th Ave.

Are helmets and locks included?

Yes. All bike rentals include free helmets and locks. Baskets are available on select models.

How long does a repair take?

Minor repairs like flat tires and small adjustments can usually be done by closing the same day or within 24 hours of drop-off. Tune-ups take longer depending on traffic flow. Visiting the area? Make an appointment ahead and get your repair done while you're here.

Do you work on e-bikes?

We'll gladly work on the bicycle portions of your e-bike that we have parts in stock for, and we can adjust the bicycle portion of your e-bike.

Can I buy something online?

We're unable to sell online, but we can hold an item for you to pick up. Stock changes, so call to check availability.

Do I need a reservation?

Reservations are advised for extended rentals and for road bikes. For everything else, just come by during opening hours.
