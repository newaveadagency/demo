Repair basics

Drop it off, ride it home.

  1. Free estimate. Bring your bike in and our mechanic will look it over and tell you what it needs.
  2. Minor repairs, fast. Flat tires and small adjustments can usually be done by closing the same day or within 24 hours of drop-off.
  3. Tune-ups take a little longer, depending on traffic flow in the shop.
  4. Visiting the area? Make an appointment ahead and get your repair done while you're here.

And air for your tires is always free. It makes a big difference in your ride.

In the workshop

What we work on.

Full tune-up

Single or multi speed

Inspection of all major bearing areas with adjustments as needed, complete drivetrain cleaning (off the bike), wheels removed, cleaned and trued as needed, front and rear brakes and gears adjusted or new cables installed if needed, then a bike wash, polish and reassembly.

Wheel building

Built by hand, onsite

Whether you just need spokes replaced or a whole wheel rebuilt, we can help with the project. Our spoke cutter handles 14 or 15 gauge spokes and can cut any length you need.

E-bikes

The bicycle portion

We'll gladly work on the bicycle portions of your e-bike that we have parts in stock for, and adjust the bicycle portion of your e-bike.

Quick fixes

Often same day

Flat tires, brake and gear adjustments: usually ready by closing the same day or within 24 hours of drop-off.
