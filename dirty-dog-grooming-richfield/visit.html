Step by step

Five easy steps

No surprises for you or your dog. Vanessa likes to know who she’s grooming before the clippers come out.

1

Get in touch

Call (385) 377-0617, email Vanessa, or send the request form below. The shop is open Monday to Friday, 8 AM to 5 PM.

2

Tell us about your dog

Before scheduling, Vanessa asks about your dog’s past grooming experiences: anything they’re scared of, sensitive spots, matting, age.

3

Pick the look

At drop-off you fill in a short information form with sample cuts to choose from, so the result matches what you pictured.

4

Groom day

Bath, brush, de-shed or haircut, at your dog’s pace. Nervous dogs and first-timers are taken slowly.

5

Pick up a new dog

Clean, fluffy and often wearing a bandana. Credit and debit cards are accepted.

Good to know

Questions & answers

Where is Dirty Dog Grooming?

At 460 Westview Dr, Richfield, UT 84701. Look for the white door with “Dirty Dog Grooming” and “Please Come In” on it.

What are your hours?

Monday to Friday, 8 AM to 5 PM. Closed Saturday and Sunday. Hours can differ on holidays, so call ahead.

How much does a groom cost?

It depends on your dog’s breed, size and coat condition. Call (385) 377-0617 and Vanessa will give you a price for your dog.

Do you groom large dogs?

Yes. Vanessa grooms big dogs as well as small ones, including huskies, shepherds and Australian shepherds.

My dog is nervous or has never been groomed. Is that OK?

Yes. Tell Vanessa about your dog’s past experiences when you book. Nervous dogs and first-timers are taken slowly until they’re at ease.

How do I pay?

Credit and debit cards are accepted.
