Meet your groomer

Hi, I’m Vanessa

Dirty Dog Grooming is Vanessa Wells, professional groomer, in a bright pink grooming room at 460 Westview Drive in Richfield, with her husband Tracy lending a hand.

Before your first appointment, Vanessa asks about your dog’s past grooming experiences. On the day, you fill in a short form and pick the look you want from sample cuts. Nervous first-timers get to go slow, matted coats get patience, and senior dogs get extra care.

What we do

From muddy to pretty

Every groom starts with your dog: breed, coat, temperament and what you want them to look like when you pick them up.

01

Full groom & haircut

Bath, brush-out and the cut you picked from the sample styles, finished with a bandana.

02

Bath & spa day

A good wash and tidy for dogs who just need to be clean and smell great again.

03

De-shedding

Huskies and other double coats get a thorough de-shed, so less of that undercoat ends up on your floor.

04

Matted & sensitive coats

Matted fur full of dirt and stickers is worked on gently, at your dog’s pace.

05

Seniors & first-timers

Older dogs and puppies on their first cut are handled slowly and calmly until they feel at ease.

06

Bandanas & color pops

Fun finishing touches, from a fresh bandana to red, blue or purple ears. Ask Vanessa what’s possible.

Prices depend on breed, size and coat. Call for a quote.(385) 377-0617
