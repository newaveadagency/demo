[03] About

Owner-operated. Built on trust, one lock at a time.

Cactus Lock & Key has offered skilled, affordable locksmith service to residents of the Phoenix metropolitan area for over 30 years.

[07] FAQ

Before you call.

Are you really available 24/7?

Yes. Emergency lockout service runs 24 hours a day, 7 days a week, for homes, businesses and cars anywhere in the Valley.

Do you give free estimates?

Yes. Estimates are free, and same-day service is available.

Are you licensed, bonded and insured?

Yes. Cactus Lock & Key is a licensed, bonded and insured company.

How can I pay?

Credit cards (Visa, MasterCard, American Express, Discover), cash and checks. Financing is available.

Can I combine discounts?

Senior citizen and military discounts are limited to one discount per transaction. The $10 new-customer discount applies to services over $100.

Can you help with car keys and remotes?

Yes: car lockouts, broken key extraction, duplicate car keys and master keys, auto door lock repair, and programming keyless entry remotes.
