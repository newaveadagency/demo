§ 02 Mardi Gras

King cakes, picked up in Alexandria or shipped across the country.

The season officially begins on the Twelfth Night of Christmas, January 6, and runs until Fat Tuesday. Be sure to order early: the party will be over before you know it.

  • Pick upOrder ahead and collect at the bakery on MacArthur Drive.
  • ShipNationwide shipping during the season. Each ship-to address needs its own cart.
  • TraditionPurple for justice, green for faith, gold for power. Whoever finds the baby is King for the day and throws the next party.

§ 04 Our story

It began with a hand-painted sign on the roof.

For over 40 years Atwood’s Bakery has been in Central Louisiana to help people enjoy friends, family, festivities and food.

We began with a tiny shop on 1st Street in Pineville that just had cake and candy supplies and a hand-painted sign on the roof. Today we have a beautiful location on MacArthur Drive with room for guests to browse and enjoy a variety of cakes, bakery items and deli delights.

§ 06 Good to know

Questions, answered.

01Do you ship king cakes?
Yes. During Mardi Gras season we ship king cakes nationwide. Order online and choose “Packed for Shipping”. Each separate ship-to address needs to be a separate cart and transaction.
02When is king cake season?
The Mardi Gras season officially begins on the Twelfth Night of Christmas, January 6, and continues until Fat Tuesday. Order early.
03Can I order online?
Yes. Order cakes, king cakes, pastries and deli food at atwoods.bakesmart.com, or order café food for in-store pickup on Menufy. Deli and a few cakes are available online the same day; if you need something sooner, call 318-445-5134.
04When is the café and deli open?
Tuesday through Saturday, 7:00 am to 1:45 pm. Some items, such as soups, omelets, shrimp & grits and eggs Benedict, are only available on certain days and times. Sunday and Monday we are usually closed.
05Do you make Valentine’s strawberries?
Yes. We dip strawberries in milk, dark and white chocolate, starting very early on Valentine’s Day morning. Our Sweetheart Collection is made only a few dozen at a time, so pre-orders are encouraged.
