Good to know

Questions we hear a lot.

Do you really come to me?

Yes. Caro's Auto Glass is a mobile company. We bring the service to your home, your workplace or wherever the vehicle is parked.

Do I need an appointment?

Yes, appointments are required. Call the office at 417-834-6974 or text the cell at 417-872-5445 and we'll set a time.

What are your hours? Do you work Saturdays?

Monday to Friday, 8 AM to 5 PM. Saturday appointments are available by request, so just ask.

Can a chip be repaired instead of replacing the windshield?

Often, yes. Smaller chips can be filled so they don't keep spreading. Send us a description or a photo and we'll tell you whether a repair or a new windshield makes sense.

Do you work on trucks, vans and fleets?

Yes. We offer commercial fleet services and work on all makes and models, including semi-truck windshields.

How should I treat a new windshield afterwards?

We'll tell you on the spot how to care for the new glass until the sealant sets. The blue tape you'll see is there to hold things in place while it does.

How can I pay?

Credit cards, debit cards and NFC mobile payments (tap to pay).

Do you guarantee your work?

Yes, we guarantee our work. Ask us about the details when you book.
