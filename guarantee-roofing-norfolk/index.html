About Guarantee Roofing

Our employees are the foundation on which all good roof systems are built.

From the office personnel to the field roofers and sheet metal workers, we strive to give every customer the quality, service and performance they deserve. We provide complete services and design for low slope to steep slope roofs: hot tar, EPDM, TPO, metal standing seam, and sub structures that put a sloped roof on an existing flat roof.

Guarantee Roofing is a multiple-year winner of the Firestone “Master Contractor” designation, and our installers bring a combined 250+ years of experience to every job.

Roofing systems

No matter what you need in a roof, we can provide it.

New roofs, reroofs and maintenance for commercial buildings, big and small, including older downtown buildings that need a cost-effective, minimally disruptive fix for a leaky, failing roof.

01

Single-ply

EPDM rubber roofing, TPO heat-welded systems and PVC single ply.

02

Hot roof & mod bit

Hot tar and modified bitumen roofs, with tear-off and removal of the old roof.

03

Metal & retro-fit

Architectural standing seam, retro-fit metal roofing and sub structures that turn a flat roof into a sloped one.

04

Evaluations & repairs

Roof inspections, maintenance, repairs and reroofing, plus taper insulation systems.
