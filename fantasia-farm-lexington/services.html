Full boarding

A clean, calm barn and room to graze

All the stalls are large, well ventilated and cleaned three times daily. Feed is fresh, high-quality oats, sweet feed and pellets, with clean alfalfa and timothy hay. Cool, clean water is available at all times.

A large, tidy aisle way holds tack trunks and supplies, and two spacious, orderly tack rooms store bridles, saddles and training equipment. Several large, grassy turnout paddocks are available, and as an owner you choose either daily or nightly turnout.

Riding lessons

From first-timer to most advanced

Choose from Western, Huntseat and Saddleseat. Our youngest student has been two; our oldest has been seventy-six. Every lesson lasts an hour and is divided into three parts:

Get ready

Grooming and tacking up. Each piece of tack is explained by name, use and relevance, and the parts of the horse’s body are identified by name and function.

Ride

Riding skills, taught at the level that fits what the student has already learned.

Cool down

Untacking and cooling down the horse, then a short summary of the day and goals for the next lesson.
