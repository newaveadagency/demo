Full boarding

Good for your horse. Easy for you.

Boarding your horse at Fantasia Farm is an excellent choice, and both of you benefit. Every stall is large and well ventilated, and the barn is kept tidy from aisle to tack room.

  • Stalls cleaned three times daily
  • Fresh oats, sweet feed and pellets, plus clean alfalfa and timothy hay
  • Cool, clean water available at all times
  • Large grassy turnout paddocks: you choose daily or nightly turnout
  • Two spacious tack rooms and a wide aisle for tack trunks
  • Jump course, schooling areas, trails and wash areas steps from the stalls

FAQ

Good to know

Anything else? Call 859-272-7751 or email info@fantasiafarmonline.com.

Who can take riding lessons?

Everyone, from the first-timer to the most advanced rider. Our youngest student has been two; our oldest has been seventy-six.

Which riding styles do you teach?

Students choose from Western, Huntseat and Saddleseat.

How is a lesson organized?

Lessons last an hour in three parts: grooming and tacking up (with tack and the horse’s body explained), riding at the level that fits the student, then untacking and cooling down, with a short recap of the day.

What is included in full board?

Large, well-ventilated stalls cleaned three times daily; oats, sweet feed and pellets with alfalfa and timothy hay; clean water at all times; two tack rooms; grassy turnout paddocks with daily or nightly turnout; a jump course, trails and wash areas next to the stables.

Are trail rides available?

Trail rides are temporarily suspended. Please call for updates.

Do I need to sign a release form?

Yes. Every rider signs the farm’s General Release of Liability (Kentucky farm animal activity law). Download the release form (PDF).
