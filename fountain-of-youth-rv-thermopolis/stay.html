The sites

Shade in summer, steam in the fall.

Many sites sit under tall cottonwoods that keep the park cooler. Sites have room for large family gatherings, and the wide roadways make it easy to get in and out.

Use the park as a central base for day trips: Hot Springs State Park and downtown Thermopolis are about 10 minutes away, Wind River Canyon is just south of town, and Yellowstone is about 170 miles.
