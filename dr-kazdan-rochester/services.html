01

Children & adult eye exams

We know your vision is important to you, and it’s important to us. We provide comprehensive eye care for families of any size or age and check your eyes extensively for any potential eye disorders or diseases, including screenings for glaucoma, retinal disorders and diabetes-related eye problems.

Our courteous, skilled staff help us deliver excellent care in a way that respects your time. Your visual skills and abilities are carefully evaluated and the right treatment is prescribed, whether that’s medication, spectacles, contact lenses or surgical intervention, using the latest diagnostic equipment.

02

Contact lenses, even for tricky prescriptions

Our professionals help fit you with lenses that are just right for you. We are experts in soft and rigid lenses as well as custom designs, and can handle the most specialized needs and difficult prescriptions.

Allergies or dry eyes making lens wear uncomfortable? New lens materials, much better lubricating drops and allergy eye medications have helped many of our patients get past the burning, itchy eyes of winter and allergy season and go back to wearing their lenses comfortably.

03

Cutting-edge eyeglass prescriptions

Choosing eyeglasses means choosing a frame, a lens type and lens coatings. The best options depend on your lifestyle, work and leisure activities, so tell us how you’ll use your glasses and we’ll find the optimal lens for you.

ZEISS technology. The i.Profilerplus® by ZEISS maps subtle imperfections in the eye to create a super-precise prescription, and the i.Terminal® 2 by ZEISS also takes into account how your frames sit on your face.
04

Optomap® retinal imaging exam

The Optomap® ultra-wide digital retinal imaging exam produces an ultra-wide-field, high-resolution image of approximately 82% of the retina in one image. These images help detect retinal detachments and tears, glaucoma, diabetic retinopathy and age-related macular degeneration, and can also show evidence of non-eye or systemic diseases such as hypertension and certain cancers.

06

Glaucoma, cataracts & diabetic eye care

Cataracts. As we mature, the eye’s natural lens gradually becomes less flexible and less clear. We diagnose and manage your cataract care, and when a cataract keeps you from reading or seeing distant objects comfortably, we recommend surgery and coordinate it with proven cataract specialists.

Glaucoma. Glaucoma gradually damages the optic nerve with little or no warning, and is painless until significant damage has occurred. We monitor for it during routine exams by measuring eye pressure, screening for visual field defects and analyzing the optic nerve. Most cases are successfully managed with eye drops alone.

Diabetes. The American Diabetes Association strongly recommends a dilated retinal exam every year for people with diabetes. We use a retinal camera to detect subtle changes, and if we find diabetic eye disease that needs treatment or further evaluation, we coordinate your care with a board-certified retinal specialist.

Eye infections, injuries & urgent care

We offer emergency services for eye infections and injuries. Microscopes let us examine the front surface of the eye and surrounding areas to determine the nature of the problem, and we explain your treatment plan, including medication, supportive care and follow-up visits as needed.

Call us during office hours: Rochester (585) 586-6524 or Spencerport (585) 352-1960. After hours: 749-7112. Our staff will work to schedule you as soon as possible within the same day.
08

Dry eye relief

Scratchy, burning, red and gritty-feeling eyes? Oral medications, reading, computer work and dry environments can aggravate dry eyes. We diagnose the condition by reviewing your symptoms and using dyes to observe your tear patterns. Treatment may include artificial tears, allergy eye drops, prescription drops for dryness and/or punctal plugs.

09

Surgery co-management & LASIK consultations

We provide consultation, referrals and co-management for surgical services such as cataract extraction, laser and refractive surgery, glaucoma and oculoplastic procedures, and work with other specialty eye doctors on retinal tears, detachments and hemorrhages, so your aftercare can happen in our conveniently located offices.

Curious about laser vision correction? LASIK is not for everyone. We’ll help you decide whether it’s right for you, guide you to the best surgeon and procedure, explore non-surgical options, and monitor your eyes after the procedure for the best possible results.
