Our story

A family business serving friends and families

Abuelita's opened its doors in 1998 in Shawnee, Oklahoma. Today there are restaurants in Shawnee and Sulphur, and the restaurant is still family owned and operated.

  1. 1998

    Doors open in Shawnee

    Abuelita's in Sulphur

Good to know

Questions & answers

When are the lunch specials served?

Monday to Saturday, 11 a.m. to 5 p.m. On Sundays and after 5 p.m., add $1.00 to any lunch special.

Where are your restaurants?

Abuelita's I is at 715 E. Independence in Shawnee, (405) 275-1157. Abuelita's Sulphur is at 2514 W. Broadway in Sulphur, (580) 622-2285.

What are your hours?

Shawnee: Sunday to Thursday 11 am – 8 pm, Friday and Saturday 11 am – 9 pm. Sulphur: Sunday to Thursday 11 am – 9 pm, Friday and Saturday 11 am – 10 pm.

Can I order a smaller portion?

Yes. The guacamole salad, all nachos and the fajitas come as a full order or a half order.

Anything for the non-Mexican food fans?

The American section has a cheeseburger, a grilled chicken sandwich and chicken strips, all served with french fries.
