Feeding a crowd?

Ask about a big order.

Office breakfast, a team, a family get-together. Jot down what you have in mind and the day you need it, then give the shop a call to talk it through.

Good to know

Questions

When are you open?

Tuesday through Sunday, 5:30 am to noon. Closed on Mondays.

Where exactly is the shop?

49 Main St, Suite A, in downtown Blanco, TX 78606. Look for the red brick front.

Is it just donuts?

No. Alongside the donuts there are kolaches, croissants, biscuits, tacos and donut sandwiches.

What kolaches do you make?

Savory kolaches such as brisket, sausage, bacon and pizza, plus sweet fruit-and-cream kolaches.
