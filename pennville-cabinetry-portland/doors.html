How we build

Chisels and CNC, under one roof.

  • Made, not bought

    Using our own tools, our cabinetmakers create virtually every wood product in our 60,000 square foot facility, from custom turnings and unique door styles to signature furniture pieces.

  • Stained by hand

    We continue to stain by hand and wax our panel doors and cabinetry the way artisans of an earlier era perfected it.

  • Reviewed every week

    Items are discussed in detail at weekly management meetings until every Pennville craftsperson agrees that each item in our catalogue is extraordinary.

  • FSC on request

    FSC products are available upon request.
