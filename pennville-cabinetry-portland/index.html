Our story

It started with a promise to build one kitchen.

In 1956 a Pennville, Indiana woodworker promised his wife he would build her a kitchen, if only she would let him buy a $47 table saw. That garage workshop became Pennville Custom Cabinetry.

The Goldman family later bought the shop, moved it from Pennville to Portland and quadrupled the factory. Since 1990 it has been led by President Mark S. Goldman, who first walked through the plant at the age of five.

Questions

Good to know.

Still wondering? Call 260-726-9357.

How can I buy Pennville cabinets?

Pennville cabinetry is sold through dealers and design partners. Call 260-726-9357 or email Mark@PennvilleCabinetry.com for the location of your nearest dealer.

Can I visit the factory?

Yes. Daily plant tours and group tours are available at 600 E. Votaw Street in Portland, Indiana. Contact Mark@PennvilleCabinetry.com to arrange one.

Do you make your own doors and parts?

Yes. Our cabinetmakers make virtually every wood product in our 60,000 square foot facility, from custom turnings and unique door styles to signature furniture pieces. We still stain by hand and wax our panel doors and cabinetry.

Do you offer FSC-certified products?

FSC products are available upon request.

Which wood species and finishes can I choose?

Our door styles are shown in species such as rift white oak, walnut, pecan, knotty alder and painted maple, as well as acrylic and textured veneers, in finishes from natural to Hale Navy. See the door styles page for examples.
