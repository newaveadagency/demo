Our specialty

Carved redwood. 23 carat gold.

We can handle nearly any type of advertisement, but our specialty is sandblasted and carved redwood signs with 23 carat gold leaf lettering and accents. These elegant signs project a strong image of confidence and style – and they’re not as expensive as you might think.

  • Available in wood or highly durable high-density polyurethane – we’ll help you choose.

Good to know

Questions, answered.

What kinds of signs do you make?

Sandblasted and carved signs, electrical and LED sign systems, real estate and job site signs, wood and high-density polyurethane signs, changeable copy signs, aluminum, corrugated plastic and PVC signs, banners, window lettering, magnetic signs, billboards, wall murals, trade show displays and complete sign systems.

What is your specialty?

Sandblasted and carved redwood signs with 23 carat gold leaf lettering and accents. They are available in wood or in highly durable high-density polyurethane, and we help you decide which material fits best.

Can you letter big trucks and trailers?

Yes. We letter pickups, vans and tractor-trailers, and our shop space can handle trailers up to 53 feet. We also do full and partial vehicle wraps and pinstripe accents for truck cabs and vans.

How long will my vehicle be off the road?

Most pickups and vans can be completed in one day. We work with you to find the best time for your vehicle graphics so your deliveries stay on schedule.

Do I need my own artwork?

No. We can help with the concept and artwork, or work with your design team to get the best possible result.

Do you print shirts and team apparel?

Yes. We offer one-color and multi-color screen printing plus embroidery on t-shirts, sweatshirts, hats, jackets, blankets and tote bags, and we handle group sales items for sports teams, booster clubs and Parent Teacher Associations.
