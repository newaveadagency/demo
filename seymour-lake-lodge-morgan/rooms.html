Accommodations · 28 Valley Road, Morgan

Rooms witha lake view.

Located in a remote setting directly across the street from Lake Seymour and its half-mile of beach: great views of the lake and mountain ranges, lakefront mountain air and area wildlife.

Guest rooms

from $95/night

Nine guest rooms, each decorated with 19th- and 20th-century antiques. Rate from $95.00 per night for a single.

  • Continental breakfast 7–9 am
  • Full kitchen privileges in the community kitchen
  • Private entrance, alarm clock

Guest apartment

up to $155/night

For a little more, rent the second-floor guest apartment with its own private full kitchen and a balcony, great for breakfast with tremendous lake and mountain views.

  • Private full kitchen
  • Balcony with lake & mountain views
  • Second floor
