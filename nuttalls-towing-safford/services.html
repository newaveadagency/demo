Before you call

Have this ready and it goes faster.

  • 1Get safe first. Off the travel lane if you can, hazard lights on, and stay clear of traffic.
  • 2Where you are. Highway and mile marker, or the nearest cross street in town.
  • 3What you drive. Car, truck, moped, motorcycle or ATV, and whether it rolls.
  • 4Where it should go. A shop, your home, or the Nuttall’s yard on S 7th Ave.
