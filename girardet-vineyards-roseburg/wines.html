Red wines

The reds.

Fermented in small open-top vats, hand-punched twice a day, aged 9 to 30 months in small French oak barrels.

01

14 Vines

Our secret, old family recipe using 14 different grapes. A medium-bodied, incredibly versatile Rhone-style blend. Formerly known as the Grand Rouge.

02

Blend #71

A Super-Tuscan style wine and a tribute to 1971, the year the first grapevines were planted on the estate.

03

Baco Noir

Aromas of juicy blackberries and dark roasted coffee; plum and blueberry, finishing with toasted vanilla and chocolate. For most it is love at first sip.

White wines

The whites.

Whole-cluster pressed, fermented cool at 45–55 °F and aged entirely in stainless steel.

01

Cayuga

The West Coast’s first and only Cayuga! Fresh, floral and nicely balanced.

02

Riesling

Light floral aromas, finishing with minerals and dry, bright acidity.

03

Seyval Blanc

Pineapple lingers in a bright, zesty finish. Dry with excellent acidity – for spicier food or as an aperitif.

04

Pinot Gris

Delicate aromas of lemon meringue and green apple, with a hint of pear.

05

Chardonnay

Brilliant light yellow-green. Flinty wet stone and lemongrass lead to a steely mouthfeel that is both soft and intense.

How we make it

Back to the basics.

01 · VINEYARD

By hand

All vines are hand-harvested and hand-pruned, watered by hand for two years, then dry-farmed to produce the most flavorful grapes.

02 · WHITES

Cool & bright

Hand-sorted, pressed whole-cluster as gently as possible, fermented at 45–55 °F and aged in stainless steel for fresh, vibrant acidity.

03 · REDS

Punched down

About 30% whole berries in small open-top vats, hand-punched twice a day, 12–21 days of maceration, pressed by gravity flow.

04 · FINISH

Bottled here

Every Girardet wine is bottled, packaged and cellared on-site before release.
