The studio

Plenty of northern light, over Panache on Main Street.

The Yoga Place opened in September 2003. It is spacious and peaceful, and our teachers cultivate safety and warmth in their teaching.

Classes are a contemplative blend of asana, pranayama and meditation, rooted in the richness of yogic tradition. We like to keep it simple and only sell yoga mats, yoga ties, eyebags and water to support your practice. The decor is influenced by many visits to Asia.

We believe that life is good, and that practice on the yoga mat offers opportunities to open to new, more joyful ways of living our lives off the mat.

Your first class

There are no rules for a good yoga practice.

Our teachers will help even the newest beginner feel welcome and supported in their learning. Here is everything you need to know before you come.

How to find us
Do I need to sign up in advance?

All classes may be attended on a drop-in basis, so please feel welcome to any class on the schedule. To register or ask a question, call (802) 447-0393 or email jane.schaeffer@gmail.com.

I have never done yoga. Is there a class for me?

Yes. Very Beginner Yoga is designed for those new to yoga, those with limitations of any sort and those who are a bit shy of trying yoga. Our teachers will help even the newest beginner feel welcome and supported.

What should I bring and wear?

If you have your own sticky mat, please bring it. Otherwise, you can buy or rent one at the studio. Wear comfortable clothing with no zippers or belt buckles. Bottled water is available.

Should I eat before class?

Eat very lightly or not at all before a class. Expect to feel deeply relaxed and energized by the end of class.

Is there a changing room?

We offer changing space, but we do not have showers.

Where do I park and how do I get in?

Our street address is 532 Main St., but we suggest you enter in the back off Pleasant St. The parking is easier and it is easier to find us: go in the back door and upstairs. We are located over Panache.

Can I take a class online?

Yes, Zoom is available for some classes. They are marked with a Z on the schedule.
