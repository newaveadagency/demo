Class descriptions

Yoga means to join together.

All physical yoga is called Hatha Yoga: Ha means sun and Tha means moon in Sanskrit. There are various styles of teaching yoga, each one enhancing self-development and self-awareness. You decide which is the best fit for you at this time in your life.

Very Beginner Yoga

Designed for those new to yoga, those with limitations of any sort and those who are a bit shy of trying yoga. A friendly class with an emphasis on body, mind and spirit awareness. A beginning centering, warm-ups, simple yoga postures and deep relaxation form the core of this class.

Vinyasa Flow

A popular, evolving form of traditional Hatha yoga that integrates breath and movement, awareness and alignment, strength and flexibility, moving the body through its entire range of motion on the wave of the breath. Classes include asana, Victorious Breath (ujjayi pranayama), drishti and bandhas. All levels welcome.

Easy Does It Yoga

This class focuses on the Sanskrit meaning of the word “asana”: steady and at ease. We explore coming to a place of steadiness and ease in all our postures.

Chair Yoga

Yoga done in a chair, or standing and using a chair for stability. Just like any yoga class, chair yoga can give you more flexibility, physical and mental strength, energy and a sense of wellbeing.

Yin Yang Yoga

An all levels class that offers something for everyone: postures that focus on connective tissues and joints (yin) and building strength by blending poses into a slow flow with some holdings (yang). Props and variations are offered, and each class ends with a soothing relaxation.

Restorative Yoga

Melt into soothing, well-supported poses, using props such as bolsters and blankets to create a completely supportive environment for total relaxation, with Thai Massage sprinkled into each pose.

Deep Integrative Relaxation (Yoga Nidra)

A 4-week class that draws you into a state of being where you are fully alert and deeply relaxed, as if asleep and awake at the same time. At the simplest level, you will learn how to relax very deeply.

Beginner Meditation

Offered periodically. An experiential and educational program geared to bring the busy mind into moment-to-moment awareness, with an emphasis on mindfulness: the body scan, walking meditation and eating meditation.

Bigger Bodies Yoga

If yoga has seemed like a daunting leap, join us for this introduction to a gentle practice, with discussion of yoga and modifications and adjustments to comfort. Yoga truly is for everyone.

Meditation with Bhante

Fridays at 12 noon, meditations are guided by Bhante Dhammarathana, the Green Mountain Monk. Free or by donation.

Our instructors

The teachers of The Yoga Place.

Jane Schaeffer, MA, RYT

Originator of The Yoga Place

A Licensed Psychologist-Master and a Registered Professional Level Yoga Teacher with a 500-hour certification. Along with teaching yoga for over 30 years, she has taught Mindfulness Based Stress Reduction and Relaxation for over 20 years, as well as beginner meditation. She has trained with Amrit Desai at the Amrit Institute, and, also an anthropologist, has traveled throughout Asia.

“Yoga brings me home to myself. It is a great privilege to teach and learn so deeply from the many students of the Yoga Place.”

Allison Rogge

200 hour RYT · Vinyasa Flow

Allison has always been a yoga teacher at heart, having worked as a Registered Nurse for over 30 years. Her classes deliver good vibes, good flows, mobility and laughter. She is a 200-hour Yoga Alliance certified instructor, and her positive attitude and calming energy provide a welcoming class environment.

Angela Rocca

Certified Kripalu Yoga Teacher · Yin Yang Yoga

“I’m excited to introduce yoga to someone for the very first time as well as to teach experienced practitioners how to explore the edges of their practice.” A Berkshire County native, Angie’s teaching intertwines the Kripalu, Yin Yoga, Vinyasa and Kundalini schools of yoga.

Nancy Brooks

Vinyasa Flow Yoga

Nancy has been teaching yoga for many years. She is a 200-hour RYT from Beth Shaw’s YogaFit program and holds a 300-hour RYT certification for Kundalini Yoga from the Ithaca Yoga Farm in New York.

Carol Steinmetz

Anusara Style Yoga

Welcome back, Carol! After about ten years on the road, Carol is home again. She teaches a thoughtful class with roots in Anusara Yoga and detailed attention to alignment, coming around the room to help your body find itself.

Ali Wassick

RYT 500+

Ali teaches many yoga classes a week in various styles and for various groups: Gentle Yoga, Chair Yoga, Yoga for the Disabled and Vinyasa Flow. We adore her.

Charles Waters

Gentle Yoga

Charles came to yoga as a young man in the mid 1970s. He completed teacher training at Kripalu Center for Yoga and Health, Ashtanga teacher training with David Swenson, and trained in Iyengar Yoga with Julianna Centner. “As I have gotten older I have appreciated the many benefits of Gentle Yoga.” He also teaches economics and math at MCLA.

Suzy Pence, RYT

Evening Flow with Sound Healing

Originally from New Hampshire, Suzy teaches yoga alongside her work as a veterinarian, and recently returned from advanced teacher training in India.

Peter Rizzo

Guest master teacher · Brattleboro

Owner and originator of Bhava Yoga in Brattleboro, Peter comes to The Yoga Place for his Saturday Old School Vinyasa class, by donation. “Peter leads one of the most challenging and tender hearted yoga classes I have ever taken.” (Ellen)
