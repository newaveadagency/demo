Good to know

Questions, answered.

Anything else? Call the store at (919) 615-1571.

What are your hours?

Monday to Friday 8:00 am to 11:00 pm, last wash at 9:45 pm. Saturday and Sunday 7:00 am to 10:00 pm, last wash at 9:15 pm. The store may close any time after the last wash.

Can I pay with a card?

Yes. The machines accept credit and debit cards, and we take Visa, MasterCard, Discover and American Express.

Do you do wash, dry & fold?

Yes. We specialize in residential and commercial wash, dry & fold, and offer a corporate discount. Call for details.

Can you pick up my laundry?

Yes. We offer pickup or drop-off at your home or office, for laundry and dry cleaning.

Can I wash comforters and rugs?

Yes. Bring bedspreads, comforters, rugs and tablecloths and use our biggest washers (up to 60 lb), or let us wash them for you.

What is the Wash Rewards Club?

Members receive our current specials. Ask the attendant how to join.
