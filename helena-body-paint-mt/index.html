Questions

Good to know.

Anything else? Call us at 406-442-8170.

Will you work with my insurance company?

Yes. Helena Body & Paint will assist by working directly with your insurance company on all claims, so you are not left handling the paperwork alone.

Is the repair covered by a warranty?

Yes. We issue a written lifetime warranty on parts and labor for as long as you own the vehicle.

Do you charge for a damage report?

No. We provide a free, computerized damage report. Call 406-442-8170 or stop by 829 N Warren Street to get started.

Which vehicles do you repair?

All makes and models. From dings and scratches to major collision damage, and we can match any auto body paint.

Can you pick up and deliver my vehicle?

Pick-up and delivery are part of our services, along with secured storage. Call us to arrange it.

Where are you located?

We are centrally located at 829 N Warren Street in Helena, across from the old Shodair Hospital. Look for the stone arch with our name above the open bay.
