01

Seasonal service

Get your mower ready for next year. Any repairs needed are an additional charge.

  • Cleaning deck
  • Sharpening and balancing blades
  • Check deck for level
  • Inspecting belts and pulleys
  • Oil and filter change
  • Replace spark plugs
  • Inspect and/or change air filter
  • Test battery and charging system
  • Grease all fittings
  • Inflate tires to proper pressure
02

Scheduled full service

The full service your manufacturer recommends, to extend the life of your equipment. We inspect your mower to determine what service interval it’s at — it may be at an interval that needs more than the yearly seasonal service.

This service is priced on parts and labor, not at a set seasonal service price.

03

Major repairs

Our certified mechanics have years of experience and access to manufacturers’ specifications and service bulletins the average mechanic doesn’t have. We diagnose and repair most major brands of lawn and garden equipment. All repairs are based on time and material.

$25diagnostic fee · all equipment except riding mowers
$100diagnostic fee · riding mowers

Diagnostic fees are waived if repairs are made. A $25.00 deposit is required at drop-off on all equipment except riding mowers; it’s applied to your balance once repairs are completed. If you decide not to have the repairs, the deposit covers the diagnostic fee. The fee covers disassembly, inspection, diagnosis and the parts-and-labor estimate — not reassembly or repairs.

04

New equipment setup

Free setup for equipment purchased from us. We’ll set up other equipment at our standard labor rate.

We sell new equipment from Encore, Husqvarna, Maruyama and RedMax — stop by to see what’s on the floor.
