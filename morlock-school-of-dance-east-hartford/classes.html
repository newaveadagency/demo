Fees & specials

Clear, simple tuition

Tuition is averaged over the ten months from mid-September to mid-June, so your monthly bill stays the same whether a month has two weeks of classes or five.

4th class freeBuy three 1-hour classes, get the 4th one free (per family). The lowest-priced class comes off first; competition classes are not counted.
$20Registration fee per student. Non-refundable, due before classes begin.
$50Recital fee per family, charged in January and due before June. Costumes are billed separately.
$25Deposit per costume, due November 1st. Costumes paid in full by the end of January.

Pay by cash, check or credit card, online through your parent account, between classes, or in the payment box in the office. Checks payable to Morlock School of Dance. Please call or email for current class prices.

Dress code

Dressed for class

A neat, tidy appearance sets an attitude of attentiveness and respect for students, teachers and the art of dance, and lets teachers see the body outline to correct posture and alignment.

For every class

  • Long hair securely tied up, off the face and neck.
  • No loose or dangling jewelry, and no gum.
  • No loose-fitting pants, blue jeans or t-shirts.
  • Arrive early enough to change if you don’t come in dance clothes.
  • No socks on the gymnastics mats.
  • Students who don’t follow the dress code may be asked to sit out.

Tap and Tutu

Girls: any color leotard and tights, pink leather ballet shoes, black tap shoes; skirts and legwarmers allowed. Boys: solid T-shirt, black dance pants or sweatpants, black leather ballet and tap shoes.

Hip Hop/Flip Flop, Pre-Gym & Acro

Leotard, unitard or tights; booty shorts or leggings OK. Boys: solid T-shirt and black dance pants or sweatpants. No shoes needed.

Ballet (Intro to Level 4 & Teen)

Any color leotard and tights, pink leather ballet shoes. Skirts for Intro, Level 1 and 2 only. No bare legs; no ponytails for Levels 3 and 4. Boys: black tights or dance pants, black or white T-shirt, black ballet slippers.

Hip hop

T-shirt, fitted top or sports bra with shorts, leggings, jazz pants or sweatpants. Clean sneakers. No jeans!

Jazz & modern

Leotard, fitted top or sports bra with shorts, leggings or jazz pants, and leather pirouettes.

Tap

Black tap shoes: Mary Jane style for Intro and Level 1, laced from Level 2 up.

Competition company

Ready for more stage?

Interested in competition? Come and talk to Miss Christine and Miss Elizabeth. Practice for company members begins in August.

Requirements

  • At least one year of study at Morlock.
  • One ballet class plus the subject you compete in, each studied for a year.
  • Full commitment from parents and students; weekends can be busy.
  • Dances, placement, music and costumes are at the discretion of Miss Christine and Miss Elizabeth.

Junior, Teen & Senior Company

$200 for one group dance, $100 for each additional group dance. Or pay monthly: $20 a month for one dance, $30 for two, $40 for three.

Includes choreography and the weekly class all year round. Competition entry fees, costumes and makeup are separate.

Studio policies

The fine print, made easy

Tuition & payments

Your account is charged monthly and payment is due at the beginning of each month. Tuition not paid by the 30th of the month gets a $15 late fee; after two unpaid months, students may be asked not to return to class until the account is paid up.

A returned check carries a $30 fee plus repayment of the check. After more than two bad checks, payment by check is no longer accepted.

Recital & costumes

The recital is held in mid-June. Tell us before we order costumes in November if you won’t take part. The $50 per-family recital fee is charged in January and must be paid before June. Costume deposits ($25 each) are due November 1st; costumes not paid in full by the end of January get a $15 late charge per month. Costumes generally range between $50 and $65.

The Parents’ Recital Week meeting in April is mandatory, so you have all the information you need.

Joining late or leaving

Joining after costumes are ordered? Your dancer can still perform if the costume can arrive in time; it must be paid in full, plus any shipping or rush fees.

If your child stops dancing, please give us a courtesy call. There are no refunds for leaving halfway through a month.

Missed & make-up classes

Please call and leave a message if your child will miss class. Missed classes must be paid for whether or not they are made up (no credits). Make up a class by joining a class of similar level on another day; occasionally a short private make-up (15–20 minutes) can be scheduled.

Weather & cancellations

Cancellations are posted on the website. Without internet access, check the studio voicemail at 860-568-0160. Cancelled classes can be made up on a different day; no credits are given.

Studio etiquette

Arrive at least 5 minutes early, ready to dance or with time to change. Please miss as few classes as possible. No parents in the dance rooms, and please don’t wait in the front entrance or office. Help keep the studio clean and pick up your children promptly after class.

Picture day

Picture day is at the end of the dance year, close to the recital. Every student can take part in the free group photo displayed in the studio.
