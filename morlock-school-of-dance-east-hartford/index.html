A little bit of history

A legacy handed down, step by step

Morlock School of Dance, formerly known as the Mary Morlock School of Performing Arts, has been in business in East Hartford for 70 years.

Mary Morlock, a well-respected and renowned teacher, dedicated her life to teaching children the art of dance. She trained Christine Sessa and Elizabeth Sanderson as dancers and as teachers, and after 60 years of teaching she handed the studio down to them so they could continue its legacy.

On stage

Recital in June, competition all year

Every dance year ends with a recital in mid-June. Dancers who want more can audition for the competition company.

The recital

Held at the end of the dance year in mid-June. A Parents’ Recital Week meeting in April covers everything you need to know, and picture day comes close to the recital, with a free group photo displayed in the studio.

The competition company

Open to dancers who have studied at least one year at Morlock and take one ballet class alongside their competition style. Junior, Teen and Senior companies; practice starts in August. Requirements & pricing

Parents ask

Good to know

When does the dance year run?

From mid-September to mid-June. Tuition is averaged over those ten months, so your monthly payment is the same whether a month has two weeks of classes or five.

Is there a registration fee?

Yes, $20 per student. It is non-refundable and due before classes begin.

How does the recital work?

The recital is in mid-June. There is a $50 recital fee per family and a separate costume bill. The costume deposit is $25 per costume, due November 1st, and costumes must be paid in full by the end of January.

Not taking part? Just let us know before we order costumes in November.

What if my child misses a class?

Please call and leave a message. Missed classes are still charged, but you can make one up by joining a class of a similar level on a different day.

What should my dancer wear?

Dance clothes that let the teacher see the body outline, hair tied up off the face and neck, no dangling jewelry and no jeans. Each class has its own shoes. See the full dress code.

How will I know if class is cancelled for weather?

Cancellations are posted on the website. Without internet access? A message is left on the studio voicemail at 860-568-0160.

How can I pay?

Cash, check or credit card, or online through your parent account. You can also pay between classes or use the payment box in the office.
