The Drellishak story

From a corner station to a Rocky River institution

1945

Frank Sr. goes into business for himself

Frank Drellishak Sr. was a mechanic for Shell in Elyria. After an accident cost him two fingers on his right hand, and another the sight in one eye, he decided to go into the service station business himself. In 1945 he bought his first shop next to the Rustic Restaurant on Center Ridge Road.

1950s

Tractors, rototillers and produce trucks

Back when Rocky River was dotted with greenhouses and farms, the station serviced more than cars: tractors, rototillers and the big open-air trucks that hauled fruit and vegetables to market. Over the years the shop worked on vehicles for familiar local names like Hoag, Pease, Christensen and Horton.

1988

Eugene moves into the old General Tire

Frank’s son Eugene Drellishak bought the old General Tire building, which became our eight-bay shop at 21512 Center Ridge Road. Another Drellishak brother opened a shop at W. 227th and Lorain in Fairview Park.

2020

75 years, honored by the State of Ohio

On our seventy-fifth anniversary the Ohio House of Representatives of the 133rd General Assembly congratulated the shop and saluted it as one of Ohio’s finest businesses.

Today

Still family owned

Drellishak’s is still a family-owned business, run by the third and fourth generation. Stop by and see why this Rocky River auto institution still prides itself on outstanding automotive service.
