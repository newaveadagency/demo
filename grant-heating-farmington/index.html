About us

A trade learned from Dad.

Chris Grant grew up watching his father work in the heating and air conditioning industry, and naturally followed in his footsteps. Since 2009 Grant Heating and Air has brought quality HVAC products, exceptional customer service and a level of craftsmanship rarely seen in the industry to homes and businesses across the south metro.

With high-end Lennox equipment, custom fabricated ductwork and precise attention to detail, we remedy or prevent many of the airflow challenges homes and businesses face – whether that means designing a new construction system or modifying and replacing the one you have.

Caring for our community

Feel the Love.

For the past three years we've given back to our Farmington community through the Lennox Feel the Love program. Each year we provide a complete Lennox HVAC system – new furnace, AC and thermostat – at no cost to a local family nominated by their community.

From teachers and coaches to local police officers, our recipients dedicate their time to serving others and making Farmington a better place. It's a privilege to give back to those who give so much.

FAQ

Good to know.

Don't see your question? Call 651-226-0515 – if you need something not listed, reach out and we'll try to help.

Do you offer free estimates?
Yes. We offer free consultations and estimates for equipment replacement, remodels and new construction at your convenience. After the appointment, you'll receive a detailed estimate for your review.
Do you offer emergency service?
24-hour emergency service is available for our Preventive Maintenance customers. Regular office hours are Monday to Friday, 8:00 am to 5:00 pm.
What brands do you install?
We install high-end Lennox heating and cooling equipment, and trust brands like Aprilaire and Honeywell Home for indoor air quality and controls.
Can you help me get a utility rebate?
Yes. We take care of everything to make sure you get the full rebate available from your utility provider – such as Xcel Energy, CenterPoint Energy, Dakota Electric or Minnesota Energy Resources.
Do you work on new construction and remodels?
Yes – new construction, remodels and basement finishes, including system design and custom fabricated ductwork.
Where are you located?
Grant Heating and Air is based out of Farmington, MN and has served the south metro since 2009.
