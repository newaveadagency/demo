Preventive maintenance

Warm on the coldest Minnesota nights.

Like an annual physical, preventive maintenance addresses minor issues before they become major problems. It extends the life of your equipment, helps you avoid costly emergency repairs – and Preventive Maintenance customers get access to 24-hour emergency service.

Spring visit

  • Check air conditioner / heat pump operation
  • Check refrigerant head and suction pressures
  • Inspect evaporator and condenser coils
  • Inspect all electrical controls and connections
  • Inspect and clean out drain lines
  • Check furnace blower operation
  • Change filters

Fall visit

  • Check furnace / heat pump operation
  • Check safety controls
  • Inspect and clean burners
  • Inspect and clean flame sensor
  • Inspect heat exchanger
  • Inspect and clean blower and blower motor
  • Inspect and clean out drain lines
  • Change filters
