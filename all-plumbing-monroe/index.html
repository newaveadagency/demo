Our story

A Monroe family since 1989.

All Plumbing, Inc. is a family owned and operated local business, founded by Gene and Sherry Martin and operated today by Jason Martin.

It was started with the motto Prompt. Expert. Service. – and those three words are still the core of the company. Our people are background checked, drug tested and professionally trained, and we take special pride in the craftsmen we train and employ.

Customers have called us “The Cleanest Plumbers in Town.” We always strive to leave the work area cleaner than we found it.

FAQ

Good questions.

Anything else? Call (318) 322-6970.

Do you offer emergency service?

Yes. Our office is open Monday to Friday, 7:30 am to 4:45 pm, and 24-hour emergency service is available after hours. Call (318) 322-6970.

How do you price a job?

We charge by the job, not by the hour, using a fair, standardized pricing manual. We review all options and pricing with you before we start, so there are no surprises.

Are you licensed and insured?

Yes. All Plumbing, Inc. is a licensed contractor (License #LMP3498), obtains all municipal and state permits as required, and is insured for workers' compensation and liability.

Who comes to my house?

Our technicians are drug tested, background checked and receive ongoing training. They wear shoe covers, cover the floor where they work and clean up when they're done.

How can I pay?

Cash, check, credit card or debit card. Financing is available with approved credit, and members of our Plumbing Savings Club can pay by bank draft (ACH).

Do you do new construction?

No – we focus on repair service and replacing equipment that breaks down, so a repair specialist is available when you have a problem.
