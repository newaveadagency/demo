02

Drain cleaning

Slow sinks and backed-up tubs mean something is building up in your pipes. Our plumbers pinpoint the root cause of the clog and present a customized solution.

Warning signs

Water backing up in or outside your home, sudden frequent leaks, slow tubs or faucets, odors from drains, peeling paint or staining near plumbing.

Skip the chemicals

Liquid drain cleaners can eat away at pipes, can crack a porcelain toilet and aren't a guaranteed fix. A professional clears the obstruction for good.

Floor drains

Utility-room and commercial floor drains collect debris. We inspect the pipe, find the cause and clear it.

Sewer lines

Sewer and drain stoppages, sewer cleaning, sewer camera inspections and water jetting.

Backflow prevention

A backflow preventer keeps dirty water from flowing back into your clean supply. Many municipalities in Ouachita Parish require it, and many local water authorities require annual backflow testing by a licensed contractor.

Install, test, repair

Backflow preventer installation, testing and repair for homes and businesses.

High-risk fixtures

Sprinkler systems, boilers, pools and hot tubs, dishwashers and washing machines, outdoor taps, toilets, water filters and softeners.

Commercial

Drink machines, janitorial sinks and catering equipment also carry backflow risk. We inspect the typical culprits and tell you what's needed for compliance.

Book backflow testing

Water treatment & testing

Hard water and strange tastes are hard to live with. Water testing identifies issues with taste, odor and hardness – then we fit the right treatment.

Water testing

Especially important on a well, in older homes with original plumbing, or if you notice discolored fixtures or odd smells and tastes.

Water softeners

Ion-exchange softeners pull excess minerals and some iron from hard water.

Whole-house filters

Pro-Systems whole-house filters are self-backwashing, remove chlorine, chloramine and THMs, and are warrantied for 10 years.

Purification

Under-sink and reverse osmosis systems – and maintenance or repair for units you already have.
