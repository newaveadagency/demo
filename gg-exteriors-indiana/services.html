Metal roofing

Shingle or metal?
We do both.

We install and repair both shingle and metal roofs, including standing seam metal like the charcoal roof pictured here.

Good to know: metal roofs can show something roofers call “oil canning”, a wavy or distorted look at certain times of the day. It can occur with any metal used in construction and is completely normal, and there are steps that help avoid it. Ask us about them when we plan your roof.

Inspections

A good roof is
the foundation
of a good home.

Many homeowners neglect their roofs, but like everything in your home it needs maintenance. Get it inspected at least once a year, and after every severe weather event.

An aging roof without proper flashing can let water in, and without proper insulation your energy bills can be sky high. Have one of our guys come out and take a look.
