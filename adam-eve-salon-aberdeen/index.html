Our story

A family salon, since 1970

The business started as a wig salon in the '70s, when wigs were the craze. More than 50 years later, it is still in the same family, and we still keep wigs in stock.

1970

Alecia (Lisa) Bean opens Adam & Eve

It began as a wig salon, and that history is why we are still a wig center today.

1981

Lori joins her mother

Lori Burgard had been watching and learning since the age of 10. She later took over ownership from her mother.

Growing

Twice the salon

Lori doubled the size of the salon: 10 stylist stations, a massage therapist, a pedicure room and a facial and waxing room.

2021

An instructor's license

In January 2021 Lori earned her South Dakota cosmetology instructor's license.

Today

The third generation

Lori's daughter Stacey Maldonado now cuts hair at Adam & Eve, the salon her grandmother opened.

Good to know

Questions, answered

Do you take walk-ins?
Yes, we welcome walk-ins. If you want a particular stylist, call 605-229-1700 first to check they're free.
Do you offer consultations?
Yes, consultations are complimentary. We'll look at your hair, talk through the hair you want, and give you the most accurate price quote and suggestion.
Are the listed prices final?
They are base prices. They may vary by stylist, color technique and hair density, and tax is not included. Massage prices already include tax.
Are wig fittings private?
Yes. We provide private wig and hair-system fittings for men and women, and keep wigs in stock.
What forms of payment do you accept?
American Express, cash, check, debit, Discover, Mastercard and Visa.
Are you hiring?
Adam & Eve is hiring licensed cosmetologists. If you're interested in our apprenticeship program, guest speaking or modeling, give us a call.
