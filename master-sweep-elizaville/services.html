Inspection levels

Level 1 or Level 2?

NFPA 211 is the national fire code for chimneys, fireplaces, vents and solid-fuel appliances. It describes inspection levels by how deep the look needs to go.

Level 1

The yearly look

For a system that hasn’t changed and that you plan to keep using as it is.

  • Readily accessible parts of the chimney, inside and out
  • Accessible parts of the appliance and its connection
  • Checks for obstructions and combustible deposits

Level 2

When something changes

Called for when the system changes, when a home is sold, or after a chimney fire or other event that could cause damage.

  • Everything in a Level 1
  • Accessible attics, crawl spaces and basements
  • A look at the internal surfaces of the flue

A general summary of NFPA 211. Your written report states which level was performed and what was found.

How often should my chimney be inspected?
NFPA 211 calls for chimneys, fireplaces and vents to be inspected at least once a year. The inspection shows whether a sweep or any repair is needed.
What is the difference between a Level 1 and a Level 2 inspection?
A Level 1 covers the readily accessible parts of the chimney and appliance when nothing about the system has changed. A Level 2 goes further and is called for when the system changes, when a property is sold, or after a chimney fire or other damaging event. We provide both as detailed written inspections.
