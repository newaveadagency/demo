What we do

Four trades, one sweep.

Every service starts with a careful look and ends with a clean hearth. If something needs work, you get it in writing, with pictures.

  • i.

    Chimney inspections

    Chimneys can suffer from a variety of issues after years of use. Our inspections find out exactly what the problem is, so you can make an informed decision on how to proceed.

  • ii.

    Chimney sweep

    Over time, even carefully crafted chimneys get clogged. We clear the flue without leaving a mess anywhere else on your property.

  • iii.

    Masonry restoration

    Handcrafted stone fireplaces and chimneys deserve to look beautiful. Don’t let them fall apart with age.

  • iv.

    Stove maintenance

    If your wood-burning stove is acting up, a careful stove inspection finds the problem, and regular maintenance helps prevent trouble later.

We are also an authorized stove dealer, so the same people who sweep your chimney can help you choose and install what burns in front of it.
