i. Carpet

Wall-to-wall carpet, in and out.

Bedrooms, family rooms, hallways and stairs. The old carpet and pad come out, the new carpet goes in, and the furniture goes back where it was.

  • Carpet and pad removal

ii. Stairs

Runners, treads and spirals.

Full stair carpet, runners bound to wood treads, curved and spiral staircases, and plank flooring carried from the landing down every step.

iii. Hard floors

Laminate, vinyl plank and hardwood.

Plank floors for hallways, kitchens, living rooms and whole open floors, with matching stair treads and step-downs.

  • Laminate flooring
  • Vinyl plank flooring
  • Hardwood installation
  • Tile and old flooring removal

What does In & Out Flooring install?

Carpet, stair carpet and runners, laminate, vinyl plank and hardwood flooring, plus baseboards and molding.

Do you take out the old flooring?

Yes. Old carpet, pad and flooring can be removed before the new floor goes in.

Can I get an estimate online?

Yes. Online estimates are offered; you can also call 775-232-6847 and arrange for someone to come out, measure and quote.

Do you move furniture?

Furniture can be moved out of the way for the install and put back afterwards. Mention large or heavy pieces when you call.

What are your hours?

Monday to Friday 8 AM to 5 PM and Saturday 9 AM to 5 PM. Closed on Sunday.

Where do you work?

In & Out Flooring works in Reno, Nevada and the surrounding area. Call to check your address.
