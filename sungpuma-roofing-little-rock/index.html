FAQ

Good questions.

Something else? Call (501) 232-7485.

Which Arkansas communities do you serve?

Homeowners anywhere in Arkansas, including Hot Springs, Benton, Bryant, Little Rock, North Little Rock, Conway, Cabot, Searcy, Jonesboro, West Memphis, Fort Smith, Fayetteville and Pine Bluff.

Are the owners involved in the jobs?

Yes. The owners are directly involved in nearly every job. In nine out of ten jobs the owner personally visits the site to oversee the work and keep quality under control.

Can you help with my insurance claim?

Yes. We document every impacted area with photos and written reports, help file the claim, meet adjusters on site when necessary and then complete the work according to the approved scope.

What warranty do you give?

All workmanship and materials come with a one-year warranty.

How do I know if my roof needs repair or replacement?

Common signs are missing shingles, leaks, sagging areas or visible storm damage. We inspect the roof and recommend the most cost-effective solution for its condition.

Do you work on commercial and flat roofs?

Yes: commercial roofing, flat roof services and modified bitumen roofing for flat or low-slope roofs.
