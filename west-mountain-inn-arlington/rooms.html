Before you book

Inn policies

  • Deposit: one night per room at the time of booking, refunded less a $20 fee if cancelled or shortened at least 14 days before arrival.
  • Minimum stay: 2 nights for most Saturday night stays.
  • Taxes: 9% Vermont State Tax on all bills; 18% service charge on dining and bar charges.
  • No pets, no smoking in the inn. Service animals are welcome in all areas.
  • Accessibility: one fully wheelchair-accessible first-floor room with roll-in shower; accessible public areas on the first floor.
  • Room requests: we do our best to honor them; final room assignments are at the inn's discretion.
