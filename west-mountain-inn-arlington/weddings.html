Weddings & events

Your wedding,your way.

Not just a wedding day: a whole weekend on 150 private acres, from the rehearsal dinner to the farewell brunch.

A weekend together

The whole property is yours

When you hold your celebration at the inn you have exclusive use of the property. We never book multiple events on one day, so you have our full attention and focus.

Plan a canoe trip down the Battenkill, a group hike on our trails, a yoga class in the garden, pre-wedding massage sessions, a BBQ and bonfire. We listen and support you in making your celebration uniquely your own.

Start planning
The Barn

Ceremony sites

The side yard overlooking our labyrinth garden and West Mountain, the open field with views of the Green Mountains, even the woodland meadows along our trails. Prefer a little white chapel? We can connect you with local churches and synagogues.

The historic barn

Wide-plank oak floors, aged-cedar siding, windows on all sides and a 2½-story open interior, with a few modern amenities added. A romantic, rustic space for receptions of up to 300 guests.

Tented weddings

Dreaming of a classic white tent? We coordinate tent rental, set-up and decor, with several garden and meadow settings depending on your party size.

Farm-to-table catering

Our chefs and event coordinator design a menu for your weekend with local, organic ingredients: wood-fired pizza and BBQ rehearsal dinners, 4-course or family-style receptions, abundant farewell brunches.

Rooms for your guests

14 guest rooms in the Main Inn and 3 Millhouse townhouse apartments keep your closest people on the property all weekend.

Off-season & small

Afternoon weddings, winter weddings, off-season weddings, even elopements. We're happy to fit your celebration to your dreams and your budget.

Intimate celebrations

Small weddings & elopements

Sometimes it's a relief to plan a small wedding with your closest family and friends. We'll help you along the way.

Small Wedding Package

$105per person, plus tax & service

Mid-week (Monday – Thursday) May – October, and some weekends November – April.

  • Ceremony in the antique barn or on the lawn in front of the labyrinth garden
  • Farm-to-table dinner in the dining room: 4 hors d'oeuvres (or an appetizer), salad and a choice of 3 entrées
  • Prosecco toast
  • Tables, chairs, place settings, glassware, ceremony set-up, staffing and coordination

Music, photography, flowers, cake and officiant are not included, but we're happy to refer you to excellent local vendors. Barn for dining and/or dancing: add a $3,500 facility fee. Dancing in the inn's living room: your guests take all 14 Main Inn rooms, plus a $500 set-up fee.
