Our mission

We showcase tap as music – one of the only dance forms that can be heard.

The CTC gives the community a better understanding of tap dance through performances of historical material, improvisation, new choreography and informative talks.

With a strong emphasis on musicality and exploration, our members create their own unique rhythmic expressions and share them with the world.

Questions

Good to know

I’ve never tapped before. Can I join a class?

Yes. The Musical Theater Tap class is for all levels – some previous dance experience is preferred, but not necessary. Tap dancing is something anyone can do.

Do I need tap shoes?

Not for the “little to no experience” level: all ages and levels are welcome there and no tap shoes are required.

Who can join the company?

The CTC is a professional-level company, but dancers of all ages and levels are welcome (16+ unless approved by the Directors). Fill out the audition form or come to an open rehearsal on the 2nd Saturday of the month, 11 am–1 pm in Hartford ($25 drop-in).

What is expected of company dancers?

All dancers are expected to improvise for a full chorus (32 bars) and adapt quickly to changes in choreography and structure. The company always creates a road map and reference videos and holds extra rehearsals when needed.

Do you offer private lessons?

Yes, private lessons are available. Book through the Hartford Dance Collective or email tapdance.ctc@gmail.com.
