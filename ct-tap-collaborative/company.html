The Company

Roots in history.Made in the moment.

An improvisation-based tap dance company. By creating new works from a rich vocabulary of historic dances, we keep the traditions – and in-the-moment creative expression – alive through jams, improvography and discussions of the many styles tap represents.

“Chameleon”, Kingswood Oxford Choreographer’s Showcase 2025 – photo Tony Spinelli. Hartford Dance Festival 2025 photos – Cedric Pilard.

How we work

Canons, counters & polyrhythms

Our founders and members uphold the traditions by performing and teaching historic repertoire, taking classes at every level, and exploring the creative process through improvisation and trading.

Together we create the basic structure of a piece and fill it in with canons, counter and polyrhythms. Every dancer improvises a full chorus (32 bars) and adapts quickly to changes in choreography. We always make a road map and reference videos, and hold extra rehearsals when needed.

We pick up performances as they become available – and whoever is available is welcome to perform.

Meet the company

Without our amazing team none of this would be happening – and we are grateful for all of our current, past and future members.

Founder · Artistic Director · Choreographer

Katie Cavenagh

Katie has over 40 years of dance experience with a specialized focus on tap, and has been teaching for 30 years. She trained primarily at the American Tap Dance Foundation in NYC; her mentors include Barbara Duffy, Brenda Bufalino and the legacy of the Masters.

She has performed at Tap City and The Big Apple Tap Festival in NYC, with #Taplife and The Boston Tap Company, and coached the UConn Tap Team for 7 seasons. Katie teaches at The CT Dance Academy and The Hartford Dance Collective, and introduced tap to the team sports program at Kingswood Oxford School in West Hartford.
