Levels

Start anywhere. Grow from there.

BEGINNER · LITTLE TO NO EXPERIENCE

Learn the basics

Foundations of rhythmic understanding and percussive body movement, with an emphasis on fun, repetition and moving our bodies. All ages and levels welcome.

No tap shoes required
INT / ADV · PREVIOUS EXPERIENCE

Clarity & expression

Fast paced, focusing on clarity, complex rhythmic phrases, musicality and personal expression. Choreography and improvisation, with variations for your level – or a challenge.

Experience required
COMPANY · PRO & PRE-PROFESSIONAL

Next level

Learn the repertoire, be part of new works and performances, and push yourself. Choreography is fast paced and improvisation is a must.

Experience required

Workshop

Discover your instrument

Hit it like a musician. A two-hour workshop on rhythm, phrasing and musicality – and why looking at tap from a percussionist’s viewpoint makes you a stronger, more creative performer.

  • Basic music theory, accents and dynamics
  • Picking up choreography and how to improvise like a pro
  • How the floor, your shoes and your body make a melodic rhythmic phrase
  • Drills to practise at home, some history you might not have learned in tap class, and resources
  • “Lecture and learning” first, then improvography and Q&A

For dancers, teachers and performers of all ages and levels – and for anyone who has never danced and wants to dip their toes in the water.

Last held October 24, 2026, 11 am–1 pm at Cheshire Performing Arts Academy, Cheshire (early bird $35; $50 with notes and videos). Ask us about the next date or book it for your studio.
