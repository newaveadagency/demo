Locks & lock openers

02

High-security locks

High-security locks provide increased protection against the most common methods of intrusion, including picking, drilling and bumping. We offer Medeco, Mul-T-Lock Interactive+, Mul-T-Lock Junior and Keymark.

Security starts with key control. When you purchase a high-security lock from us, we guarantee we will not make keys for any individuals who have not been authorized. Contact us and we will happily provide a quote for upgrading your locks.

03

Safes & safe movers

The size and style of a safe affect both how secure it is and how much it costs. We help you find a safe that is as strong as you need and within your price range, and whether a wall, floor or standalone safe works best, whether you plan to store guns, jewelry or important documents.

Moving a safe safely takes the right equipment and procedures. We deliver your new safe or relocate your current one to a different room, without harming your furnishings. We guarantee the effectiveness of each of our safes and only sell safes from reputable manufacturers.

  • Safes installed & moved
  • Safes opened & repaired
  • Safe & lock combinations recovered and changed
  • Hayman, Major, Browning, AMSEC, Gardall

Access control

Many businesses have areas that are closed to the public and to unauthorized employees. Our access control systems add an extra layer of protection by limiting who can enter sensitive areas, for both residential and commercial buildings throughout Columbia, SC.

They help promote a safer workplace and home by only letting authorized personnel or trusted people in, and simply seeing that a property has a security system is often enough to deter crime.

  • Electric strikes & electric locks
