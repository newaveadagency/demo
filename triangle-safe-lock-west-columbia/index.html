Our story

From a key & saw shop to three generations

1964

The “Key and Saw Shop”

Homer “Jack” Bachman and his wife Milbrum open on Meeting Street in West Columbia. With their son Larry they sharpen saw blades and offer basic locksmithing and carpentry. Later they move to the corner of Augusta Road and 12th Street and expand their lock and security line.

1986

Triangle Safe & Lock, Inc.

Larry Bachman takes over and incorporates the business. He serves as President of both the North Carolina and South Carolina Locksmith Associations and becomes a Certified Master Locksmith with the Associated Locksmiths of America (ALOA).

2006

Sunset Boulevard

Having outgrown 12th Street, the shop moves in June 2006 to 1400 Sunset Blvd.

Today

The next generation

Larry’s children, Kelsey and Lance, run the shop, carrying on the tradition their grandfather started.

FAQ

Good to know

What are your business hours?

Monday to Friday, 8:30 AM to 5:00 PM. The shop is closed on Saturday and Sunday.

Where is the shop?

1400 Sunset Blvd, West Columbia, SC 29169, between Lexington Medical Center and the Gervais Street Bridge (near 12th Street).

Can you make a spare car key or remote?

Yes. We sell and program automotive remotes, and cut and program transponder keys.

Can you deliver and move a safe?

Yes. We sell safes from Hayman, Major, Browning, AMSEC and Gardall, and we have the equipment to deliver and install a new safe or move your current one to a different room.

Will you copy a high-security key for anyone who asks?

No. When you buy a high-security lock from us, we only make keys for individuals you have authorized.

How can I pay?

Cash, check, or charge (Visa and Mastercard).
