FAQ

Good questions.

Something else on your mind? Call (217) 342-1099 – we would love to hear from you.

I’m a new client. How do I get started?

Call (217) 342-1099 for an appointment. To pull your information together ahead of time, use the basic tax organizer for new clients: fill it in on screen, print it, and fax or mail it to the office. Current clients can ask the office for a “proforma” organizer that already includes last year’s information and carryover data.

How do I send my documents securely?

Upload them through SafeSend or ShareFile. You can also bring them to the office at 2001 S. Banker in Effingham.

How can I pay?

All major credit cards, debit cards, e-checks, Apple Pay, Google Pay and AfterPay are accepted. Invoices can be paid online through CPACharge.

Are you open on Fridays?

Regular office hours are Monday to Thursday, 8:00 am to 4:30 pm. The office is closed on Fridays.

Can you represent me if the IRS or the State audits me?

Yes. IRS and State audit representation is one of the firm’s core services, alongside tax preparation and planning. See all services.

Can you help us with QuickBooks?

Yes. Goeckner Accounting is a Certified QuickBooks ProAdvisor® and helps with QuickBooks setup, training, support and reporting.
