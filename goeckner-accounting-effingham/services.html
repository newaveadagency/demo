Tax preparation

Significant investment in computerized tax preparation and research software lets us prepare returns accurately and efficiently for individuals, corporations, partnerships, trusts, estates and not-for-profit organizations.

Tax planning

Tax planning is an essential part of the tax preparation process. Built into your overall business strategy, our experience and access to the latest developments in tax law help minimize both current and future tax liabilities.

03

Estate & trust tax preparation

Effective estate and gift planning facilitates the orderly transfer of assets to your beneficiaries, provides security for a surviving spouse, and can reduce or eliminate tax on the transfer of your business and other assets.

04

Payroll services

As a business grows and hires, payroll administration grows with it. We help put the controls in place for a reliable, efficient payroll system and prepare all necessary payroll tax returns on time.

05

Sales tax services

Many clients collect and submit sales tax in several jurisdictions. We help compile the information and prepare sales tax returns efficiently and on time.

06

IRS representation

Years of experience with many taxing authorities mean you are properly represented before federal and state tax agencies – including IRS and State audits.

Business entity selection

S corporation, sole proprietor, limited liability company… the form of your business is a crucial decision with long-standing tax consequences. We weigh the pros and cons of each entity type with you.
