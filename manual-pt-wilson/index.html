Meet the founder

Dr. Kelly Senglar-Vitale, PT, DPT

DPTCOMTCert. MDTCMPCert. DNC-STEPFAAOMPT

Manual Physical Therapy, Inc. was built upon a dream that started many years ago in the mind of a young physical therapist. Dr. Kelly Senglar-Vitale earned her B.S. in Physical Therapy at Northeastern University (2000) and her Doctorate of Physical Therapy from A.T. Still University (2005), then completed a Residency and Fellowship in Orthopedic Manual Physical Therapy through the Ola Grimsby Institute.

She is a Fellow of the American Academy of Orthopaedic Manual Physical Therapists, a Certified McKenzie Therapist and Mulligan Practitioner, certified in dry needling, and has been adjunct faculty at Campbell University. She worked in the Rocky Mount area for over seven years before opening MPTI.

FAQ

Questions before your first visit

Will I see a physical therapist at every visit?
Yes. All patients are seen by a licensed physical therapist at all appointments and are treated one-on-one, so your care addresses your needs.
Which clinic should I call?
Whichever is closest to you: Wilson at (252) 243-6784, or Rocky Mount at (252) 937-6784. Dry needling is offered at both clinics.
Do you offer aquatic therapy?
Yes. Aquatic therapy is offered in our Endless Pool, which has two underwater treadmills and two endless lap resistance lanes for aquatic therapy, swimming and fitness.
Can I keep exercising with you after physical therapy?
Yes. The Wellness Program offers supervised and assisted workouts by trained professionals, access to physical therapy staff and our Endless Pool. Registration is $99 and membership is $85 per month. We accept Silver & Fit and SilverSneakers members. See the program.
Do you offer massage therapy?
Yes. All massages are performed by a North Carolina licensed Massage and Bodywork Therapist: 60 minutes $65, 90 minutes $90, 120 minutes $115. Gift certificates are available. Massage details.
