Manual physical therapy

Hands-on, evidence-based, one-on-one.

MPTI is a manual therapy based clinic with state-of-the-art equipment. Our clinician staff is made up of physical therapists who have completed their Doctoral degrees in Physical Therapy, and all have completed, or are working toward, advanced certifications.

We place a huge emphasis on continuing education and skill development, because it directly affects the care we can give every patient.

  • Seen by a licensed physical therapist at every appointment
  • Treated one-on-one, in a comfortable environment
  • "Pain in Pregnancy" and "Return to Sports" programs developed by Dr. Kelly Senglar-Vitale

Dry needling

Aquatic therapy

Our state-of-the-art Endless Pool.

Aquatic therapy is offered in our Endless Pool. The pool contains two underwater treadmills and two endless lap resistance lanes to meet your aquatic therapy, swimming and fitness needs.

  • Two underwater treadmills
  • Two endless lap resistance lanes
  • Also available to Wellness Program members
Wellness Program

Keep the progress you made in physical therapy.

Want to exercise in a comfortable environment unlike a gym, supervised and assisted by professionals to avoid injury? Training for a competition, a challenge, or just to be at your best? This program is for you.

  • Supervised and assisted workouts by trained professionals
  • Access to physical therapy staff, for more safety in your workouts
  • State-of-the-art equipment
  • Access to the Endless Pool with two underwater treadmills and double resistance swimming lanes
