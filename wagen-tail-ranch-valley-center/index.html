When can I drop off or pick up my dog?
During boarding office hours: Monday to Friday 8 am to 1 pm and 4 pm to 7 pm, Saturday 8 am to 1 pm only, Sunday 4 pm to 7 pm only.
Do you take senior dogs, puppies or dogs on medication?
Yes. We welcome all dog breeds and ages, and we specialize in young pups, senior kids, dogs with medication and health issues, and immobile dogs.
How is boarding priced?
Per kennel or suite, per calendar day. A Standard kennel starts at $42 for one dog; up to four dogs can share. Stays of 4+ days in the Cabins, Bungalows, Villas and Presidential Suite include a bath and brush. See all rates.
Can I watch my dog while I'm away?
Yes, in the Cabins, Bungalows, Villas and Presidential Suite. Download Easy Viewer Plus/Pro from the App Store or Google Play; our staff give you your username once your furr baby is checked in.
Can my dog play while boarding?
Yes. Half-day ($18) and full-day ($28) daycare while boarding is available for spayed/neutered dogs, plus the indoor water park ($18) and one-on-one playtime ($15).
How do I book?
New clients register online, existing clients sign in to the client portal. You can also call or email the boarding office.
