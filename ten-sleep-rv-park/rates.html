For travelers with horses

Horse Hotel.

Facilities

  • Outside pens, approximately 12 × 24
  • 50 × 90 barn
  • Certified hay for sale
  • Stock trailers available to borrow
  • Wheelbarrows and forks for cleaning stalls

Rates

$20a night per horse
$45a night, water, sewer & electric for LQ trailer / camper, 2 people

$5.00 per person per night for extra people. Deposits are non-refundable and non-transferable. Cancel 30 days in advance or full payment is due.

Rules & regulations

  • Load, unload and saddle only at the barn; tack may be stored in the barn
  • No horses on the grass or at the campsites
  • Dogs on a leash at all times; pet walk along the south fence
  • Clean pens early morning, evening and at departure ($50.00 charge if left unclean)
  • Empty wheelbarrows; clean borrowed stock trailers after use
  • Clean up after your horse in the barn and driveway; no rugs on the grass
  • No smoking in or near the barn or on the trails

Ten Sleep RV Park is listed in the Horse Motels International Overnight Stabling Directory.
