Welcome

Shade trees, full hook-ups and the Bighorns on the horizon.

Ten Sleep RV Park sits on Highway 16 in the west foothills of the Big Horn Mountains, in Ten Sleep, Wyoming. RVs, tents, horse trailers and travelers who would rather sleep in a cabin all have a place here.

  • 52 full-service pull-through sites with mature shade trees
  • 6 sleeping cabins, 2 modern cabins, 4 bunkhouses and a sheep wagon
  • A shaded, grassy tent area close to the shower house
  • 24-hour laundry, dump station, propane, free WiFi and pure artesian water

Good to know

Questions before you roll in

How do I make a reservation?

Reservations are by phone only. Call (307) 366-2250.

What does an RV site cost?

Full hook-up sites start at $45.00 for 30 amp and $60.00 for 50 amp, based on 2 people. Extra people 11 years and older are $5.00 per person. All rates

Do I need to bring bedding to the cabins?

In the sleeping cabins and most bunkhouses, guests bring their own bedding, or there is a one-time $10.00 per bed charge if the park provides it. Beds are made up in the modern cabins and Bunkhouse 2.

Are pets allowed?

Some cabins are pet friendly for $10 per day. Dogs must be kept on a leash at all times; please clean up after your dog. The pet walk runs along the south fence.

Can I bring my horses?

Yes. The Horse Hotel has outside pens and a 50 × 90 barn for $20.00 a night per horse, plus water, sewer and electric hook-ups for LQ trailers and campers. Horse Hotel details

Which payment methods do you accept?

Visa, MasterCard and Discover. A 4% credit card convenience charge applies.
