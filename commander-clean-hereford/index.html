Meet the detailer

Isaiah does it
right there.

Commander Clean is a self-sufficient mobile detailing service in Hereford. Isaiah, the name you'll see in almost every review, arrives at the appointment time, sets up on site and works through the whole vehicle: leather, trim, carpets, glass, vents, wheels and the engine.

Customers call it attention to detail. One found white dog hair on a black interior and watched him pull out every piece, even from the ceiling. Another got $600 over Kelley Blue Book on a trade-in after a full detail.

  • He inspects the vehicle first and gives you an estimate
  • Shows up prepared, with his own water supply
