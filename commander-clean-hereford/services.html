Paint, wheels, tires and trim washed and waxed in your driveway, including the bugs on the front end. Big trucks welcome, duallys included.

FAQ

Good to know.

Do you come to me?

Yes. Commander Clean is a mobile detailing service based in Hereford, AZ. Isaiah sets up at your location and brings his own water supply. Call to check that your area is covered.

Do I need an appointment?

Yes, appointments are required. Call (520) 699-5380 to book.

How much does a detail cost?

It depends on the vehicle and its condition. Isaiah looks the vehicle over and gives you an estimate before the work starts.

What vehicles do you detail?

Cars, trucks (including duallys), Jeeps and SUVs, motorcycles, motorhomes, fifth wheels and travel trailers.

Can you clean my engine bay?

Yes. Engine bay cleaning can be added to a full detail or booked on its own.

How can I pay?

Credit and debit cards are accepted.
