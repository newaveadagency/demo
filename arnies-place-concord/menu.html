02 / Food

Bar-B-Que & the grill.

Our BBQ meats are slow cooked and smoked up to 18 hours on site over hickory wood. Pork shoulder gets a dry spice rub made especially for us and 18 hours in the smoker before it's pulled. St. Louis style ribs get the same rub and 4½ hours of hickory smoke.

  • Smoked spareribs
  • Pulled pork
  • Spicy sausage
  • Smoked pulled chicken
  • Char-grilled burgers
  • Hot dogs
  • Char-grilled chicken sandwiches
  • Full kids menu

Gluten-free sandwich buns available. Barbecue is also sold in bulk. Call to order: (603) 545-2649.

04 / Catering

We'll bring Arnie's to you.

Our full menu can come to your home or event: BBQ, the grill menu or our ice cream. You can also order from the full BBQ menu and have it packaged for reheating at your event. Barbecue menus are available for any size gathering.

In summer our ice cream is on the road at celebrations, fairs, festivals, car shows and balloon rallies, and we're available for school fundraisers and corporate parties. Catering is available throughout the year.

Call (603) 545-2649

05 / Christmas trees

Trees & wreaths from Thanksgiving.

Since 1992 Arnie's has worked with the same tree farmers to offer Concord its largest display of fresh cut Balsam and Fraser trees, from 4 to 10 feet, plus tabletops, wreaths, sprigs and Christmas lights inside the store.

  • Open every day from Thanksgiving Day until the trees are gone
  • 10 am – 8 pm, with good parking lot lighting for evening shopping
  • We cut the trunk to fit your stand and help load your car
  • The 10-footers go quick. Come early.

Tree care tip: make a fresh, straight ½-inch cut before the tree goes in the stand, get it into water within 6 to 8 hours, keep it away from heat and check the water level every day.
