Doing it yourself?

DIY work is encouraged in the yard, with strict rules enforced. Dustless sanders with vacs are available for half- or full-day rental in the store.

Marina & storage

Slips, moorings & storage

An extremely well-protected anchorage half-way between Baltimore and Philadelphia, easy to reach from I-95.

  • 137 boat slipsCall the office for current prices.
  • 32 moorings for sailboatsSailboats up to 37 ft and 5 ft 6 in draft; one mooring takes a 38 ft sailboat. No power boats, steel sailboats or catamarans. Includes the dinghy dock and/or dinghy rack, first come, first served.
  • Winter storage – wet & dryIndoor and outdoor, for up to 300 boats.
  • Summer land storageWith ramp access, for trailered power boats 23 ft and under.
Moorings

To reserve a mooring, contact Sheila at 410-287-8113 ext. 113 or sheila@bayboatworks.com.
