FAQ

Good to know before you call

Do you handle plumbing emergencies?

Yes. Emergencies can happen any time – holidays, weekends or off hours – and our staff will be there as soon as possible. Emergency service is available for additional costs.

Which areas do you serve?

Residential and commercial customers in Kanawha and Putnam Counties, including St. Albans, Charleston, South Charleston, Winfield, Scott Depot and Hurricane, WV – and Huntington.

Are you licensed?

Yes. West Virginia contractor’s license WV025451. We are licensed, bonded and insured, and we provide all needed permits and inspections for remodeling projects.

What warranties do you offer?

A 90-day warranty on all repairs and a lifetime warranty on residential faucets and toilets. Ask about our 10-year warranty on water heaters and our maintenance/warranty programs.

Do you install tankless water heaters?

Yes. We are an authorized Rinnai Tankless dealer, and we also supply and install traditional electric and gas tank water heaters, including A.O. Smith and American.

How can I pay?

We accept Visa, MasterCard, American Express and Discover.
