Payments

  • Cash, check, debit cards, Visa, Mastercard, Discover and American Express.
  • Statements go out on the 1st; payments are due on the 10th.
  • September tuition is paid at the studio; online payments and Auto-Pay start in October through the Parent Portal.
  • Cash and checks can go in the locked box in the lobby; cards are taken at the desk during office hours.
  • $30 fee for returned checks or insufficient funds.

Costumes & refunds

  • Costume balances must be paid in full before a costume is ordered; costumes can be refunded before they're ordered.
  • Each dancer is measured; we usually add an inch to allow for growth. Any alterations are the family's responsibility.
  • Withdrawal must be submitted by a parent in writing or by email. No prorated refunds mid-month; months paid in advance are refunded at the studio.
  • Adult classes are billed monthly, with no registration or costume fees.

Dress code

What to wear to class

Dress code is strictly enforced and varies per class. Leotards, tights and shoes are available in our online store and at the studio for items in stock.

Combo classes, ages 3–4
  • Girls: pink leotard, pink tights, pink full-sole no-tie ballet shoes (no skirt, or attached skirts only); Capezio Jr Tyette or no-tie tap shoes.
  • Boys: white tee, black dance pants, black full-sole ballet shoes; Capezio Teletone tap shoes.
  • Hair secured back.
Combo classes, ages 5–6
  • Girls: lavender leotard, pink tights, pink full-sole no-tie ballet shoes (no ballet skirts or tutus); Capezio Jr Tyette, no-tie or buckle tap shoes.
  • Boys: white tee, black dance pants, black full-sole ballet shoes; Capezio Teletone tap shoes.
  • Tan slip-on jazz shoes (order in studio, or Bloch SO495 / Capezio EJ2).
  • Hair secured back.
Ballet I & Ballet II
  • Girls: black leotard, pink tights (no skirts or tutus); pink full-sole shoes in Ballet I, pink split-sole in Ballet II/III.
  • Boys: solid color tee, black dance pants, black ballet shoes.
  • Hair in a neat bun.
Jazz (all levels)
  • Leotard & shorts, or sports bra/dance top & shorts/leggings; boys tee or tank & shorts/pants (no jeans).
  • Combo, Jazz I & II: tan slip-on jazz shoe. Jazz III: black slip-on jazz shoe.
  • Hair secured back.
Lyrical / Contemporary (all levels)
  • Leotard & shorts, or sports bra/dance top & shorts/leggings; boys tee or tank & shorts/pants (no jeans).
  • Half-sole pirouettes matching skin tone.
  • Hair secured back.
Tap (all levels)
  • Comfortable dance attire; tees and tanks allowed (no jeans). Hair secured back.
  • Ages 3–4: black Capezio Jr Tyette or no-tie. Ages 5–6: black Mary Jane buckle tap shoes.
  • Tap I & II: black tie tap shoe. Tap III: black tie tap shoe (a better shoe from a local dance store recommended).
  • Advanced Tap: Bloch Respect or Jason Samuels recommended (Respects not recommended for high arches).
Hip Hop (all levels)
  • Comfortable dance attire; tees and tanks allowed (no jeans). Hair secured back.
  • Clean sneakers that are not worn outside.
Acro (all levels)
  • Leotard & shorts, biketard, or sports bra/dance top & shorts/leggings; boys tee or tank & shorts/pants (no jeans).
  • No shoes.

Studio policies

Good habits, happy classes

  • Arrive 10 minutes before class and pick up promptly after.
  • Let the teacher know by phone or email if your dancer can't attend.
  • No street shoes on the dance floor; no gum, candy or food in class.
  • Phones silenced and put away during class.
  • Please don't leave valuables unattended in the lobby.
  • Tell your teacher about any injury or limitation before class.
  • Prefer no photos of your dancer online? Email us and we'll note it on your account.
