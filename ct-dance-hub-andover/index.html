Meet the owner

Stephanie Meleounis

Stephanie grew up in Manchester, CT, started dancing at three and excelled most at tap. After years of teaching at local studios while working full-time at Travelers Insurance, she decided dance had to be her full-time job, and opened CT Dance Hub to share it with her community.

Her goal: a stronger dance community in Connecticut, with dance for anyone, at any age. She loves seeing students accomplish their goals, conquer their fears and become comfortable with who they are.

Good to know

Questions parents ask

How are dancers placed into a level?

Our teachers place each student by age, experience and skill level. At the end of the year the teachers decide whether a dancer stays in the same level or moves up. Moving up isn't automatic with age; we always keep the dancer's best interest in mind.

Can parents watch class?

Yes. There is an observation window in the lobby. Most days the curtains are shut, but on some days they're open, and parents are invited into the classroom from time to time to see what the dancers have been working on.

What if my child misses a class?

Please call the studio or send an email. Missed classes are not refunded, but a student can make up the class by attending a similar class at their level. The teacher will tell you which classes are available.

How will I know if the studio is closed?

Studio closings are posted on social media and emailed to parents. If a class is cancelled unexpectedly, families are contacted by email and by text through our notification system. See the 2026–27 closings.

Does my 3-year-old need to be potty trained?

Children in combo classes should be able to go to the bathroom on their own. If they can't yet, an adult needs to stay for the duration of class.

Do adult classes take part in the recital?

Adult classes run year-round and don't have to take part in the recital, unless they want to, of course.
