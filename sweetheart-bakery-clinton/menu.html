Sandwiches, soups & salads

Our girls make the egg and chicken salad from scratch and smoke the pork for the pulled pork. Call (563) 242-3959 to order ahead.

Sandwiches & wraps

Shaved deli sandwiches (ham, turkey, pepperoni, Genoa salami, roast beef), egg salad, chicken salad, BBQ pulled pork, Club, Reuben, Mad Russian, Homer’s Sub, Spicey Sub, grilled chicken ranch wrap and oriental chicken wrap.

Breads: white or wheat kaiser, wheat, dark rye, onion bun, wrap, gluten free, croissant.
Cheese: Swiss, cheddar, American, provolone, pepper jack.

Soups

Made from scratch and changing with the seasons: chicken noodle with homemade noodles, potato bacon, cheese broccoli, goulash and chili. Cup, bowl, pint, quart or gallon. Try a half sandwich & soup.

Salads

Homemade pasta, potato and pea salad by the pound, in 1 and 2 lb containers in the cooler. Chef, grilled chicken and oriental chicken salads. Oreo fluff and strawberry fluff by special order.
