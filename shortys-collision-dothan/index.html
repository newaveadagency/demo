Good to know

Before you come in.

How do I get an estimate?

Call (334) 655-2431 or stop by the shop at 3446 S Brannon Stand Rd, Monday to Thursday between 7:30 AM and 5 PM.

Do you work with insurance companies?

Yes. Customers mention Shorty’s handling the insurance side of the repair, including with a difficult insurance company. Bring your claim information when you come in.

Can I bring my own parts?

No. Shorty’s does not install parts that customers bring in themselves.

Can the repair cost be brought down?

Ask about it. One customer writes that the owner offered to look up aftermarket parts to bring the cost of the repair down.

What are your hours?

Monday to Thursday, 7:30 AM to 5 PM. Closed Friday, Saturday and Sunday.

How can I pay?

Credit cards, debit cards and tap-to-pay (NFC mobile payments).
