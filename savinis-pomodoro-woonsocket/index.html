Our story

Rooted in tradition. Growing through community.

Pomodoro began as the historic Sons of Italy Club in the Italian district of Diamond Hill. Today that heritage is a warm, accessible place for Italian-American food, big celebrations and our own soups and sauces to take home.

1938

The Sons of Italy build on Rathbun Street

The SOI Club constructs our two-story building in the historic Italian district of Diamond Hill.

1980

Doors open on Rathbun Street

Since we first opened our doors, people have come from far and wide just to dine with us.

Restaurant & lounge

Something for everyone.

Pasta, seafood, steak, sandwiches, the soup & salad bar, pizza and more. Come for something quick, a date night or a night out with family and friends – good food and drink suit any occasion.

Two horseshoe bars

Casual lounge dining with booth seating. The bar & lounge stay open later than the kitchen.

Soup & salad bar

Unlimited – build your own salad, hot soups and a fresh bread bar. With an entrée or as a meal on its own.

At the bar

Weekends

Savini's family style dinners

A local tradition derived from old family-style dinners: every Saturday and Sunday, all day, Pomodoro brings out some of our classic recipes.

Good to know

Questions

Do I need a reservation?

Walk-ins are always welcome when there is room, but reservations are honored first. For a group, call 401-762-5114 at least a day in advance.

How many guests can the banquet hall hold?

Up to 300. For smaller parties there are two private VIP rooms.

Do you cater events outside the restaurant?

Yes – prepared foods and pizzas for events at home, at school or at your business.

Is there a gluten-free option?

A gluten-free crust is available for medium pizzas.

Can I order takeout?

Yes – dine in or take out. You can order online here.
