Good to know

Questions we hear often

Do you accept Medicare and Medicaid?
Yes. We accept and bill all major insurers, including Medicaid and Medicare, for your prescriptions and immunizations.
How do I transfer my prescriptions to Dowd Drug?
Give us a call at 641-747-8317 (or use the request form above) and we'll contact your current pharmacy to transfer your prescriptions.
Which immunizations do you offer?
A wide variety, including but not limited to influenza, pneumonia, shingles and Tdap.
Is the medication review really free?
Yes. Wondering whether you're taking your medications properly, at the right times, or have questions about side effects? Sit down with us and we'll review all of those questions and concerns.
Can you bill Medicare for diabetic shoes?
Yes. We can bill Medicare or Medicaid on your behalf. Call to schedule an appointment with our certified therapeutic shoe fitter.
What are your hours?
Monday to Friday 8:30 am – 5:30 pm, Saturday 8:00 am – 1:00 pm, closed Sunday.
