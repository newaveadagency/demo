What you learn

Hands-on skill and the theory to pass.

Students learn in an apprenticeship-style environment, on the job. From day one you learn to provide professional-grade cuts and services under close one-on-one instruction.

  • Cutting and styling techniques, traditional and modern
  • Customer service and administration
  • Business management skills
  • Academic and theoretical tools for the AZ Barber Board license
  • Practice with real clients through community partners

FAQ

Questions before you enroll.

Something else? Call 623-341-5082 or head coach Jerry Ibarra at 602-373-6609.

How much does the barber course cost?

The course is $9,000, with a $1,500 down payment and $500 monthly payments. Payment options are available.

How old do I have to be to enroll?

Licensure requirements are age 16 and up. The course is available to underage applicants.

When can I start?

Hair J's Barber School has open enrollment. Call 623-341-5082 to talk about a start date and schedule.

I'm already a barber and only need my license. Can you help?

Yes. Hair J's has options and discounts for barbers who only need their license. Contact the school for personalized scheduled course options.

I'm licensed in another country. Is there an option for me?

Yes. There are options for barbers licensed in another country. Contact the school for a personalized plan.

I'm a cosmetologist. Can I cross over to a barber license?

Yes. Cosmetologists who need a crossover or barber license can ask about a personalized course option.

Can I train while working or raising a family?

Yes. The school offers individual schedules and payment options for students who balance work and family.
