Our history

From a mall barbershop to a barber school.

Hair J's Barber Shops LLC first opened its doors in 1997 at Desert Sky Mall (formerly Westridge), with several shops soon after. A family-oriented atmosphere, an emphasis on customer service and a commitment to quality barbering have stayed the priority ever since.

Over three decades the team has cultivated the skills of countless barbers in the West Valley, polishing and refining its expertise as styles and trends evolve. With that philosophy, the business created Hair J's Barber School, built on a strong work ethic and quality service.
