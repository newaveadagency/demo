Rentals · 01

Luxury pontoons

Our pontoons are the easiest way to get a whole group out on the water: room to stretch out, a bimini top for shade and a deck made for swimming, fishing and long sunset cruises. Every boat has its own name.

Before you leave the dock we walk you through the boat, and you get a phone number to call if anything comes up.

  • “Idle Time”20′ Northwoods · seats 10–12
  • “Happy Times”22′ Aqua Patio · seats 14
  • “The Minnow”22′ Palm Beach · seats 10–12
  • “Blue Water”22′ Palm Beach · seats 10–12
  • “Bella Rose”22′ Starcraft · seats 10–12
  • “Fish N Chips”22′ Suncruiser · seats 10–12
  • “Decked Out”24′ Weeres · seats 14
  • “Trouble Hook”24′ Starcraft tritoon · seats 15

Fleet can change from season to season. Call for rates per day and to check which boat is free for your dates.

Boat services · 04

Winterization

A thorough process so your boat is stored properly for the winter and ready for the water come spring.

  • Engine oil change
  • Oil filter change
  • Lower unit lube
  • Prop shaft greased
  • Lucas fuel treatment
  • Steering & pivots greased
  • Cylinders fogged
  • Prop pulled to check for fishing line
  • Battery disconnected

Two and four stroke outboards and jet skis. Call 320-834-3939 for this season's rates.

Boat services · 06

Repair & refurbishing

If your boat is having issues and you are anxious to get back on the water, our mechanic can find the problem. Want to upgrade your pontoon? We do that too.

Motor repair

Bring in your boat, the mechanic diagnoses the problem, we call you with a time and cost estimate, and with your approval we make the fix.

Prop rebuild

Broken propeller? Bring in your boat and we will get you back on the water.

Pontoon refurbishing

New flooring and seating. We look at the updates you would like, call you with an estimate and start once you approve.

Trailer repair

Rewiring, plus greasing and replacing bearings. Don't see what you need? Give us a call.
