Boat services

Off season, we take care of your boat

When the docks come out, bring your boat to us. We winterize it, wrap it and store it, and fix what needs fixing so it is ready for the water come spring.

Winterization

Oil and filter change, lower unit lube, fuel treatment, greased steering and pivots, fogged cylinders, prop pulled to check for fishing line, battery disconnected.

Shrink wrapping

Professional-grade shrink wrap protects your boat from the elements and helps extend its life in storage.

Storage & transportation

A safe place for your boat through the winter, including indoor storage, and help getting your boat to us.

Repair & refurbishing

Motor troubleshooting and repair, prop rebuilds, trailer rewiring and bearings, new pontoon flooring and seating.

FAQ

Good to know before you book

What can I rent?

Pontoons, a fishing boat, kayaks, stand up paddle boards and trailers. Call 320-834-3939 to check what is available for your dates.

How do I reserve a boat?

Call 320-834-3939, email garfieldmarinestorage@gmail.com or send the booking request below. A request is not a confirmed booking until we get back to you.

Will someone show me how the pontoon works?

Yes. Before you head out you are walked through the boat and told what you need, and you get a phone number to call if anything comes up.

Do I need a permit to drive a rental pontoon?

Minnesota has rules for operating rental watercraft and boat operator permits. Check the current Minnesota DNR rules before your trip and ask us when you book.

Do you winterize and store boats?

Yes: winterization, shrink wrapping, indoor storage and storage transportation, plus motor repair, trailer repair and pontoon refurbishing.

How can I pay?

Credit and debit cards are accepted.
