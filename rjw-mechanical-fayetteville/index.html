Questions

Straight answers.

Do you offer emergency HVAC service?

Yes. RJW Mechanical offers 24/7 emergency service. Call 479-443-9331.

What is the Comfort Club?

Our service plan. Members get automatic, regular inspection service, priority front-of-the-line service, 15% off repairs, no overtime charges on repairs, a 5-year guarantee on parts we replace and discounts on service calls.

Can I finance a new system?

Yes. We offer financing through Synchrony Financial, and we can link you to the local utility rebates available in your area.

Which areas do you serve?

Washington and Benton Counties, plus parts of Madison and Crawford Counties: Fayetteville, Springdale, Rogers, Bentonville, Bella Vista, Siloam Springs, Prairie Grove, Farmington and many more.

Do you work on commercial systems?

Yes. We install, repair and maintain commercial heating and cooling systems, and we specialize in commercial refrigeration for bars, bakeries, restaurants, supermarkets, schools, warehouses and plants.

How often should my system be serviced?

We recommend an AC tune-up once a year in the spring, before you need to turn the system on, and regular heating maintenance ahead of the cold season. Comfort Club members get this inspection service automatically.
