Furnace replacement

Rising energy bills and uneven temperatures are often caused by an inefficient, aging furnace. Consider replacing it if:

  • It needs frequent repairs

03 · Heat & cool in one

Heat pumps

Heat pumps don’t generate heat, they move it. That is why high-efficiency heat pumps have very low operating costs: they use heat that is already naturally available. One system heats and cools your home.

Air source

Harness heat from the outdoor air. Best for moderate climates, and safe and convenient since they don’t rely on flammable fuels.

Water source

Mostly used commercially, with simultaneous heating and cooling and zone control. A good option for a larger home with a pond or lake.

Geothermal

Ground source systems that collect heat from the earth.

Ductless mini-split

Cheaper to install and easy to zone. Great for homes without ducts and for add-on rooms.
