Questions

What are the hours?

Shank's opens at 8 AM every day. Tuesday through Sunday it closes at 6 PM. For Monday, call 864-621-7214 to check the closing time.

Where is the range?

11506 Anderson Rd, Greenville, SC 29611. Use the Directions button to open it in Google Maps.

Is it wheelchair accessible?

Yes. The range lists a wheelchair-accessible car park and a wheelchair-accessible entrance.

How much is a bucket of balls?

Prices aren't posted online yet. Call the range at 864-621-7214 for today's bucket prices.

Can I email the range?

There is no published email address yet. Call 864-621-7214, or use the note form on the home page to get your question ready before you call.
