The range

A whole field to aim at

From the air you can see all of it: the white clubhouse, a long line of tees, target greens scattered across the field and a practice bunker off to the right, all ringed by trees.

  1. 1Clubhouse
  2. 2Tee line
  3. 3Target green
  4. 4Practice bunker

Buckets of balls

Balls come by the bucket. Grab one and head to the tee line. Shanked one? The owner's advice: shank it off and buy another bucket.

Room to swing

The tee line looks out over a wide, open field ringed by trees, so you can watch every ball land.

Targets out there

Flags and greens are set out across the field at different distances. Pick one and dial in your wedges, irons and driver.

Easy to get in

Wheelchair-accessible parking and a wheelchair-accessible entrance, right off Anderson Road.
