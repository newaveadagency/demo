Our pit

A carnivore's dream since 1999.

Opie's is housed in a metal building with several pit cookers and a couple of pit servers out front. A 12-foot-long smoke pit holds pork chops, pork ribs, sausage, chicken and brisket.

The ambience is friendly and focused on food. Pick your meat, add a side and finish with cobbler. Come join us at Opie's Barbecue, because “Life is good in Spicewood”!

Good to know

Questions at the pit.

How does ordering work at Opie's?
Pick your meat straight from the 12-foot smoke pit out front. It's dipped in our vinegar-based sauce, weighed, wrapped and placed on a cafeteria tray.
What comes with the meat?
Pinto beans, bread, pickles, onions, peppers and warm sauce are included. Butterbeans are served Friday, Saturday and Sunday.
What are your hours?
Monday–Thursday 11–4, Friday and Saturday 11–7, Sunday 11–5, or until the meat runs out daily.
What kinds of sausage do you have?
Three: regular, jalapeño and jalapeño-cheese.
Do you cater?
Yes. Choose pick-up from Opie's, delivery only, full-service off-site catering with a staffed buffet, or a private after-hours event at Opie's. Send a catering request or call (830) 693-8660.
