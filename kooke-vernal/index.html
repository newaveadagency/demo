Good to know

Questions

Do the flavors change?

Yes. Next to the regular favorites there are weekly flavors. A Box of 4 lets you pick any four of the week's flavors. Follow Kookē on Facebook to see what is in the case.

Do you deliver?

Kookē is a hot cookie delivery business, and delivery is on the menu with a delivery fee. Call (435) 781-1987 to set it up.

Do you have gluten friendly or keto cookies?

Yes. Vanilla Sugar and Chocolate Chip come gluten friendly and individually wrapped, and the keto Chocolate Coconutty cookie has coconut, walnuts, pecans and dark chocolate chips.

Can I bake Kookēs at home?

Yes. A Take & Bake bag holds a dozen Chocolate Chip or Party Animal dough balls, ready for your oven.

Do you sell gift cards?

Yes, for a single Kookē and for a Box of 4.
