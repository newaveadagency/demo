Meet your dentist

From Lima to Long Island to Louisiana

Dr. Marcela Jolivette is a bilingual general dentist. She grew up in Long Island after her family migrated from Lima, Peru.

After high school she joined the United States Air Force, where her career in dentistry began as a dental lab technician. She then earned her Bachelor of Science in Biology from Louisiana State University in Baton Rouge and her Doctor of Dental Surgery degree from the LSU School of Dentistry in New Orleans.

She keeps up with continuing education courses on the latest advances in dentistry, and she is passionate about helping underserved people around the world through yearly dental mission trips. Outside the office she enjoys time with her family, running and yoga.

Questions

Good to know

Do you accept Medicaid and insurance?

Yes. Most insurance plans are accepted, including employer PPO plans and Medicaid. We also take major credit cards and cash, and financing is available.

Do you speak Spanish?

Yes. Dr. Jolivette is a bilingual general dentist, and our office proudly serves the Latino community in Spanish. ¡Hablamos español!

What happens at my first visit?

You fill out a short questionnaire, review your medical and dental history with your dentist, have any needed X-rays reviewed, and get a full exam with an oral cancer screening. Your dentist then recommends the right cleaning and explains all findings, and our Patient Care Coordinator goes over fees and your insurance benefits. See every step.

What are your office hours?

Monday to Thursday 8am to 4pm. Saturdays by appointment only. Closed Friday and Sunday.

I have a dental emergency. What should I do?

We offer emergency services. Call (225) 256-6386 and tell us what is going on so we can help you as soon as possible.

Do you see children?

Yes. We provide personalized dental care for each member of your family, including sealants and preventive care for kids.
