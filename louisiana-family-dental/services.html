Your first visit

What to expect, step by step

We realize there are many dental options in the community. Here is how we make your first appointment with us clear and comfortable.

  1. A warm welcome

    You’re greeted by our Patient Care Coordinator and fill out a short questionnaire that becomes part of your dental record.

  2. Your history, together

    You meet your dentist and review your medical and dental history together.

  3. X-rays and a full exam

    Your dentist reviews your radiographs, then performs a dental examination and an oral cancer screening.

  4. A clear plan

    Your dentist recommends the hygiene service that suits your needs and explains all findings. You can ask anything, and some services may be done the same day if you wish.

  5. No surprises on cost

    Before you leave, the Patient Care Coordinator goes over the fee schedule and gives you a summary of your insurance benefits.

  6. Take it home

    You receive a welcome package and a copy of your treatment plan.

Emergencies

Tooth pain can’t wait

We offer emergency services. If you have a toothache, a broken tooth or swelling, call the office and tell us what’s going on.
