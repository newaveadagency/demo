About Biskamp

A Silsbee company, serving Texas and Louisiana since 1976.

Biskamp Electric, Inc. is an electrical and instrumentation contractor that has been serving all of Texas and Louisiana since 1976, and is licensed and bonded. We offer emergency service calls, new construction and repairs with prompt service.

As a merit shop E&I contractor, we are active members of the BBB, ISN Net and the Industrial Safety Training Council. Our mission is simple: “Make Our Clients Successful.”

Safety first

Our goal on every job site: Zero Incidents.

Questions

Good to know.

Can’t find your answer? Call the Silsbee office at (409) 385-4445.

Do you only work on industrial plants?

No. Industrial E&I work is our core, but we also provide a full range of residential and commercial electrical services, including new construction, remodels and service calls.

Are you licensed?

Yes. Biskamp Electric is licensed and bonded and holds Texas Electrical Contractor License TECL #17110.

Where do you work?

We have served Texas and Louisiana since 1976, with offices in Silsbee, Evadale and Orange, Texas.

Do you handle emergency service calls?

Yes. Biskamp Electric offers emergency service calls, and we have service technicians on standby for residential electrical problems. Call (409) 385-4445.

Do you install standby generators?

Yes. Our residential services include Generac generator systems.
