Good to know

Questions we hear a lot

Do I need an appointment?
We take all customers on a first come, first serve basis. Our first focus is the quality of our work, and every vehicle is serviced in as timely a manner as possible.
What do you need to give me a quote?
The year, make and model of your vehicle, plus either the tag or the VIN. That lets us give you an accurate estimate.
Are tire rotations free?
On tires you buy from us, yes: rotations are free for the life of the tire. Otherwise a tire rotation is $25 ($35–50 for heavy-duty tires).
Can I wait while my car is serviced?
Of course. There’s free Wi-Fi and TV in our comfortable customer lounge.
Do you work on fleets, heavy-duty vehicles and box trucks?
Yes. We offer fleet services, and our alignment menu covers heavy-duty vehicles ($125) and box trucks ($165).
Which parts and oil do you use?
We only use parts from reputable brands and Valvoline products, so your vehicle is always ready to drive.
