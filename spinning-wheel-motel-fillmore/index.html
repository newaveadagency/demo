Good to know

Questions before you book

How do I reserve a room?

Call 435-743-6260, or send a request by email with the form below.

How do I pay?

Cash and barter. Credit cards (Discover, MasterCard, Visa, American Express) are not accepted.

Can I park a truck or trailer?

Yes. The court is single level with an easy pull-through loop and extra parking for trucks and trailers.

What is in the room?

Cable TV, WiFi, a phone, a fridge and queen beds. There is an ice and pop machine on the property.

Can I ride my ATV from the motel?

There is ATV access at the back of the lot. Our area map shows the ATV routes through town and out to the Chalk Creek trailhead.
