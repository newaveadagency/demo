Find us &explore Fillmore

Off I-15, onto the business loop, 1.5 miles into town. We are on the east side of Main Street, across from the Utah Territorial Statehouse.

  1. 1

    Get off I-15 at either Fillmore ramp. You are on the business loop.

  2. 2

    Drive 1.5 miles into town on the loop.

  3. 3

    Find the Territorial Statehouse. We are across Main Street, on the east side.

ATV & outdoors

Ride out from the motel

There is ATV access at the back of the lot. The map shows the ATV routes through Fillmore and where they lead:

  • Chalk Creek trailhead, east of town
  • Sand Rock Ridge, to the south-east
  • The airport, Red Dome and the Black Rock Desert, to the west
  • The KOA and the sand pits, to the south

The mountains above town are the Pavant Range in the Fish Lake National Forest.
