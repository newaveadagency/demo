Termites

Over $5 billion a year in damage to American homes.

Termites work around the clock, and within six months without intervention they can cause major structural damage. If you think you have them, call us before it gets worse. We inspect, give you an estimate based on the square footage of the home and the extent of the infestation, and treat with Termidor.

  • Swarming in spring, when termites leave the old colony to start a new one.
  • Dirt in wood, the clearest difference between termite and water damage.
  • Moisture and wood together around the outside of the house.
  • Weak spots such as cracks in the foundation, plumbing and doors.

Questions

Good to know.

Something else? Call 580-704-9270.

Is the spray safe for my family and pets?

For homes we use an odorless, water-based spray that is not harmful to you or your pets, and it won’t leave stains on carpet or wood flooring.

How long does a home treatment last?

We spray the inside and outside of your home, and the results typically last for 2–3 months.

How is a termite estimate worked out?

After an inspection confirms termites, the estimate is based on the square footage of the home and the extent of the infestation. We treat with Termidor.

Do you work with real estate agents?

Yes. We help prepare properties for the market, most often with termite and rodent control, and provide the reports and paperwork for the future residents.

Do I need pets to get fleas or ticks?

No. Ticks and fleas can be carried in by people or on items brought into the house.

How do you deal with rodents?

Either catch-remove-release, with traps and lockboxes around the home and in the attic, or a dehydrating rodenticide that sends the animal back outside to search for water, so it doesn’t die inside your home.
