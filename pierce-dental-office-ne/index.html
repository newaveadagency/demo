[ 05 ]  Questions

Good to know before you come in.

Which insurance plans is the office contracted with?

Pierce Dental Office is a participating (PPO) provider with Ameritas, Delta Dental Companies, Blue Cross Blue Shield and Principal. For other insurance, the office files your primary claim as a courtesy.

How can I pay?

Cash, checks, Visa, Discover, Mastercard and American Express. CareCredit patient payment plans are also accepted. Payment or your estimated co-payment is due at the time of service unless other arrangements were made in advance.

Is there a discount for paying in full?

Yes. A 7% courtesy applies to services paid in full by cash or check on the day of service or in advance. It is not available to Blue Cross Blue Shield, Delta Dental, Ameritas and Principal subscribers, because those plans pay the office directly with a PPO fee adjustment.

What should I bring to my first visit?

Please print and fill out the new patient forms (Patient Information, Medical History and Financial Policy) so your first visit goes faster, and bring your dental insurance details.

How is payment handled for crowns, bridges or dentures?

For work that needs a dental lab, such as crowns, bridges, partials, dentures and orthodontics, at least half of the fee is due at the impression and the balance when the work is placed.
