Our team

The people behind every Westside smile.

Two dentists and a team of hygienists, assistants and coordinators.

Brandon E. Beaver, DDS

Dentist · Co-founder

Associate’s degree from Murray State College, a bachelor’s in Biology/Chemistry from Southeastern Oklahoma State University, and his Doctor of Dental Surgery from the University of Oklahoma College of Dentistry in 2002.

Since 2002 he has provided comprehensive, restorative, implant, sedation and cosmetic dentistry in private practice, and is one of the few dentists in Oklahoma licensed in IV sedation and implant surgery.

Away from the office: Lake Texoma, Dallas Cowboys and Oklahoma State football, hunting, fishing and a herd of registered Black Angus cattle.

Taylor C. Marcum, DDS

Dentist · Co-founder

Bachelor of Biotechnology from Southeastern Oklahoma State University and Doctor of Dental Surgery from the University of Oklahoma College of Dentistry in 2013, followed by a one-year residency in advanced general dentistry focused on implants and cosmetic dentistry.

“I love practicing general dentistry and can treat most dental conditions.”

He enjoys time with his wife Heather, is an avid sports fan and loves being outdoors.
