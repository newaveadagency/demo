[08] FAQ

Good to know.

Q/01Do you come to me?

Yes. V.I.P is a mobile detailing service: the detail is done at your driveway or your office.

Q/02Which areas do you cover?

Baton Rouge, Baker, Central, Zachary, Denham Springs, Watson, Prairieville, Port Allen, Brusly and Addis.

Q/03What are your hours?

Every day of the week, Monday to Sunday, 7 AM to 7 PM.

Q/04Do trucks and SUVs cost more?

Not on the listed prices: a full detail is $285 and a full interior detail is $200 for cars and for trucks and SUVs alike.

Q/05How can I pay?

Checks, credit and debit cards (Visa, MasterCard, American Express, Discover) and NFC mobile payments.

Q/06Is any job too big or too small?

No job is too big or small, from a quick engine bay clean to a full detail with paint correction.
