Good to know

Before you come in.

How do I sign up for a class?

Call the store at 412-308-6236 or stop in. The next Quilting 101 session is scheduled as spots fill, so ask to be on the list.

Where is the calendar?

Each month's newsletter comes with the calendar for open sews, Kathy's Korners and Yarn Time, including holiday closures. You can sign up to get it by email.

Newsletter sign-up

Can I learn to knit or crochet?

Yes. Come talk to Emily during a Yarn Time and see what can be set up for lessons.

Do you offer anything for charity sewing?

In 2026 we sew dresses for Dress A Girl Around the World (10% off fabric bought for the dresses) and make beds and toys for Angel Ridge Animal Sanctuary and Bailey's (10% off yarn for the project).
