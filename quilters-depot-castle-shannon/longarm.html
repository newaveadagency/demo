Before you bring it in

Seven steps, then meet with Jenn.

  1. Batting. You provide the batting. It can be purchased at Quilters Depot.
  2. Backing. You provide the backing. Check out our wide selection of 108" wide fabrics.
  3. Iron and hang. Tops and backings must be ironed and hung on a hanger to keep from wrinkling. “I do not iron!”
  4. Mark the top. Pin a safety pin at the top of each piece, including the batting, so the top and any directional design go on the machine right.
  5. Extra all around. Have 2 to 3 inches of extra backing and batting on all sides of the quilt.
  6. Trim the strings. All strings should be cut before giving the quilt to Jennifer.
  7. Pieced backing? Use a half-inch seam and press it open.
Please don't just drop off. You'll meet with Jenn to go over what you want done and her ideas before she accepts the quilt. Quilts are accepted only when Jenn is in the store and the steps above are done. If you're not ready when called, you move to the bottom of the waiting list.
