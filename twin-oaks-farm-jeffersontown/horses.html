Our horses

Gentle, well-trained,and full of character.

Our lesson and share board horses come in all levels. Every one of them has a story, and a few were born right here at Twin Oaks.

Simon

"One Trick Pony" · 1998 red roan Appaloosa

Kentucky State 4H Hunter under Saddle Champion Pony and 2nd in the state over fences. A brave, willing jumper who also excels in western events. Be sure to ask to see his tricks!

Lyric

"Will She Be A Diva" · 1999 Paint/TB/Hanoverian

A bay tobiano mare and a treasure to ride. Awesome gaits and able to do many advanced movements.

Daisee

"Boston Country Girl" · 2000 AQHA black mare

Likes the quiet, respectful rider. The easier you are, the better she is, but she will test an inconsistent rider.

Kriquet

"Glitter 'N Gold" · 2003 Quarter/Haflinger

A quiet, easy going sorrel gelding who will definitely test your leadership skills. Awesome on the trail and great over fences.

Cruise

"Chips Dunnit Again" · 2003 AQHA red dun

Born at Twin Oaks, son of Jolene and grandson of Chips Chocolate Chip and Zippo Pine Bar. Debbie is a bit selfish about riding him, but a few good students get the honor.

Honey

"Terras Parr Scooter" · 2003 AQHA palomino

A palomino mare and a trail horse delight.

Rooster Cogburn

2010 bay roan Appaloosa gelding

An awesome, forward-thinking little guy.

Seven

"Cowboy Steppin Whiskey" · 1997 sorrel Paint

In high demand! Looks of "get up and go" but quiet with the right rider.
