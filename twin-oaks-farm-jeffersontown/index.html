Questions

Before you come out.

Do I need my own horse to take lessons?

No. We have gentle, well-trained horses of all levels available for lessons and share boarding.

What disciplines do you teach?

Western and Huntseat, all based on a firm foundation of Dressage principles and Natural Horsemanship. Our students range from young and older beginners to advanced riders interested in showing.

How much is full care boarding?

Full care boarding is $500 a month. It includes matted stalls cleaned daily, hay, grain and water twice daily, daily turnout, salt, and feeding of owner-provided supplements. Horses that need additional hay and grain to hold their weight have additional fees.

What if I need to cancel a lesson?

Please give notice by 10:00 AM on the day of your lesson (6:00 PM Friday for Saturday lessons) by calling or texting 502-432-2623. Missed lessons can be made up within 60 days. Without notice, a $25 late-notice payment is due before your next ride.

Can parents watch lessons?

Yes. Parents, family and friends are always welcome to watch, and to help younger students get their horse ready. During riding time we ask them to observe only, so the rider can focus on the two or three things that matter most.

Is sales tax included in the prices?

No. All lesson fees are subject to 6% sales tax.
