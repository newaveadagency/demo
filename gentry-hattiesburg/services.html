How it works

Walk-ins only.

  1. Come by

    Monday to Friday, between 8 AM and 5 PM, at 4600 Hardy St, Suite 24.

  2. Take a seat

    No appointment and no app. Have a seat in the lounge with the leather couches.

  3. Get your cut

    Prices are on the board. Credit and debit cards are accepted; military, law enforcement, first responders and seniors (65+) get 10 percent off.

Questions

Good to know.

Do I need an appointment?

No. The Gentry takes walk-in haircuts only, so there is nothing to book. Just come in during opening hours.

When are you open?

Monday to Friday, 8 AM to 5 PM. The shop is closed on Saturday and Sunday.

How much is a haircut?

A shear and clipper cut is $25 and a clipper cut is $21. Straight razor prices vary, so ask your barber.

Who gets the 10 percent discount?

Military, law enforcement officers, first responders and seniors (65+) receive 10 percent off.

Do you cut kids' hair?

Yes. The Gentry offers barbering services for gentlemen of all ages.

How can I pay?

Credit and debit cards are accepted.

Is the shop wheelchair accessible?

Yes. The entrance, the parking and the restroom are wheelchair accessible.
