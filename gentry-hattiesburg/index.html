A barber shop for gentlemen of all ages.

The Gentry, est. 2003, sits in Suite 24 at 4600 Hardy Street: a long row of barber chairs, a lounge with leather couches and a hand-lettered chalkboard with every price on it.

There is no booking system and no app. For your convenience, The Gentry takes walk-in haircuts only, Monday to Friday from 8 to 5.
