01 · Jewelry repair

The right specialist for every repair

Instead of depending on one person to be great at everything, Sanders Jewelry uses jewelers who have dedicated themselves to their own field: diamond setting, pavé setting, watch repair and more. That is how the work comes back at top quality, in a timely fashion.

  • Broken necklaces, chains and bracelets
  • Loose or missing diamonds, prongs and settings
  • Worn or broken shanks, including arthritic shanks
  • Tangled delicate chains
  • Heirlooms reworked: rings turned into pendants, family gold remade into a new ring

02 · Ring sizing

Sized up, sized down, engraving kept

Engagement rings, wedding bands and family pieces, whether you bought them here or somewhere else. Engraving and design details are preserved through the resize.

  • Ring sizing for rings bought at other stores
  • Large resizes on heirloom rings
  • Ring cutting when a finger is swollen, or before surgery, then repair and resizing afterwards
  • Bracelet sizing
