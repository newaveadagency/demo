Our story

A fine jeweler, tucked away on Southbridge Parkway.

Sanders Jewelry is located in the “Magic City” of Birmingham. Founder and owner Gregory Sanders has over 30 years of experience in the jewelry business and is dedicated to top-quality, finely crafted jewelry at competitive prices.

The store specializes in bridal, fashion jewelry and expert jewelry and watch repair. And the specialty we are proudest of is customer service that is second to none.

“Because I keep my overhead and advertising costs low I am able to pass those savings on to my customers.”

Greg Sanders, owner

How we repair

Not one jeweler for everything. The right one for each job.

We did the research so you don’t have to, and put together a team of jewelers who each dedicate themselves to being the best in their own field.

Good to know

Before you come in

We are a little off the beaten path, inside an office building. Here is how to find us.

Where exactly is Sanders Jewelry?

Inside the office building at 2000A Southbridge Parkway, Suite 420, Birmingham, AL 35209. There is on-site parking and a wheelchair-accessible entrance.

What if the building door is locked?

The building locks when the guard leaves. Call (205) 414-8477 and we will send someone down to let you in.

Who works on my jewelry?

A team of jewelers who each specialize in their own field, such as diamond setting, pavé setting and watch repair, instead of one person trying to be great at everything.

Can you make a custom piece?

Yes: engagement rings, wedding bands, earrings, necklaces and bracelets, designed around your idea, price range and timeline. Pictures help, so bring any you have. Start a custom request.

Do you clean jewelry?

Yes. Drop by anytime for a free cleaning.

What are your hours?

Monday to Friday, 10 AM to 5 PM. Closed Saturday and Sunday.
