03

Standby & portable generators

WireMaster Electric, LLC is an Authorized Generac Service Dealer. This means we are factory certified to sell, install, maintain and service air-cooled standby Generac generators. A standby generator will automatically restore power to your home or business during a power outage. We are also able to install generators from other manufacturers.

  • Generac standby generator sales & installation
  • Installation of other manufacturers’ generators
  • Repairs, with most Generac parts in stock
  • Maintenance plans
  • Maintenance kits, oil and air filters, spark plugs and oil for Generac air-cooled units
  • Manual transfer switch & exterior generator inlet for portable generators

Why a transfer switch? It prevents the dangerous feedback of electricity from your generator to the utility’s power lines. And routine maintenance is the most important thing you can do to make sure the generator is ready for an emergency.
