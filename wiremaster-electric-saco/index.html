03 Standby power

When the power goes out, yours comes back on.

A standby generator automatically restores power to your home or business during an outage. As an Authorized Generac Service Dealer we are factory certified to sell, install, maintain and service air-cooled Generac standby generators, and we install generators from other manufacturers too.

  • Most Generac parts in stock for timely repairs
  • Maintenance kits, oil, filters and spark plugs on hand, plus maintenance plans
  • Portable generators safely connected with a manual transfer switch and exterior inlet

07 Questions

Good to know.

Do you offer free estimates?
Yes. Call (207) 651-3426 or send the form below to arrange a free estimate, and we will discuss your project with you.
Is emergency service available?
Yes, WireMaster Electric offers 24/7 emergency service.
Which areas do you serve?
Our service area covers most of Southern Maine, from our base in Saco. We are licensed and insured in both Maine and New Hampshire.
Do you only install Generac generators?
No. We are an Authorized Generac Service Dealer for air-cooled standby generators, and we are also able to install generators from other manufacturers.
Can my portable generator power the house safely?
Yes, with a manual transfer switch and an exterior generator inlet. The switch prevents dangerous feedback of electricity from your generator into the utility’s power lines.
Do you remove knob-and-tube wiring?
Yes. We rewire older homes and businesses, remove knob-and-tube wiring, upgrade fuses to circuit breakers and correct code violations.
