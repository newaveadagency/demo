Hand tools & lumber

Hammers, screwdrivers, hand saws, files and vises, plus select lumber from 2×4s to plywood, including appearance grade.

Plumbing & heating

The plumbing aisle got so large it has its own room: schedule 40 and fittings, sewer and drain, copper, PEX and all the parts. New faucets and toilets, electric and propane water heaters, pumps, furnace parts, and galvanized or black stovepipe.

Paint & sundries

Interior and exterior Valspar, with color matching from another brand's color name and number. Brushes, trays, roller covers, sandpaper, fillers and putties.

Electrical

Building materials

Concrete, mortar mix, blocks, Sonotube and rebar. Roll and blue board insulation, felt paper, joint compound, corner bead and tape, weatherstripping, door hardware, and a full aisle of fasteners.

Farm & feed

Fencing, posts, fence tape, buckets, poultry fountains and feeders, bucket deicers, float valves, poultry netting and heat lamps. Out in the barn: Blue Seal feed for chickens, horses, goats, pigs, rabbits, birds and sheep. Blue Seal orders go in every other Thursday and arrive the next day.

Lawn & garden

Vermont Natural Ag products, pine, cedar and hemlock mulch, Scotts products and lime. Organic seeds from High Mowing Seeds and Burpee, starter kits, weed block, and grass seed by the pound or in 25 and 50 lb bags. Shovels, rakes, hoes and wheelbarrows too.

FAQ

Good to know.

What are your hours?

Monday to Friday 7 AM to 6 PM, Saturday 8 AM to 4 PM and Sunday 9 AM to 1 PM.

Can you order something you don't have in stock?

Yes. We find it in our Do it Best catalog or online and add it to our regular weekly order, usually ready to pick up on Tuesday. You can also buy online at doitbest.com and have it delivered to the store.

Do you fill propane tanks?

Yes, tanks of any size, as long as the tank is no older than 10 years. There is also a tank exchange outside our back door that takes tanks of any age or condition.

When does Blue Seal feed come in?

We place Blue Seal orders every other Thursday and they are delivered the next day, Friday. We can order any special item you're looking for.

Can I bring my dog?

Yes. We love having pets visit, and they get a biscuit while you shop.
