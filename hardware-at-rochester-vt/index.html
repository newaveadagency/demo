Our story

Same building, same Main Street.

The business began around 1890, and the building on Main Street was built at the same time.

  1. c. 1890The store opens under the name Campbell and Martin.
  2. 1919The firm is the largest plumbing and heating business in the upper valley. The building also housed the Rochester Town Office, the Post Office, a meat market and a barber shop over the years.
  3. 1961After seven decades with relatives of the founders, the store is sold to new local owners.
  4. 2016Dave and Pat Harvey take over The Hardware.
