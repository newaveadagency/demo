Questions

Good to know

Which plows do Groundz Keepers fit?

Groundz Keepers are made for Western, Fisher, SnowEx, Snow Dogg, Meyer and Blizzard plows, and for UTVs. We can also customize a Groundz Keeper for any cutting edge, so call if yours isn’t listed.

I have a Boss plow. Where do I order?

Through any Boss dealer. We supply Boss with our Groundz Keepers, sold as Boss Turf Defenders.

What kinds of welding do you do?

MIG, TIG, arc and gas welding, on aluminum, steel, stainless steel and cast iron.

Can you design something from scratch?

Yes. We offer design services from start to finish, and on-site fabrication, welding and repair services for all types of metalwork and materials.
