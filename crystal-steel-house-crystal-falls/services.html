05 · Our top product

Groundz Keeper

The Groundz Keeper is a snow plow attachment that takes the mess out of early snow removal, when thawed ground would otherwise be torn up by a conventional plow. It is essentially a steel tube that encapsulates the cutting edge, for smoother, less aggressive first pushes of snow.

The patented brackets are welded along the entire length of pipe to withstand harsh impacts. Each set is offered as HD with 1/4″ AR400 plate pads for heavy plowing of gravel. Tested for two years by Boss and designed to last.

Patent No. US D799,554 S · October 10, 2017
