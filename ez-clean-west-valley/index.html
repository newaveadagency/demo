05 / FAQ

Good to know

Anything else? Text a photo of the stain or the couch to (385) 218-3192.

Can I text instead of calling?
Yes. EZ Clean takes calls and texts at the same number, (385) 218-3192.
Do I need an appointment?
Yes. Cleanings are by appointment and done on site, at your home or business. Call or text to set a time.
Can you clean a couch or a sectional?
Yes. Couches, microfiber sectionals and chairs are cleaned on site, including furniture with a strong smell.
How does the cleaning work?
Chad looks over the carpet first and tells you what he can do about each stain. Every area is pretreated, then a second pass rinses and extracts the dirt.
Do you do tile, hard floors or businesses?
Yes. Besides carpet, EZ Clean cleans tile and grout and hard floors, and works in offices and clinics as well as homes. See services.
