Carpet cleaning

Whole houses, single rooms, bedrooms, closets and halls. Before anything starts, the carpet is looked over so you know what can be done about each stain.

Then every area is pretreated, and a second pass rinses the carpet and suctions the dirt out.

  • Pretreatment of all areas
