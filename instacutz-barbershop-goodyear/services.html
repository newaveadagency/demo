§ 02 — What you get

Four classics, done right.

01

Traditional Haircut

A standard cut for a shorter, traditional men's style, using shears or clippers as the barber sees fit, then cleaning the neckline with clippers and finishing with our signature shoulder massage.

02

Specialty Cut

The artistic talents of our barbers and stylists, using their skills to meet your needs: a flat top, a skin fade or the like.

03

Hot Shave

Pre-shave oils and hot towels prepare the skin for a close shave with a straight razor outfitted with a protective guard, followed by after-shave moisturizer and even more relaxing hot towels.

04

Beard & Mustache Trim

Clippers or shears to trim or shape facial hair, followed by an optional razor line-up of the jaw, neck and cheeks. Finished with a relaxing hot towel.

§ 05 — FAQ

Do I need an appointment?

No. You can check in online with Barbershop Check-In, or book a set time online. Appointments are an additional $10.

Do you cut kids' hair?

Yes. Boy's haircuts are part of what we do, and a kids haircut is $30.

Are there discounts?

We are proud to offer a $5 discount to all service men, and Tuesdays are $20 for all seniors.
