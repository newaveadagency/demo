§ 01 — The Barbershop

Welcome home, gentlemen.

We are a West Valley barbershop in Goodyear, AZ, specializing in men's haircuts, boy's haircuts, clipper cuts, straight-razor shaves, flat tops, line-ups, edge-ups and fades. Our beard, mustache and goatee trimming and shave services keep you looking sharp.

Come hang out with us in a relaxed, friendly environment. We're a short drive from Buckeye and Verrado, in "The Market at Estrella Falls" plaza on the northeast corner of McDowell Rd and Pebble Creek Parkway / Estrella Parkway, walking distance from C-A-L Ranch.

“There were a lot of dirt fields. So, we were like, ‘hey, it's going to start booming over here.’”

Saul Enriquez, co-founder, to ABC15 Arizona on opening the shop in 2018
