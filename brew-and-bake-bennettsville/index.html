Our story

From summers with a great aunt to Cheraw Street

Owner Lora Biron learned cake decorating as a kid, spending summers with her great aunt in Oklahoma. That’s where the love of desserts, and of making everything look pretty, began.

After almost 23 years in New Hampshire, working in sales and baking on the side, Lora moved to Bennettsville to be closer to family. Her booth at the quarterly Downtown Market kept selling out, and in November 2021 Brew & Bake opened its doors in the former Sandhill Connextions office, renovated over five months by Lora and her family.

The goal is simple: a hearty, simple breakfast, good coffee, and baked goods worth getting up early for.

Good to know

Questions

When should I come in?

Early is best. Signature items often run out, so for the best selection visit in the morning. Brew & Bake opens at 6 am Tuesday to Friday and 8 am on Saturday.

Do you have a drive-through?

Yes. You can dine in or grab your coffee and breakfast from the drive-through window.

How far ahead should I order a cake?

Specialty cakes should be ordered 1–2 weeks ahead, and catering or specialty baked goods are typically booked 2–3 weeks out. Orders placed the day before or the same day carry a last-minute fee, so please call to ask about availability.

Do you have sugar-free or dairy-free options?

There is a sugar-free blueberry jumbo muffin and sugar-free syrups (caramel, hazelnut, French vanilla, vanilla bean, brown sugar cinnamon and chai spice). Drinks can be made with almond, oat, coconut or soy milk.

Do you serve boba?

Yes. Bubble tea in vanilla, matcha, black, brown sugar, Thai and taro, plus popping pearls and jellies in lots of fruit flavors.
