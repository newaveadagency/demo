Good to know

Questions

Can I choose my own body shop after an accident?

Yes. You have the right to choose your auto body repair facility, so select one you know and trust. We work with all insurance companies and with self-pay clients.

How do I get an estimate?

Come in for a free computerized estimate that is detailed, accurate and fair. Estimates are written Monday through Friday, 8:30 am to 3:00 pm, or by appointment. Call 515-955-8812.

Do you do mechanical repairs too?

Yes, some general auto repair: brakes, mufflers and pipes, shocks and struts, air conditioning, belts and hoses, cooling systems, starters, alternators and front end work. Call us with your questions.

What else can ArmorThane be sprayed on?

Besides truck beds: boat and marine vehicles, trailers, Jeep tubs, horse and livestock trailers, RVs, rocker panels, roll cages, tool boxes, van interiors, undercarriages and much more.

How should I care for fresh paint?

For the first 30 days, clean with a water rinse only, and do not wax for the first 60 days. Wash by hand, out of the hot sun, and avoid car washes with brushes. More tips on the services page.
