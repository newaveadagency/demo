Car tips

New paint care

Keep your new finish in superb condition with these suggestions.

Park in a garage or under a roof whenever possible. Avoid parking under trees: sap and bird droppings can damage the finish.

Wash the car frequently, preferably by hand. During the first 30 days, clean it with a water rinse only.

For heavy soil, use a mild liquid soap (no detergent). Don't wash your car in the hot sun.

Never wipe the car with a dry cloth. Avoid automatic car washes with brushes; stiff brushes can scratch the finish.

Don't let gasoline or anti-freeze stand on the surface. Do not wax for the first 60 days after painting.

Have paint nicks touched up as soon as they occur to protect against rust. Never scrape ice or snow off painted surfaces.
