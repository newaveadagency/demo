Questions

Good to know.

Do I need an appointment?

Appointments are recommended. Call 302-674-5685, Monday to Friday between 8:00 AM and 4:30 PM, to set a time.

Can you help if I don't live near Dover?

Yes. The office lists its bookkeeping and accounting services as available remotely. Call to talk through how to send your documents.

Do you work with small businesses and LLCs?

Yes. Rich and his team prepare business taxes, keep books for small businesses and answer LLC and tax questions, and Rich works alongside clients' own bookkeepers too.

What should I bring to my tax appointment?

A photo ID, last year's return, all W-2 and 1099 forms, records of deductions such as mortgage interest, property tax, childcare and charitable gifts, and any letters from the IRS or the State of Delaware. The checklist above has the full list.

Is the office accessible?

Yes. The office has a wheelchair-accessible entrance, parking and restroom.
