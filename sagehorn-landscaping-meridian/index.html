Why it pays

A good landscape is a good investment.

High quality landscape construction and maintenance increases the value and beauty of your home or business for years to come.

  1. Increase the value of your propertyQuality installation and upkeep add lasting value to your home or business.
  2. Green the environmentNew plants, shrubs and flowers contribute to the “greening” of the environment.
  3. Maximize usable outdoor spaceHardscape construction such as paver patios, concrete, retaining walls and more.
  4. Attract customers to your businessA well-kept landscape draws people to your store, and satisfied tenants mean lease renewals.
  5. Create your private sanctuaryA unique surrounding that adds character to your home with a sense of hominess.

Questions

Good to know

Which areas do you serve?
Sagehorn Landscaping serves the entire Treasure Valley, including Eagle, Meridian, Kuna, Star, Caldwell, Boise and the surrounding areas.
Do you work on homes and businesses?
Yes. Sagehorn is a full-service landscape, installation, maintenance and irrigation firm specializing in small to medium size residential and commercial projects.
How do I get an estimate?
Call 208-794-2770, email mike@sagehornlandscaping.com, or send the form on this page to schedule a free estimate.
What does an installation package include?
Installation packages generally include a concrete patio, sprinklers, topsoil importation, sod, lawn edging, mulch or rock installation and a number of trees and shrubs. A custom package can also be put together to suit your needs.
Can maintenance be tailored to my property?
Yes. Regular maintenance programs can be tailored to your needs and feature weed control, mowing, blowing, edging, pruning and scheduled fertilization, as well as general clean-up.
