Careers & externships

Grow with a privately owned mixed animal practice.

We do approximately 75% small animal and 25% large animal work, in a great little northwest Missouri town about an hour and a half from downtown Kansas City.

Veterinarians

We are seeking full-time veterinarians who want to work in a mixed animal practice, with a 3-month mentoring program by two very experienced veterinarians at the start of employment.

  • Health insurance, paid time off, flexible schedule
  • Retirement plan (3% matching SIMPLE IRA)
  • Professional development assistance
  • Missouri, AVMA and DEA dues paid
  • Additional emergency call pay, employee discount
  • Salary competitive and negotiable based on experience

Requires a DVM or equivalent from an accredited university and Missouri licensure and accreditation.

Send your inquiry to info@youngvetclinic.com

Externships

Year-round for 3rd and 4th year veterinary students from U.S. accredited schools, for any length of time, tailored to your interests and school requirements. Undergraduates and 1st/2nd year students can contact us about shadowing.

  • Two free bedrooms with kitchen and bath, 4 minutes from the clinic (first come, first served)
  • Your own transportation is not required
  • Send a letter of intent, a letter from your school (or graded externship form) and a copy of your health insurance card

Externship inquiry form · materials to youngveterinaryclinic@gmail.com
