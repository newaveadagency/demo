How do I book a barber appointment?

Michael (MJ) James takes appointments through the theCut app at book.thecut.co/miketheotherbaber. You can also call the shop at (601) 342-5949.

How do I book with La'Neil on the beauty side?

La'Neil (Nell'd It hair artistry & design) books through Vagaro at vagaro.com/thehairpalace2, or call the shop at (601) 342-5949.

Do you cut kids' hair?

Yes. The barber menu has kids' cuts under 13 ($15) and for 13 and over ($20), including taper fades, mohawks and high-tops.

Are design and graphics priced separately?

Yes. Designs and graphics start at $20 and are priced separately from the haircut, so total time and cost can vary.

When are you open?

Wednesday and Thursday 8 AM to 5 PM, Friday 8 AM to 6 PM, Saturday 7 AM to 12 PM. Closed Sunday through Tuesday.

Is there parking and wheelchair access?

Yes. There is on-site parking, a wheelchair-accessible entrance and a wheelchair-accessible car park.
