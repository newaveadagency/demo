Our story

It started with one slide bed truck.

In July 1991 D & S Tow, Inc. bought its first tow truck, a slide bed used to service the wrecking yard we ran at the time. We grew into a fleet of over 30 late model car carriers, wrecker tow trucks and service trucks.

In more than 35 years in the automotive industry we ran an auto body shop, a wrecking yard, an insurance salvage pool, an auction company and a used auto dealership. In the end we found we had one thing to sell: our service.

  1. 1991First tow truck, a slide bed for the wrecking yard
  2. 2013Primary AAA provider in the Carson City, NV territory (November)
  3. Today30+ trucks working out of Sparks and Carson City

“Some might say it’s unusual for a woman to own a towing company, and they’d be right. But it’s what I know and love, so I’m all in.”

Susan Zinser President/CEO · the “S” in D & S Tow

Susan and her husband built D & S Tow together, along with several other automotive businesses. In 2009, as they approached their planned exit, the economy turned and she lost her “D” to cancer. She chose to continue, rebuild and grow the company into what it is today: built on integrity and trust.

Good to know

Quick answers.

Can I get a tow outside office hours?

Yes. Our services are available 24 hours. The office is open Monday to Friday, 8 am to 5 pm, and weekends by appointment. Call for after-hours office access.

Are you a AAA provider?

Yes. Since November 2013 we have been the primary AAA provider in the Carson City, NV territory.

Can you move RVs and heavy equipment?

Yes. Besides light and medium duty towing, we provide Landoll service for big equipment and RVs.

Where are your yards?

1590 Marietta Way, Sparks, NV 89431 and 3051 N Deer Run Rd, Carson City, NV 89701.

Do you sell vehicles at auction?

Yes. We hold sealed-bid vehicle auctions at both lots. Photo ID is required to bid and all vehicles are sold as is, where is. See the auction terms.
