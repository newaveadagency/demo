Drivers & office staff

We’re willing to train the right person.

All too often the right mechanically inclined, hardworking and safe-driving person makes the best operator. D & S Tow has trained most of the drivers in this area. You could be the next one.

  • Clean DMV printout
  • Drug free
  • Basic automotive experience
  • Self starter, professional appearance and attitude, organized
  • Class A or C license
  • Extensive background check required

Applying for clerical / dispatcher? Add a cover letter that answers:

  • Do you know how important a dispatcher is? This is not a “stop-over” job, so how can you be part of our team?
  • What do you know about vehicles? Are you familiar with the automotive industry? Ever had your car towed?
  • Have you ever managed in a work setting? How do you work with upset customers?
  • What relaxes you? What angers you? How do you calm down?
  • What can you contribute to help grow our team and streamline our efficiency?
