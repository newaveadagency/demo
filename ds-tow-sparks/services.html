Auction terms & conditions

  1. Sealed bidding starts Thursday at 10 am before auction day and continues until 10 am on auction day. No bids are taken after the cutoff.
  2. Results are announced by 11 am Friday, by email or phone.
  3. Photo ID is required for everyone bidding on auction vehicles.
  4. All vehicles must be paid for by 2 pm on auction day and picked up by noon the following Monday.
  5. Paperwork is ready no sooner than 2 pm on auction day unless other instructions are stated.
  6. All sales are final, no refunds for any reason. Vehicles are sold “as is, where is”.
  7. Anyone tampering with an auction vehicle will be escorted from the property and lose all bidding rights.
  8. Buyers are responsible for custody of all paperwork. Duplicate paperwork is $50.00.
  9. Vehicles not picked up by Monday noon are charged storage at $41.00 per day, no exceptions.
  10. A $35.00 charge applies for every forklift, service charge or facilitation fee. Making keys may cost extra; if so, it is noted in the catalog on auction day.
  11. The catalog is a buyer’s guide only and should not be relied on as fact. Breaking these rules may end all bidding rights.
