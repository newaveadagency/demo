Our commitment to you

Preventing disease is less costly and more rewarding than correcting problems once they occur.

Our dental office is committed to your improved health, and our main emphasis is on prevention. Good health is the result of good habits, proper nutrition and regular professional care.

Our staff has been selected for their caring, friendly attitudes as well as their technical skills. We understand the needs of our patients and take great pride in our work. Feel free to ask anyone for assistance. We are all here to serve you.

Meet the dentist

Dr. Randon Prather, DMD

Dr. Prather earned his dental degree at the University of Louisville School of Dentistry and came home to the area to practice. Before that he studied at Bethel University in McKenzie, Tennessee, on the Hendricks scholarship, graduating Summa Cum Laude in Biology and named a Hutchen’s scholar for the highest GPA in his class.

He planned on medical school, until working as a dental assistant convinced him to pursue dentistry. Since graduating he has continued his training in orthodontics, implant dentistry and endodontics, and he plans to keep expanding his techniques and technology.

He lives in Harvey with his wife Angelica, who also runs the front office, and spends his spare time on a hobby farm with chickens, turkeys, ducks, geese, goats, sheep and a cow.

Questions

Good to know before you visit.

Will you file my dental insurance?
We can file most insurance claims for you. Please bring your insurance plan information with you on your first visit, and we will work with you to get the maximum benefits you are entitled to.
I don’t have dental insurance. Do you have a plan?
Yes. The Discount Wellness Program covers all cleanings, x-rays and exams for the year, plus 15% off any additional treatment. See the prices above or call (270) 527-1479.
I get very nervous at the dentist. Can you help?
Yes. Dr. Prather and key members of the team received extensive training in sedation dentistry. After a consultation you take a small pill and snooze through the appointment, and our team monitors you the entire time. More about sedation.
Do you treat children?
Yes, we care for the whole family. Benton Elementary kindergarten classes have even visited the office during Children’s Dental Health Month to learn how to take care of their teeth.
What should I bring to my first appointment?
Your insurance plan information, the completed New Patient Form and the signed acknowledgement of our Notice of Privacy Practices. Ask the front office for both forms.
