01 · Family dentistry

Prevention comes first.

Our main emphasis is on prevention: preventing disease is less costly and more rewarding than correcting problems once they occur. Regular cleanings, x-rays and exams, good habits and proper nutrition keep teeth healthy for the whole family.

When a tooth does need work, new tooth-colored filling materials can be used in a wider range of cases, so you can keep your smile as white as it can be. Patients without insurance can cover their cleanings, x-rays and exams through our Discount Wellness Program.

02 · Sedation dentistry

Snooze through your appointment.

Dr. Prather, along with key members of the dental team, received extensive training in sedation (“sleep”) dentistry. After a consultation, you take a small pill about an hour before your visit. You are not unconscious and can still respond, but patients describe it as seeming to be asleep and usually remember little or nothing of the appointment. No IV, tubes or needles are needed for the sedation.

A small sensor on your fingertip lets the team monitor your vital signs the entire time. A companion drives you to the office and home again.

  • High anxiety or past traumatic dental experiences
  • Difficulty getting numb, a strong gag reflex or fear of needles
  • TMJ trouble that makes it hard to keep your mouth open
  • No time for multiple visits

Sedation dentistry is only for adult patients, and Dr. Prather screens for any medical reasons you may not be eligible during the consultation.

03 · Laser & technology

Less drill, more comfort.

The DeLight™ Erbium YAG dental laser is a pulsed light that gently removes decay without the need for anesthesia or the drill in most cases. It helps sterilize as it works and is less traumatic to the tooth than the drill.

DIAGNOdent

Laser light helps detect early cavities in the grooves of chewing surfaces, without x-ray exposure.

OralCDx

A computerized brush biopsy that helps detect oral cancer quickly and painlessly at its earliest stage.

Intraoral camera

A tiny fiber-optic wand takes vivid photos inside your mouth, so you can see what we see.

Patient education

Treatment rooms and the waiting room have video systems to explain any procedure you are unsure about.

04 · Cosmetic dentistry

Small changes, big smiles.

Even a subtle change can make a big difference in the way you look and feel. Whitening, bonding, enamel shaping, veneers and white fillings can create a beautiful smile, at times with only minimal changes to your existing teeth.

  • Teeth whitening with custom trays made from impressions of your teeth, using a product proven safe under a dentist’s direction.
  • Bonding with tooth-colored composite to “fill in the blanks”, such as a gap between front teeth.
  • Enamel shaping to smooth rough edges or correct minor crowding and overlap.
  • Porcelain veneers, about 1 mm thick, to whiten, close spaces, straighten or repair chipped and worn teeth.

See real before-and-after results in our smile gallery.

05 · Orthodontics

Straighter teeth, at any age.

Orthodontic treatment starts with an evaluation: Dr. Prather checks whether you are a good candidate, takes impressions, x-rays and measurements, and then meets with you to go over the recommended treatment, its expected length and its cost.

Options include braces with metal or white brackets, arch expanders for younger children (which can minimize or eliminate the need for braces later) and “invisible” clear plastic retainers for adults with mild to moderate crowding. Children should be evaluated by age 6, or sooner if you notice an open bite or are concerned about how their teeth are developing.

06 · Implants & dentures

Replace missing teeth with confidence.

A dental implant is a post placed in the jaw with an artificial tooth or teeth attached. The surgical phase is completed at a specialist’s office, and the restorative phase is completed at our office. Implants can replace a single tooth or bridge, or anchor an implant-retained denture, which also slows the shrinking of bone and gum tissue.

When most or all natural teeth are lost, dentures restore eating and speaking ability and support the lips and cheeks. We offer complete dentures, overdentures, implant-retained dentures, immediate dentures (placed the same day teeth are extracted) and removable partials. Dr. Prather will help you choose the option that is best for you.

07 · TMJ / TMD

Relief for jaw problems.

Popping or clicking in the jaw joint, headaches, earaches, facial pain, a jaw that locks or limited movement can all be signs of TMJ syndrome. An accurate diagnosis comes first: a review of your history, an exam of the teeth, bite and jaw muscles, x-rays of the jaw joints, models of the teeth and, where helpful, a TENS unit to relax the jaw muscles. The results show whether you have TMJ syndrome and how best to treat it.
