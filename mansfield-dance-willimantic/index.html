02 Why dance?

Acro at MAD

Because our kids deserve more than just an activity.

Dance class is more than a way to stay active. It’s a place where children develop life skills that stay with them long after they leave the studio.

In a world where kids spend more time on screens, struggle with social anxiety or feel disconnected from others, dance gives them a way to move, express themselves and feel part of something bigger.

01

Discipline. Confidence. Connection.

Each class teaches more than steps: focus, commitment and the joy of progress. Dancers learn to set goals, bounce back from mistakes and work with their peers. These aren’t just dance lessons, they’re life lessons.

02

A place to belong

Every child is seen, supported and celebrated. Whether your child is shy, high-energy or simply looking for a creative outlet, dance meets them where they are.

03

Movement matters

Dance builds strong, capable bodies and minds. It boosts energy, improves mood and supports healthy development, and gives kids a reason to keep moving with purpose and joy.

08 FAQ

Questions parents ask.

Something else? Email info@mansfielddance.com or call 866-393-2623.

Is my child too old to start dance?

No. They might be a beginner, they might have some catching up to do, they might feel nervous on the first day, but none of that means they’re too late. Older beginners often learn incredibly quickly.

We look at the whole dancer – age, experience, confidence, goals and learning style – and an older beginner isn’t automatically placed with very young children.

How does the New Student Intro Program work?

New students try two weeks for $29.99 before committing to a full program, plus free ballet shoes (for classes requiring them) or a studio t-shirt. We’ll help you find the right class for your dancer.

What ages do you teach?

Ages 1 through adult: Move With Me and Intro to Dance for babies and toddlers, Fun 2 Dance for Pre-K and Kindergarten, Junior Foundations, Fundamentals of Technique, the Performance Program and adult dance & fitness.

What should my dancer bring?

The correct dancewear and shoes for their class (ballet slippers, tap shoes or sneakers for hip hop), hair pulled back securely with a few extra ties and pins in the dance bag, and a leak-proof water bottle. Most important of all: a positive attitude.

Do you have classes for adults?

Yes, at both locations: tap, ballet, contemporary, jazz and Zumba for all experience levels. See the adult schedule.

Do you compete?

We do, but not for trophies alone. Our competition programs are designed to inspire growth, dedication and teamwork, always with a focus on artistry, effort and personal progress.
