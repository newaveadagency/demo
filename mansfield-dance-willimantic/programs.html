Move With Me · Intro to Dance

A joyful first step into the world of dance! Our baby and toddler classes build connection, coordination and confidence through movement, music and fun.

P/02

Fun 2 Dance

Pre-K & Kindergarten

Energetic, age-appropriate classes that mix fun with foundational skills. They introduce young dancers to the structure of a classroom while encouraging self-expression and teamwork.

  • Tap/Ballet · rhythm, movement, early technique
  • Flip Hop · tumbling + hip hop

Along the way, students build not only physical coordination, but also confidence, connection and a love for learning through dance.

Get started
P/03

Junior Foundations

Two-day program

For dancers ready to take the next step in their training. Core classes build a strong foundation in both technique and artistry; optional add-ons bring more variety and challenge.

  • Tap
  • Ballet
  • Jazz
  • Lyrical
  • + Acro (optional)
  • + Hip hop (optional)

Junior Foundations is about more than dance steps. Integrated leadership training, goal setting and team-building help each dancer show responsibility, focus, resilience and collaboration.

Get started
P/04

Fundamentals of Technique

Grow with intention

A focused program for dancers who want to strengthen their technical base and refine their performance across styles, with an emphasis on alignment, strength, flexibility and precision.

  • Ballet
  • Pointe
  • Tap
  • Jazz
  • Lyrical
  • Modern
  • Hip hop
  • Acro

Whether preparing for performance, competition or personal goals, students receive clear guidance, corrections and support from instructors who care about the whole dancer.

Get started
P/05

Performance Program

For the dedicated dancer

Advanced training, expanded performance opportunities and focused mentorship. A curriculum that blends technique, choreography and leadership challenges dancers to excel in artistry and personal growth, on stage and off.

Our pre-teen and teen dancers also train at conventions. After one convention weekend they came home talking about choosing inspiration instead of jealousy, a lesson we keep using in the studio.
