Our shop

Not your average bookstore.

The Maine Bookhouse, formerly Shoestring Books, has been a family-owned and operated bookstore since 2002.

Curious minds will find every imaginable subject in paperback and hardcover at affordable prices, from best-selling fiction and classic literature to collectible children’s books, first editions, signed books and the uncommon. With framed local art, record albums, rare vinyl and select CDs also in stock, the shop is a destination unto itself.

  • We buy, sell and trade books every day
  • Free searches, special orders, free quotes and free appraisals
  • Books shipped anywhere in the world

FAQ

Good to know.

When is the shop open?

Year-round Thursday to Saturday 10 am – 5 pm and Sunday 12 – 4 pm. Other times by chance or appointment, with extended summer hours. Please call ahead at 207-743-9300, especially if you’re traveling from afar.

Do you buy books?

Yes. We buy, sell and trade books, and we offer free quotes and free appraisals. Call or send us a message with what you have.

Can you find a book I’m looking for?

Yes. Book searches are free and special orders are welcome. Use the form above, email books@themainebookhouse.com or call.

Do you ship?

We ship anywhere in the world, and shipping is free within the continental United States.

How can I pay?

Visa, Mastercard, Discover, PayPal, checks, money orders, cash, or trades.
