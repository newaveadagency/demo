FAQ

Good questions.

Who conducts the lessons?

Our lessons are led by skilled instructors who are committed to enhancing your musical journey.

Can beginners enroll in lessons?

Yes. We provide lessons for all levels, from beginners to advanced musicians looking to refine their skills.

Which instruments can I take lessons on?

Guitar, bass, drums, piano, voice, violin and ukulele.

Do you repair instruments?

Absolutely. We provide professional repair services for band instruments and guitars to ensure optimal performance.

What commercial services do you provide?

We specialize in custom audio and video installations for businesses.

Do you offer discounts?

Yes, special discounts for churches, schools and military personnel. Ask at the counter or call (931) 552-1240.
