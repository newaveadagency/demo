Clarksville's place to play.

Mary's Music has served Fort Campbell, Clarksville and the surrounding communities since 1992.

Walk in and you'll find guitars on the walls, drum kits on the floor, band instruments behind the counter and a lesson schedule that runs all week. We carry new and used instruments and pro audio from brands like Fender, Taylor, Ibanez, Roland, Pearl, Shure and JBL, repair band instruments and guitars in the shop, and rent instruments for school band.

Churches, schools and military personnel get special discounts.
