What we do

A full-service animal clinic

From vaccines to yearly checkups, we go to great lengths to make sure your pet is happy, healthy and comfortable.

Wellness exams

Yearly checkups plus puppy, kitten and senior care to keep your pet healthy at every age.

Our team

Friendly faces on Leestown Road

Gary Clark, DVM

Practice owner

After earning his doctorate of veterinary medicine at Virginia Tech (class of 1986), Dr. Clark worked in Virginia and West Virginia, then returned home to his birthplace of Lexington in 1992. After nearly 20 years at other clinics, he bought Animal Clinic Leestown Road in the fall of 2011.

He has built on the existing clientele ever since, creating a place where you’re not just taking your pet to the vet – you’re bringing your pet to visit a family member.

Interests: animals, reading journals, attending lectures, and watching his patio garden fail miserably.

Teresa Renfroe

Questions & answers

What do I do in an emergency after hours?

Call MedVet Lexington at (859) 276-2505, 150 Dennis Dr, Lexington, KY 40503. During our hours, call us first at (859) 225-5678.

Where exactly is the clinic?

1590 Leestown Road, Suite 128. We’re off Leestown Road in the Kroger parking lot, in the corner between H&R Block and Family Dollar.

I’m a new client. Is there a form?

Yes – download the New Client Form, fill it in and bring it along to your first visit.

Do you offer grooming?

Tracy Campanella, a nationally certified master groomer, rents space at the clinic on certain days. She takes small dogs and cats up to 25 lbs and schedules her own appointments at 859-684-5144.

Which payment methods do you accept?

Visa, MasterCard, Discover and American Express. You can also apply for CareCredit.

Can I order my pet’s food and medication online?

Yes. Our online store carries products approved and recommended by us, and you can manage appointments in the pet portal.
