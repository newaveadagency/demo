Our services

Wellness exams

Yearly checkups to catch problems early and keep your pet happy, healthy and comfortable.

Vaccinations

Annual vaccinations for dogs and cats.

Puppy & kitten care

First exams and vaccines for the newest members of your family.

Senior care

Attentive care for older pets as their needs change.

Surgery

All aspects of veterinary surgery in a friendly environment.

Dental care

Dental care to keep your pet’s teeth and gums in good shape.

Radiology (X-rays)

X-rays at the clinic to help us make accurate diagnoses.

Ultrasound

We can arrange to have ultrasounds performed at our location for your convenience.

Parasite prevention & control

Protection against fleas, ticks, worms and other parasites.

Microchipping

A permanent ID that helps lost pets find their way home.

Pain management

Keeping your pet comfortable after surgery, injury or with chronic conditions.

Behavioral medicine

Help with behavior problems as part of your pet’s overall health.

Nutritional counseling

Advice on the right diet for your pet’s age, size and health.

Fully stocked pharmacy

Medications available right here at the clinic.

Hospice & euthanasia services

Compassionate care and support when it’s time to say goodbye.

Grooming

Small dogs and cats up to 25 lbs, with master groomer Tracy Campanella on certain days.
