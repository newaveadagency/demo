The dojo way

Three things we train for.

Technically correct

Details matter: how you bow, how you perform kata, how you stand at attention. Posture, breathing, preparation, form and footwork.

Physically fit

Training combines the mental and physical disciplines that improve strength, flexibility, speed and mental alertness.

Self-disciplined

Zanshin, mental focus. Karate-ka learn to stay focused and in full control of themselves in any situation.

Lineage

Where the training comes from.

Masters named in the dojo's Shotokan and Goju Ryu lineage.

The instructors regularly attend black belt and instructor training with Hanshi R. Johns of Dai Ichi Shotokan Karate-Do in Houston and Goju Ryu training with Hanshi B. Wright of The Society of Harmonious Fist in San Antonio.
