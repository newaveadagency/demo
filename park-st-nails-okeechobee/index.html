04 — Who does your nails

Joe, Randi & Joleen.

Three people, one small salon: a husband, wife and daughter, as our customers like to point out.

01

Joe

Plexinail sets · gel pedicures

Often the voice on the phone. Takes his time on shape and explains the products he's using as he works.

02

Randi

Fills · color · nail design

Regulars come back to her for fills, color and designs, and she'll get as close as possible to the picture you bring.

03

Joleen

Full sets · detailed nail art

Known for patient, detailed full sets. Customers who are hard on their nails say her sets hold up without breaks or lifting.
