02 — Getting in

Three easy steps.

No app, no account. Just a phone call or a walk through the door.

  1. Call or walk in

    Ring (863) 623-2498 to set a time, or stop by Thursday to Sunday during open hours.

  2. Bring your idea

    A photo, a color, a holiday theme. Talk it through at the table before anything is painted.

  3. Come back for a fill

    When your Plexinails grow out, book a fill ($35) instead of a new full set.

03 — FAQ

Good to know.

Short answers to what people ask most. Anything else, just call.

Do you take walk-ins?

Yes. Our Google listing shows walk-ins accepted during open hours (Thursday to Sunday). If you want a set time, call (863) 623-2498 first.

What are your hours?

Thursday, Friday and Saturday 10 AM to 6 PM, Sunday 10 AM to 3 PM. Closed Monday, Tuesday and Wednesday. Hours around holidays can differ, so call ahead.

Where are you now?

At 401 SW 2nd St, Okeechobee, FL 34974. We moved there in 2026 from our previous Park St. location, just around the corner.

What's the difference between a full set and a fill?

A Plexinails full set ($45) is a brand-new set. A fill ($35) is the follow-up visit for a set you already have, once your nails have grown out.

Can I get gel polish on a manicure or pedicure?

Yes. A spa manicure with gel polish is $35 and a spa pedicure with gel polish is $45.

Can I bring a picture of the design I want?

Please do. Nail art is priced from $5 and up depending on the design.

How can I pay?

Our Google listing shows credit and debit cards accepted. Ask when you call if you're unsure.

Is the salon wheelchair accessible?

Yes. The listing shows a wheelchair-accessible entrance and accessible parking, and there is a restroom.
