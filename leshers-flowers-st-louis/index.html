Delivery & FAQ

Good to know before you order

Still have a question about an order or about caring for a plant you received? We're just a call or an email away.

leshersflowers@gmail.com
How late can I order for same-day delivery?

Order by 11:30 AM central time for same-day delivery. The cut-off can change near flower holidays, so call the shop if you are ordering close to a holiday.

Where do you deliver?

We deliver fresh flowers daily to St. Louis, Missouri and the surrounding areas: to homes, hospitals and funeral homes. We can also send flowers across the street or around the world.

Can I order by phone instead of online?

Yes. Call 314-832-3500 or toll free 800-657-6947 and speak with our designers, or order online at any time.

What can I send besides flowers?

Fresh flowers, plants, silks, balloons and stuffed animals are our specialty.

Do you do weddings and events?

Yes, for weddings, events, celebrations and holiday luncheons or dinners. Request a wedding consultation or call for an appointment.
