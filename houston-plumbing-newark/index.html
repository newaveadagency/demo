FAQ

Straight answers.

Do you offer emergency plumbing service?

Yes. Houston offers 365 days/24-hour emergency service. A live person answers during office hours (Monday to Friday, 8:00 am to 5:00 pm). After hours, leave a message and the team will contact you.

Are you licensed and insured?

Yes. Houston Plumbing & Heating Inc. is licensed, bonded and insured, Ohio License #25394, and is an Accredited Business with the Better Business Bureau.

How do I get an estimate?

Request a free estimate by email at office@houstonplumbingheating.com, use the form below, or call 740-763-3961.

Do you offer financing?

Yes. Financing is available through Synchrony Bank, and you can apply online.

Which payment methods do you accept?

Visa, MasterCard, Discover and American Express.

Who checks the technicians that come to my home?

All Houston technicians undergo background checks, drug testing and training before they complete repairs in your home.
