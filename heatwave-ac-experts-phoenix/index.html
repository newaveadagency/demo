FAQ

Questions, answered

Do you offer emergency services?

Yes, we offer 24/7 emergency AC services. In Phoenix, extreme heat can make a broken air conditioner more than an inconvenience; it can be urgent.

Our team responds quickly to emergency calls, diagnoses the issue and works efficiently to restore your system as soon as possible, day or night.

How often is maintenance needed?

We recommend professional maintenance at least once a year, ideally before the peak cooling season. In Phoenix’s extreme heat and dusty environment, some homeowners and businesses choose twice-yearly maintenance (spring and fall).

  • Improves system efficiency
  • Reduces the risk of unexpected breakdowns
  • Extends the lifespan of your AC unit
  • Keeps energy costs lower
  • Ensures consistent cooling during high temperatures
What services do you provide?

A full range of residential and commercial HVAC services in Phoenix and surrounding areas: AC repair, emergency AC services (24/7), AC installation & replacement, maintenance & tune-ups, commercial & light commercial HVAC, tenant improvement (TI) projects, air quality solutions, insulation, ductwork, and thermostat installation & smart upgrades.

Are your technicians certified?

Yes. All our technicians are fully trained, licensed and certified HVAC professionals with the experience to service all major air conditioning systems and brands. We prioritize ongoing training to stay up to date with the latest HVAC technology, safety standards and best practices.

What areas do you serve?

We proudly serve Phoenix, AZ and the surrounding cities and communities, and in general we offer services throughout Arizona. If you’re outside the Phoenix area, contact us. We’ll do our best to accommodate your location or let you know if we can help.

Do you offer a warranty?

Yes. We offer warranties on parts and labor, depending on the type of service. For installations and replacements, manufacturer warranties apply to the equipment, and most of our manufacturers offer 10-year or longer parts warranties.

We also provide a 2-year workmanship warranty on our installation services, with options to extend coverage. For repairs, we warranty the parts and labor used.
