03

AC maintenance & tune-ups

Regular maintenance is essential to keep your system running efficiently in extreme desert temperatures. We recommend a tune-up at least once a year, ideally in spring before temperatures rise. For older systems or heavy usage, twice a year gives even better performance and reliability.

Regular maintenance helps lower energy bills, prevent costly breakdowns and keep your home consistently cool.

What a tune-up covers

  • Complete system inspection
  • Seasonal tune-ups (spring & summer)
  • Air filter replacement
  • Thermostat calibration & testing
  • Refrigerant level check
  • Coil cleaning (evaporator & condenser)
  • Condensate drain line cleaning
  • Electrical component inspection
  • Capacitor & contactor testing
  • Blower motor inspection & lubrication
  • Airflow testing & adjustment
  • Ductwork inspection for leaks
  • System performance testing
  • Safety check & system diagnostics
  • Preventative maintenance plans
