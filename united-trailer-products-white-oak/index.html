Questions

Good to know before you hitch up.

What kinds of trailers do you repair?

All sizes: utility, boat, enclosed and gooseneck trailers. We specialize in oil field, manufacturing, lawn care, vehicle transport, recreational and utility trailer repair.

What repairs do you handle?

Structural repairs, axles, suspension, couplers, jacks, ramp gates, lights and brake replacement, plus refloor and repaint. We also do planned maintenance on hubs and brakes (electric and hydraulic).

Can you get my trailer ready for its annual inspection?

Yes. Preparation for annual trailer inspections is part of our planned maintenance work.

Do you sell trailer parts?

Yes. We stock standard parts for utility, boat and gooseneck trailers: couplers, brakes, axles and suspensions, hubs, bearings, seals, fenders, jacks, lights, steel, lumber, hitches, wheels and tires.

Do you refill propane?

Yes, cylinders of any kind (BBQ, camper, forklift). We also inspect and re-certify cylinders and can help with valve replacement and parts.

How do I set up a repair?

Call 903.759.1851, email jrichardson@utptx.com or use the repair request form. Be as detailed as possible and tell us about anything specific the trailer will haul.
