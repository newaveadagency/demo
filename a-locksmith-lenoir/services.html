02 · Safe moving

Let us help you with your safe move.

A large safe on an upper floor? A safe that won’t fit through the door of the room it’s in? Moving a safe can be a tricky and dangerous affair, due to its size, weight and unwieldy nature. We’ll gladly provide a quote, help you preserve your investment in a quality safe, and keep everyone involved in the move safe.
