FAQ

Good to know before you call.

What do I do if I’m locked out?

If you have a lockout emergency, call us directly at (828) 754-1183, any time.

Can you open a safe other locksmiths couldn’t?

Shane is a Certified Master Safecracker (NSO). We regularly open Adesco, AmSec, Bischoff, Browning, Diebold, Fichet, Mosler, NCR, Sargent and Greenleaf, Sentry Safe… and anything else with a lock, mechanical or electronic.

Can I visit your showroom?

Yes. The general public is welcome at our showroom during normal business hours to discuss or purchase a safe. Because of the unpredictable nature of our business we may be out assisting a customer, so please call to schedule an appointment in advance.

What area do you serve?

Locksmithing: Caldwell and all adjacent counties (Alexander, Avery, Burke, Catawba, Watauga and Wilkes), from our home office between Lenoir and Gamewell. Safe and vault services: North Carolina, South Carolina, Tennessee, Kentucky, West Virginia and Virginia.

Do you deliver and install safes?

Yes. We hand deliver and install your safe to make sure it’s effective at safeguarding your most valuable possessions. We also quote moves of existing safes and vault doors.
