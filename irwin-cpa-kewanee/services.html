A

Tax services

Returns, IRS matters and information reporting.

A1

Income tax preparation

Preparation of income tax returns for individuals and businesses in our area, prepared from your tax documents and prior-year return.

A2

Offers in compromise & negotiating back taxes

When a tax balance can’t be paid in full, the IRS has formal options such as an offer in compromise, which settles a liability for less than the full amount when the taxpayer qualifies. We prepare the paperwork and negotiate back taxes with the IRS on your behalf.

A3

IRS representation

An IRS letter or examination is easier to deal with when a CPA is involved. We represent clients before the IRS.

A4

Preparation of 1099s

Businesses that pay contractors and other non-employees generally must issue 1099 forms each year. We prepare 1099s for our clients.

B

Audit services

Assurance and attestation work for organizations of many kinds.

B1

Audits

The most thorough level of assurance: we test the underlying records and issue an opinion on whether the financial statements are presented fairly, in all material respects.

B2

Reviews

Analytical procedures and inquiries that give limited assurance on your financial statements, often enough for lenders and boards that don’t require a full audit.

B3

Compilations

We present your financial information in the form of financial statements, without expressing assurance, which is useful for management and some lenders.

B4

Agreed-upon procedures

Specific procedures that you and other parties agree on in advance. We perform them and report the findings, without giving an overall opinion.

For

Corporate, governmental, pension plan & not-for-profit organizations

Our audit services cover a range of organizations, from corporations to local government bodies, employee pension plans and not-for-profits.

B+ A plain-English guide

Audit, review or compilation?

Three levels of service on financial statements. Which one you need usually depends on who asks for the statements, such as a bank, a board or a grantor.

ServiceLevel of assuranceWhat the CPA doesOften used for
CompilationNone
Presents your financial information as financial statements, without testing it.Internal management use; some smaller loans.
ReviewLimited
Analytical procedures and inquiries of management.Lenders and boards that need more than a compilation.
AuditReasonable
Tests records and transactions, confirms balances, considers internal control, and issues an opinion.Larger loans, governmental and not-for-profit requirements, pension plans.

General information only. Requirements differ by lender, agency and organization; call the office to talk through what applies to you.
