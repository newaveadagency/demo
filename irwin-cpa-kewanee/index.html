05 Questions

Frequently asked questions

01Which areas do you serve?

Our office is at 222 N Main St in Kewanee, and we serve clients mainly in Henry, Bureau, Knox and Stark counties.

02What is the difference between an audit, a review and a compilation?

A compilation presents your financial statements in proper form without providing assurance. A review adds analytical procedures and inquiries and gives limited assurance. An audit is the most thorough: it tests the underlying records and gives reasonable assurance that the statements are free of material misstatement.

03I owe back taxes to the IRS. Can you help?

Yes. We negotiate back taxes with the IRS, prepare offers in compromise and represent clients before the IRS. Call 309-852-5515 to talk through your situation.

04Do you work with not-for-profits and local governments?

Yes. Our audit services cover corporate, governmental, pension plan and not-for-profit organizations.

05Can you help with QuickBooks?

Yes. QuickBooks support is part of our financial services, alongside bookkeeping, payroll and business set-up and planning.

06What should I bring to a tax appointment?

Usually your prior-year return, all W-2 and 1099 forms, records of deductible expenses and any letters you received from the IRS or the Illinois Department of Revenue. If you are unsure, call the office before your visit.
