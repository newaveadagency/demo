Dance classes

From first steps to pointe shoes.

Year-long classes meet once a week for 45 or 60 minutes; seasonal sessions run 4 to 10 weeks. Levels: Mini Stars (7–9), Starlettes (9–11), Stars, Juniors and Seniors.

Ages 2–3 · with a parent

Twinkle Tots

Creative movement: waiting turns in line, basic stretching, songs and stories set to movement, ribbon play and first ballet steps. A seasonal session; no recital.

Ages 3–6

Ballet & Tap Combo

An introduction to both ballet and tap, with age-appropriate stretching and basic steps. Needs both pairs of shoes; one recital dance.

Ages 7–9 · hour-long

Ballet & Jazz

Builds ballet steps into combinations and introduces jazz technique. Performs both a ballet and a jazz dance in the recital.

Ages 10+

Ballet

Builds technique and introduces barre work, with choreography for a recital performance.

Ages 10+

Classical Ballet

A foundational technique class: full barre, center work, across-the-floor progressions, petite and grand allegro.

Ages 7+

Tap

Rhythms and combinations built on the basics, plus choreography for the recital.

Ages 10+

Jazz

Jazz technique practice and choreography for a recital performance.

All levels

Hip Hop

Street dance styles including funk, popping, locking and very basic break dancing, starting with a warm-up and moving across the floor.

By teacher invitation

Footwork / Pointe

Chosen by the ballet teacher once a dancer shows interest and physical readiness. Students stay in ballet class; pointe shoes only under the teacher’s guidance.

Intermediate ballet

Modern / Lyrical

A continuation of ballet with more free expression and improvisation. Students must also be enrolled in Ballet & Jazz.

Ages 10+

Choreography / Improv

Builds artistry and individual voice: choreographic structure, improvisation, musicality and storytelling.

Ballet & Jazz dancers 10+

Leaps, Turns & Jumps

Pure technique, offered as a summer session and as a pop-up class during the year. No recital dance.

Adults · all levels

Adult Dance

Grew up dancing and want to get back into it, or never had the chance? Now is your time. 10-week sessions, no registration fee, no recital.

Ages 4–13

Sunshine Stars

Tailored for students with Down Syndrome, Autism and other developmental disabilities, building balance, muscle control, confidence and friendships.

Fitness · adults 18+

Becoming healthy is a lifestyle change.

The first step is finding the right place to start. Fitness runs in 10-week sessions through the school year; shorter summer sessions may be offered. Bring a mat and water.

Total Body Tone

Exercises that work several muscle groups at once with varied equipment, a short intense cardio section halfway, and a relaxing cool-down.

Mat Pilates

Joseph Pilates’ “Contrology”: spinal and pelvic alignment, breath and a strong, efficient core for flexibility, strength, coordination and balance.

Barre

Inspired by ballet and Pilates: body weight, weights, exercise balls, stretch bands and the ballet barre, to improve posture while strengthening and defining muscles.

Studio policies

The small print, made simple.

Dress code & shoes

Any color or style leotard with tights and the right dance shoes. Hair pulled back, no baggy or loose clothing (hip hop included). Ballet skirts or spandex dance shorts may go over leotards. Sneaker classes need a clean pair used indoors only, and dance shoes should never be worn outside. Adult classes may wear leggings, t-shirts or athletic wear. Samples for sizing and gently used dance wear are available in the studio.

Drop-off

Always enter through the front door and walk your child down to the locker room; don’t leave them there alone. Please exit through the back door. If you’re late, walk your child to the studio room so the teacher sees them. The back door stays locked: never unlock it or let anyone in as you leave.

Pick-up

Pick-up is in the downstairs locker room. Wait in the hallway past the locker room so the staircase stays clear. Students are dismissed only when we see a parent. Only Seniors, Juniors and Stars are dismissed to the parking lot.

In the studio

No eating, drinking or gum during class; only water, kept in the locker room. No outside shoes on the dance floors. Students may wait in the locker room between classes with Miss Janelle’s approval. If your child will be late or absent, please email the studio.

Snow days

4 built-in snow days per dance year, with makeup sessions after that. Classes cancelled in a seasonal session are added to the end. Check email, social media and NBC Connecticut; we do not follow school system cancellations.

Announcements

Reminders, cancellations and the monthly newsletter go out by email through Mailchimp, and are posted on social media and in the studio.
