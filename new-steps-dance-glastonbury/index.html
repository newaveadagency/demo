Our story

Three directors. One vision.

Through the years the director may have changed, but the original vision Miss Tammy created has stayed the same: a comfortable, fun place to learn, grow and express yourself through dance.

1984

Miss Tammy Carlson founds New Steps Dance Center, with locations in East Hartford and Enfield.

1988

Her younger sister, Miss Tracy Lombardo, begins teaching, choreographing and directing, and later takes ownership.

2004

Miss Tracy moves the studio to Glastonbury.

2016

The fitness program is added under the direction and ownership of Janelle Bradway.

2021

In the spring, Miss Janelle moves New Steps to a stand-alone building at 31 Concord Street in the town center.

Sunshine Stars

Every child is a ray of sunshine, in their own unique, beautiful way.

Sunshine Stars is a class specifically tailored for students with Down Syndrome, Autism and other developmental disabilities. Through simple patterns, sequencing and repetition, students learn the basics of movement, rhythm and dance in Ballet, Jazz, Tap and Hip Hop, with creative movement, storytelling and hands-on sensory activities.

It is made possible by Miss Lauren and Miss Meghan, volunteers with experience working with children with special needs, and current New Steps dancers who demonstrate in class. The program was featured in Channel 8’s CT STYLE “Living Local” segment.

  • Confidence
  • Socialization

FAQ

Good to know before the first class.

More on tuition, dress code and drop-off on the classes & pricing page.

How long have you been in business?

New Steps was founded in 1984 by Tammy Carlson, with locations in East Hartford and Enfield. Her younger sister, Miss Tracy Lombardo, took ownership years later and moved the studio to Glastonbury in 2004. Today it is owned and run by Miss Janelle, who started at New Steps as a young dancer.

What do you have for adults?

Tap, ballet and modern dance classes, plus barre, Pilates and Total Body Tone fitness classes. No experience is required for adult dance classes, and adults don’t need to follow the dress code.

Does my child have to be in the recital?

No. There is one recital a year (typically about 2½ hours) and it is not mandatory. If your child takes part, the dress rehearsal is mandatory. Twinkle Tots, Adult Dance and Sunshine Stars do not participate.

Do you do competition?

No, not of any kind. We believe dance is a way to express oneself; you dance because you love to dance.

My child has never danced. Is there a beginner class?

Yes, there are classes for all levels. Placement is based on experience and ability as well as age, so please email the studio about class placement before registering.

Do I have to pay in full?

No. The dance year is split into two semesters, each with two payments. 50% of the first semester is due at registration and the remaining balance in October.

What happens when it snows?

There are 4 built-in snow days in the dance year; makeup sessions are scheduled after that. Check email, our social media and NBC Connecticut. We do not follow any school system cancellations.
