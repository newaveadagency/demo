Good to know

Questions about wedding flowers

What's included in the $999 package?

One bride's bouquet of hand-tied stems finished with a satin wrap, two attendant's bouquets, two mother's corsages or hand-held bouquets, two father's boutonnieres, a groom's boutonniere, two groomsmen's boutonnieres and seven centerpieces of moderately priced, seasonal flowers.

What's the difference with the $1,499 package?

It adds a third attendant's bouquet and a third groomsman's boutonniere, the bride's bouquet can include roses, calla lilies and hydrangea, and you get ten centerpieces of coordinating flowers. Elevated centerpieces are not included.

Can I add extras like a chuppah or cake flowers?

Yes. On top of a standard package you can add veil or hair floral decor, a chuppah, an aisle runner and florals, elevated centerpieces, candles and seasonal lighting, card table flowers and cake floral décor.

Do you only do weddings?

No. We also design sympathy and funeral flowers, corporate events and services, seasonal plantings, birthdays, anniversaries and other special occasions.

How do I order flowers for delivery today?

Browse arrangements and order online at mugfords.com, or call (508) 836-5554.
