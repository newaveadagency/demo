Welcome to the Palomino

An old-school Route 66 stop, kept simple.

The Palomino is a single-story motor court on East Route 66 Boulevard: you pull in, park by your door and sit out front in the evening while the traffic and the neon go by.

Rooms are no-frills and affordable, with comfortable beds, air conditioning and Wi-Fi that works. Gas stations, restaurants and parks are within walking distance, and the famous Del's Restaurant is directly across the street.

After dark

When the sun goes down, the Palomino lights up.

The vintage neon sign, with its white horse, yellow boomerang and red Motel panel, lights up after dark and changes colors. It's part of what gives Tucumcari's stretch of Route 66 its glow.

"The vintage neon sign lights up and changes colors. It adds character to Tucumcari."Gary S., Google review

How do I book a room?

Call the front office at (575) 461-3622 for rates and availability, or send your dates with the form below and then give us a call.

Can I bring my dog?

The Palomino is a pet-friendly motel. Mention your pet when you call so the office can give you a suitable room.

Is there Wi-Fi and parking?

Yes, both are free, and you park right outside your room.

What's nearby?
