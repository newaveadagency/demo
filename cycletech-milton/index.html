FAQ

Before you bring it in

Which motorcycles do you work on?

CycleTech services most makes, models and years. Mike is factory trained in Honda, Harley-Davidson, Suzuki and Yamaha.

Do you work on carburetors?

Yes. We service carburetors.

Do you sell tires?

Yes. CycleTech carries a large selection of tires with competitive pricing. Call 608-868-5445 for your size and availability.

Do I need an appointment?

Appointments shorten your cycle's down time, so call ahead when you can. Saturdays are by appointment only. You are also welcome to stop by during normal business hours.
