06 Questions

Good to know.

Q1Which areas do you serve?

Ashton, Island Park, St. Anthony, Rexburg, Rigby, Driggs, Victor and everywhere in between. We're also licensed to serve West Yellowstone, Montana, Alta, Wyoming and the surrounding areas.

Q2How does a cabin winterization work?

We hook an air compressor to your water lines to blow out the whole house, then pour −50° RV antifreeze into all toilets and drains. That prevents freezing and burst lines, so you can turn off heat and power to cut costs. Leave a key with us and we can winterize after you leave, then open the place again before your summer stay.

Q3Can I buy parts and fixtures for my own project?

Yes. Our store at 421 Main Street in Ashton stocks parts and fixtures from Moen, Kohler, Price Pfister, Delta, Gerber and more. If we don't have it, we can order it quickly.

Q4Do you service oil furnaces?

Yes. We repair and, if needed, replace oil furnaces. Oil furnaces need their filters and nozzles replaced every year, so call us for your yearly furnace service.
