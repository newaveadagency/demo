05 Cabin winterizations & openings

Close it in the fall. Open it in June.

For second homes and rentals that don't need to run year round. No frozen fixtures, no burst lines, and you can turn off the heat and power to cut costs.

Step 01

Blow out the lines

We hook an air compressor to your water lines and blow out the whole house.

Step 02

RV antifreeze

−50° RV antifreeze goes into all toilets and drains in the house.

Step 03

Power down

Turn off heat and power to the house without worrying about damage.

Step 04

Leave a key

Leave the key with us and we can winterize after you leave.

Step 05

Opening day

We turn the water on and check for issues before you arrive for your summer stay.

06

  • Oil furnace repairs
