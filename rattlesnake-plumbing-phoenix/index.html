Questions & answers

Still not sure? Call (623) 707-3869.

Are you licensed and insured?

Yes. Rattlesnake Plumbing LLC is licensed, bonded and insured for residential and commercial plumbing, Arizona ROC #365896.

Do you offer emergency plumbing?

Yes — 24-hour emergency service. For an active leak or backed-up drain, call (623) 707-3869 rather than sending an online request. Availability and arrival time depend on your location and the current schedule.

Do you give free estimates?

We offer upfront pricing and free estimates for many services. Contact us for details on your job.

Which areas do you serve?

We’re based in Tonopah and serve the Phoenix metro and West Valley, including Phoenix, Surprise, Buckeye, Goodyear, Peoria and Glendale. These are service areas, not branch offices — call to confirm availability for your address.

Does an online request guarantee same-day service?

No. Online requests are confirmed by our team. For anything urgent, call to check the schedule.
