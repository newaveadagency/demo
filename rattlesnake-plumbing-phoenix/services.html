01 · 24-hour emergency service

Emergency plumbing

An active leak or backed-up drain needs a conversation, not a long online form. Call (623) 707-3869 and tell us what’s happening and where the property is so we can confirm availability and the next step.

We handle repair requests involving leaking water lines, overflowing fixtures, drain backups and water-heater problems. A single slow sink and several fixtures backing up at once can need different work — describe every affected fixture when you call.

Have this ready when you call

  • Your address, city and a callback number
  • Whether water is still leaking or a fixture is overflowing
  • Which sinks, toilets, showers or water lines are affected
  • Access instructions and whether anyone can meet the plumber

Emergency availability and arrival time depend on your location and the current schedule. No immediate arrival time is promised online.

02 · Tank & tankless

Water heaters

No hot water, a leaking tank or plans for a replacement? We service tank and tankless water heaters for homeowners and businesses — repair, replacement and maintenance.

Tell us whether the unit uses gas or electricity, whether hot water is completely gone, and whether you can see a leak. A photo of the unit and its model label helps us prepare. Tankless isn’t automatically a direct swap for a tank unit — the requirements are evaluated for your property.

  • Tank and tankless service
  • Replacement and new installation planning
  • Drain-and-flush maintenance
  • Inspection of existing connections and installation

03 · Drains & camera inspections

Drain cleaning

A slow kitchen sink, a blocked toilet and a whole-house backup call for different questions. We handle drain cleaning and camera-inspection requests for homes and commercial properties.

Drain cleaning restores flow through the affected line. If a blockage keeps coming back, a camera inspection may help find a condition that cleaning alone can’t fix. We discuss findings and the next step with you before agreeing on more work.

  • Kitchen, shower and bathroom drain blockages
  • Toilet clogs and recurring backups
  • Drain and sewer camera inspections
  • Follow-up repair planning when a pipe section needs attention

04 · Whole-home & underground

Repiping & water lines

Repeated leaks, a remodel or a planned replacement are good reasons to look at your piping as a system. We take on whole-home repiping, water-line replacement and underground water-line work.

A repipe estimate needs more than the number of bathrooms: we review the fixtures served, existing pipe, routing and access. For underground lines, the route, distance and surfaces above it matter — the estimate should spell out pipe material, excavation, backfill and any restoration included or excluded.

  • Whole-home hot and cold water repiping
  • Water-line repair and replacement
  • Underground line routing and replacement estimates
  • Sewer line replacement

05 · For owners & contractors

Commercial & new construction

We work with business owners and contractors on commercial plumbing, remodels and new-construction projects in the Phoenix metro and West Valley.

For a construction bid, include the plumbing plans, fixture schedule, project address, bid deadline and expected schedule. If the plans are too large for a form, email rattlesnakes97@gmail.com to arrange delivery.

  • Commercial repair and replacement
  • Underground water and drain work
  • Water, drain and vent rough-ins
  • Remodel and new-construction fixture installation
