Fishing locations · southeast Wyoming & northern Colorado

Where to fishfrom the Fly Store.

Lakes, rivers and creeks the crew knows well, from the Laramie Plains Lakes just down Highway 230 to the North Platte and the Snowy Range. Call the store for today's conditions before you head out.

Laramie Plains Lakes

The easiest access is 8.5 miles from the Fly Store, down Highway 230 at Pahlow Lane. Please be mindful of your speed on the access roads to the lakes.

About 2,500 acres

Lake Hattie

The largest of the Laramie Plains Lakes; its size fluctuates with how much water the year brings. Rainbows, browns, cutthroat, kokanee and perch, and it is stocked with other strains from time to time, including lake trout.

About 250 acres

Twin Buttes

The second largest of the Plains Lakes, known to produce amazing brown trout. Rainbows and cutthroat as well.

Just under 120 acres

Meeboer

A great trout fishery that has wintered over well and holds a high fish count. Fish mostly run 14″ to 22″.

34 acres

Gelatt

The smallest of the main Plains Lakes. Grayling were stocked a few years back and are showing good size, along with the trout.

North Platte River

Blue-ribbon water, tailwaters and float stretches. Check current flows before you go.

Kortes Dam to Pathfinder

Miracle Mile

One of the many blue-ribbon access points on the North Platte. A short section full of fish, which is why anglers from all over flock here.

Kortes Dam flows

Tailwater · open year round

Grey Reef

Below Alcova Reservoir off Grey Reef Dam. Winter flows around 500 CFS, runoff and flush flows up to 6,000–7,000 CFS. Known for big browns and rainbows; some bank access, but many stretches are only fishable from a boat.

Routt, CO to Seminoe Reservoir

Upper North Platte

Great wade and float fishing. The most popular sections are Pickaroon and Six Mile Gap; at Pickaroon, Douglas Creek joins the river and brook trout can move in from the creek.

From Colorado into Wyoming

Encampment River

Usually clear and cool except in spring runoff. Browns (some close to 18 inches) in the lower river, more rainbows and the odd brook trout up north. Famous for its late-spring, early-summer green drake hatch.

Snowy Range

High country west of Laramie in the Medicine Bow National Forest.

500 acres · 9,000 ft

Rob Roy Reservoir

National Forest campground, boat ramp and day-use area. Stocked with brown, rainbow and brook trout as well as kokanee salmon. Don't forget Douglas Creek, which flows in and out of Rob Roy.

Rob Roy to the North Platte

Douglas Creek

Flows from the middle of the range into Rob Roy, then through the Platte River Wilderness to the North Platte near Pickaroon. Easy access below Rob Roy on Forest Service Rd 543 and along the Douglas Creek Trail.

Other area lakes

Worth the drive when the wind lets you.

Up to ~4,700 acres when full

Wheatland Reservoir #3

North of Laramie, east of Rock River along Fetterman Rd. Renowned for large trout and large walleye, but big and short on structure. Expect brutal winds and roads that can turn treacherous in winter and spring.

2 miles north of Saratoga

Saratoga Lake

Along HWY 130/230. Fishing, boating and recreation, many trout species, efficient ice fishing and an annual ice fishing derby.

Curt Gowdy State Park

Granite Reservoir

24 miles from both Laramie and Cheyenne off Highway 210 (Happy Jack Road). Excellent rainbow trout and kokanee fishing, camping and water sports.

Northern Colorado · Walden area

Check each lake's specific fly and lure regulations before you fish.

About 20 min west of Walden

Delaney Buttes

A State Wildlife Area with East (68 acres), South (160 acres) and North (160 acres) Delaney Butte lakes. “Gold medal” fishing for brown, cutbow and rainbow trout.

565 acres

Lake John

About 30 minutes northwest of Walden. A state wildlife area with quality fishing for rainbow and Snake River cutthroat trout.

On the ice? Conditions change fast as the season turns. Use extreme caution and call the store for the latest before you go out.
