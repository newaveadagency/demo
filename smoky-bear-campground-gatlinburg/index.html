Welcome to Smoky Bear

There is always one of us on the premises.

Smoky Bear Campground is privately owned by Barry and Chong O’Leary and managed by Patrick O’Leary and Klaire Todd. We are owner operated, and our top priority is you: we do everything we can to make your stay the most enjoyable camping experience you have ever had.

Camping is about taking a break from the stresses of the real world and enjoying the simple, outdoor life. The experiences you have camping make memories that last a lifetime.

Good to know

Before you pack the car.

Full rules, deposits and the cancellation policy are on the rates & policies page.

When is the campground open?

We are closed in December, January and February. The office and camp store are open 9:00 am – 5:00 pm. The pool and hot tub are seasonal, from mid-May until mid-September.

What are check-in and check-out times?

Campsites and tents: check in after 1:00 pm, check out before 12:00 pm. Cabins: check in after 2:00 pm, check out before 11:00 am. Everyone registers at the office before going to their site.

Can I bring my dog?

Yes – quiet, non-aggressive pets on a leash no longer than 6 feet are welcome on RV sites, and owners must pick up after them. No pets on tent sites. In cabins we allow small, house-trained, non-shedding pets up to 15 lbs for $10 per pet per day, except in Baby Bear and Papa Bear.

Do the cabins have linens and towels?

No – please bring your own bed and bath linens. Every cabin starts with dish soap, paper towels, toilet paper and garbage bags (not replenished during your stay). No smoking in the cabins.

How do I reserve?

Call 865-436-8372 from 9 am to 5 pm, or book online any time. A deposit equal to at least your first night is charged to your card when you reserve, and you’ll get a confirmation email from the campground.

Should I use my GPS to get there?

Don’t rely on it – signals are intermittent in the Smokies, and GPS may not account for traffic or the size of your rig. Use the directions below, or call us for a scenic route.

Directions for big rigs

Skip the Parkway traffic.

These directions avoid Pigeon Forge and Gatlinburg traffic and are appropriate for big rigs. Smoky Bear Campground is on the right.

  1. From the North, Northwest, West or South (via Knoxville on I-40 East): take Exit 435 and follow Hwy 321 South for 17 miles.
  2. From the North East (I-81 South): take exit 1A (I-40 East – Asheville), then I-40 Exit 435 and follow Hwy 321 South for 17 miles.
  3. From the East, South East or South (via Asheville on I-40 West): take exit 440 and follow Hwy 321 South for 14 miles.

Make sure to take the turns to stay on Hwy 321. Questions, or prefer a scenic route? Call 865-436-8372.
