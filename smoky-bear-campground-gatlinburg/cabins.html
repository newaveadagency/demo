Six bears, six cabins.

Every cabin has air conditioning and heat, an outdoor charcoal grill, a fire ring and a picnic table – and full access to the pool, hot tub, bathhouse and clubhouse.

Cabin information

Don’t forget your linens.

Linens and towels are not provided – please bring your own bed and bath linens. All cabins will have initial dish soap, paper towels, toilet paper and garbage bags; these items are not replenished during your stay. And please, no smoking!
