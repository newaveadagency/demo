The fine print

Policies & rules

Smoky Bear Campground reserves the right of eviction of any guest without refund for non-compliance of policies, rules or regulations. All rates, discounts and policies are subject to change without notice.

Reservations & deposit

Call 865-436-8372 from 9 am to 5 pm, or request a reservation online any time. To guarantee your site, a deposit equal to at least your first night is charged to your credit card when you reserve; you’ll receive a confirmation email. Reserve for the entire length of your stay – renewal of your site can’t be guaranteed. If you arrive with more people than on your reservation, please let us know. All guests register at the office before going to their site.

Cancellation policy

To cancel, please call 865-436-8372 before the deadlines below to receive a refund of your deposit, minus a cancellation fee. Email or text cancellations are not accepted.

  • RV & tent sites: 10 days’ notice, $10 fee.
  • Cabins: 20 days’ notice, $20 fee.

Reservations made after these deadlines require non-refundable deposits, and cancellations after the deadlines forfeit all deposits. No refunds for early departure or no-shows, and – since we are not responsible for the discomforts of nature – no refunds or credits for weather.

Payment

We accept cash, Visa, MasterCard, Discover, American Express and debit cards. Personal checks are not accepted.

Campfires, trash & fireworks

Campfires only in the fire rings, with adult supervision – please put your fire out before you turn in. Garbage is picked up at your site daily until 11:00 am; just leave the bag at the end of your site, and don’t leave trash out overnight. No fireworks in the campground.

Pets

Clean up after your pets. Only quiet, non-aggressive pets; they must stay on a leash no longer than 6 feet and may not leave your site unsupervised. Pets that make noise or create a nuisance must go to a local kennel at the owner’s expense. No pets in public buildings, pool areas or tent sites. Cabins: $10 per pet per day, 15 lbs max, non-shedding, house-trained – not in Baby or Papa Bear.

Pool, hot tub & bathhouse

The swimming pool and hot tub are open from mid-May until mid-September, weather permitting. The bathhouse and laundry room are open 24 hours during the season. Items left behind after check-out are not our responsibility.
