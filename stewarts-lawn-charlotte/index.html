Questions, answered

Anything else? Just call (704) 913-8091.

Do you offer free consultations?

Yes. We offer free consultations for our services and give you an actual quote. Call (704) 913-8091 or send a request through the form on this page.

What is included in an annual maintenance program?

Weekly mowing, edging and blowing, fertilization, herbicide and fungicide applications for turf, trees and shrubs, spring and fall annuals, fall aeration, overseeding, fertilization and lime, leaf and storm debris removal, snow and ice removal, pruning, spring and fall cleanups, mulching and pine straw.

Will you come on the same day each week?

Yes. We schedule regular appointments to visit your property on the same day and time of the week.

When should I aerate and overseed my lawn?

In the fall. With cooling temperatures and adequate rainfall it is time to aerate, overseed, fertilize and lime cool season turf that is burnt out from the summer heat.

Do you work on commercial properties?

Yes. We maintain and install landscapes for HOAs, home builders and general contractors, restaurants and retail, industrial and government properties, and city and urban spaces around the Charlotte metro.

Are you licensed and insured?

Stewart’s Lawn and Landscaping is insured and bonded, and holds a licensed pesticide applicator credential. References are available on request.
