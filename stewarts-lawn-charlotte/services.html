Fall turf care

Core aeration, overseeding & fertilization

With cooling temperatures and adequate rainfall, fall is the time to aerate, overseed, fertilize and lime cool season turf that is burnt out from the summer heat. Aerating and overseeding is recognized by turf experts as the best treatment to control thatch, reduce compacted areas and fill in bare spots.

Key benefits

  • Fills in turf damaged by summer stress, disease or insects
  • Thickens thin lawns
  • Decreases thatch buildup
  • Relieves soil compaction
  • Improves overall health and appearance
