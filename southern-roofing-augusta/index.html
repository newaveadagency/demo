Our story

Here through the good times and the bad.

Southern Roofing and Insulating Company has been the go-to roofing company in the CSRA since 1932. Unlike many roofing companies that come and go, we have stuck to our roots in the roofing industry through the good and the bad times.

As a local, independent and family-owned small business, we depend on our good word and on making each customer a satisfied one. Through referrals and repeat business, we have grown from a pipe dream to a thriving company. Our crews and craftsmen are trained in asphalt shingles, copper, sheet metal, clay tile, concrete tile, TPO, EPDM and flat roofing.

Questions we hear

Do you give free estimates?

Yes. Call (706) 724-3535 or send the estimate form below and we will be in touch to set up a no-hassle free estimate for your project.

Is financing available?

Yes, financing is available. Ask about it when you call or when we come out for your estimate.

Do you handle emergencies?

Yes. We offer 24/7 emergency services. Call (706) 724-3535.

Which shingle brands do you install?

We carry and install shingles from IKO, Owens Corning, Atlas and GAF.

Do you take care of permits and clean-up?

Yes. We handle the whole project for you: site inspection, material procurement and delivery, permits when necessary, site preparation, installation and clean-up.
