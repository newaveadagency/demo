Violin

Part of the string program since about 1990. Classical technique sits next to the region's tradition of fiddle tunes and playing by ear. In 2017 the evening recital featured the violin students.

Guitar

Guitar was added to the curriculum in 2001, taught with the same grounding in technique and theory as every other instrument.

Since 2001

Flute

Flute is one of the instruments taught at the studio. Call to ask about current lesson times.

Celtic harp

The lever harp is Clara Atkins-Pope's own instrument, which she brought into her teaching around 1990. Supported by the Kentucky Arts Council, she has composed, performed and recorded original works for it.

Since about 1990

Cello (added in 2006), viola and other folk instruments have also been taught at the studio. Call to ask what is available this season.

How we teach

Classical roots, mountain ears

  • Solid traditional classical technique and theory on every instrument.
  • Real respect for playing by ear, improvisation and the folk music of Appalachia, such as traditional fiddle and Celtic tunes.
  • One standard suggested repertoire of method books, so students can move from one teacher to another without losing ground.
  • Memorized performance: awards for the measures you memorize, and recitals to play them in.
