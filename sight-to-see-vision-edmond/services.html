Facilities & equipment

Technology that helps us see more

Optomap ultra-wide digital retinal imaging

A panoramic view of your retina

The retina is the lining at the back of your eye. It receives light through the lens and produces a picture that is sent to the brain. An unhealthy retina can lead to impaired vision or blindness.

Optomap, one image80%+ of the retina
Traditional view, at a time10–12%

The larger view helps our doctors detect the earliest signs of disease and gives them more time to review your images. It is fast, painless and does not typically require dilation. You can find more information and choose an Optomap in the patient portal.

TearLab

An objective number for dry eye

TearLab gives an osmolarity number: a measurement of the salt content of your tears. The test is fast and easy and needs only 50 nanoliters of tears, a sample smaller than the period at the end of this sentence.

Our doctors test both eyes and use the result to determine whether you have dry eye disease and its category: mild, moderate or severe. That number guides the treatment needed to bring your ocular surface and tear film back to good health.

Vision care plans

Such as VSP, Vision Care Direct and EyeMed. They cover routine vision exams, eyeglasses and contact lenses, plus a basic screening for eye disease. They do not cover diagnosis, management or treatment of eye diseases.

Medical insurance

Such as Blue Cross/Blue Shield and Medicare. It must be used if you have an eye health problem, or a systemic health problem with eye complications. If you have both kinds of coverage, we may bill some services to each plan and use coordination of benefits to keep your out-of-pocket cost as low as possible.

Medicare: what it covers and what it doesn’t

Medicare covers the medical portion of the eye exam when there is a medical complaint, such as cataracts. Without a medical complaint or diagnosis, Medicare may not cover the exam. Per federal law, Medicare does not cover a refraction: the part of the exam where the doctor checks your glasses prescription.
