Welcome to Sight to See

Our passion is our patients

We pride ourselves on individual attention and personalized eye care solutions. We love seeing familiar faces and making new connections.

The retina is the only place in the body where blood vessels can be seen directly, so the Optomap helps our doctors spot early signs of glaucoma and macular degeneration, and of conditions like diabetes, hypertension, heart disease and stroke.

TearLab dry eye testing
50nanoliters

That’s all the tear sample the TearLab needs, smaller than the period at the end of a sentence. It measures the salt content (osmolarity) of your tears in both eyes.

The result tells our doctors whether you have dry eye disease and whether it is mild, moderate or severe, so treatment can be matched to you.

Questions

Good to know

Can’t find your answer? Call us at 405-341-7055.

What ages do you see?

We see patients ages 5 and up, and we greatly appreciate referrals.

Which insurance plans do you take?

We participate with VSP, Vision Care Direct, Medicare, Medicaid (Dr. Simon), Superior Vision, Humana Vision, Healthsmart, EyeMed (Dr. Simon), Blue Cross/Blue Shield and Vision Benefits of America. Check with our staff if you have other coverage.

What’s the difference between vision and medical insurance?

Vision plans cover routine exams, glasses and contacts, and a basic screening for eye disease. Medical insurance is used for eye health problems or systemic conditions with eye complications. If you have both, we may bill some services to each plan to keep your out-of-pocket cost as low as possible.

Does Medicare pay for my glasses prescription?

No. By federal law, Medicare does not cover a refraction (the part of the exam where the doctor checks your glasses prescription). Our charge for a refraction is $45.

Can I come in before work?

We open at 8:15 am Monday to Thursday and 8:30 am on Friday, and the doctors will often accommodate 8:00 am appointments if needed.

How can I pay?

Cash, personal checks, debit cards, American Express, Mastercard, Visa and Discover.
