Our doctors

One family, three optometrists

Dr. R.V. Simon began his practice in Edmond in 1971. His daughter, Dr. Raquel Strange, and his son, Dr. Scott Simon, now care for patients alongside him.

RS

Founder · since 1971

  • University of Oklahoma, B.S. Zoology
  • Southern College of Optometry, O.D.

Dr. Richard V. Simon, O.D.

Dr. R.V. Simon graduated from the University of Oklahoma with a Bachelor of Science in Zoology. He then attended Southern College of Optometry in Memphis, Tennessee, where he received his doctorate of Optometry and was included in “Who’s Who in American Colleges and Universities”.

He began his practice in Edmond in 1971. He was a member of the Rotary Club and active in the Chamber of Commerce, and he is a member of the American Optometric Association, the Oklahoma Association of Optometric Physicians and the Edmond Church of Christ.

Dr. Simon and his wife Lois have four children. He is an avid reader, enjoys running and video gaming, and is a huge fan of Oklahoma football and the Thunder. It has been his pleasure to care for so many patients and friends over the years.

Edmond Sun “Best Eye Doctor”, 2009

RS

Diplomate, NBEO (2012)

  • NSU Oklahoma College of Optometry, O.D., Summa Cum Laude (1998)

Dr. V. Raquel Strange, O.D.

Dr. Raquel Strange is a proud alumna of the Northeastern State University Oklahoma College of Optometry, where she graduated Summa Cum Laude in 1998. She achieved “Diplomate” status from the National Board of Examiners in Optometry in 2012.

She is a member of the AOA, the Oklahoma Association of Optometric Physicians, Beta Sigma Kappa International Optometric Honor Society, the Edmond Women’s Club and the American Diabetes Association.

Raquel is passionate about people and loves living and working in Edmond. She considers it a blessing to be in a profession that lets her help patients improve the quality of their lives, and she uses every opportunity to educate patients about their diagnosis and encourage active involvement in their health care. Raquel is the daughter of Dr. R.V. and Lois Simon; her husband Steve practices dentistry next door. She is an avid reader and likes to always shop in Edmond first.

Edmond Sun “Best Eye Doctor”, 2017

Dr. L. Scott Simon, O.D.

Dr. Scott Simon holds Bachelor of Science degrees from the University of Central Oklahoma (Biology) and the University of Oklahoma (Medical Imaging and Radiological Sciences). He graduated from the Northeastern State College of Optometry and reached his life-long dream of becoming a physician.

He is a member of the AOA, the Oklahoma Association of Optometric Physicians, the Alpha Eta Honor Society and the American Diabetes Association. Scott and his wife Amy are high school sweethearts (Edmond Memorial, 1990), and the family are members of Edmond Crossings Church.

He is the son of Dr. R.V. and Lois Simon. Scott has a kind and gentle approach and enjoys working with patients of any age.
