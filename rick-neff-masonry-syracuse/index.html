Family business

Two generations of Neff masons.

Rick Neff Masonry, Inc. is in its second generation of providing much of northern Indiana and southern Michigan with quality masonry construction work.

The company was founded by Rick’s father as a family business, and Rick E. Neff is now President. Commercial and residential brick, block and stone construction is what the company specializes in – run from the office on Syracuse/Webster Road in Syracuse.

Why masonry?

  1. Increases the value (and typically the resale price) of your home or business
  2. Offers increased protection against fire, termites, strong winds, airborne debris and earthquakes
  3. Structures can last hundreds of years
  4. Thermal mass in concrete block aids insulation and soundproofing
  5. Low maintenance
