Services

Cleaning, restoration & remodels.

From a single wine stain to a flooded basement and the remodel that follows. Here is everything Magic Carpet Services does for homes and businesses in North Idaho and Eastern Washington.

01

Residential carpet & upholstery

We do not use any chemicals: our method of cleaning is truck-mounted hot water extraction. We are a licensed applicator of Scotchgard carpet and upholstery protectant, and our services can be tailored to any budget.

02

Tile & grout

The tile and grout in your home collect grime and dirt over time and can become unsanitary. Light grout that used to set off the dark features of your floors gets darker with accumulated soil. Restoring floors to their original state takes powerful ingredients and determination, and that is what we bring.

03

Area, Persian & Oriental rugs

Your area rug is often the focal point of the room. Delicate synthetic rugs can be destroyed if cleaned conventionally. Magic Carpet Services has certified cleaners who know how to clean delicate rugs, using people- and pet-safe products that make your rugs vibrant again.

04

Pet odor & stain removal

Pets are our best friends, but their odors and marks take a toll on carpet and upholstery. Conventional cleaners clean the surface of your carpet; our professional cleaners get to the root of the problem to clear your space of unwanted odors and stains.
