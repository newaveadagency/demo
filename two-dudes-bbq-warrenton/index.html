Good to know

Questions

When are you open?

Tuesday through Saturday, 11 AM to 7 PM. Closed Sunday and Monday. Holiday hours can differ, so call 636-456-9922 before a special trip.

Where exactly are you?

612 N State Hwy 47 in Warrenton, MO 63383. From I-70 take exit 193 and go south on Highway 47; it’s the red building on the left.

Can I order meat by the pound for a party?

Yes. Pulled pork, brisket, turkey and ham by the pound with sauce included, sides by the pint or gallon, and cornbread casserole by the half or full pan. Plan on 3–4 servings per pound of meat, then call to order.

Can I eat there or pick up?

Both. There is dine-in and kerbside pickup.

Do you have kids’ meals?

Yes: a hot dog or a ham and cheese sandwich with chips and a drink, plus chips and cheese.
