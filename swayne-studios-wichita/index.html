About

Meet Ryan.

Ryan Swayne founded Swayne Studios in 2016, right around the time he was finishing college with a Bachelor of Fine Arts from Fort Hays State University.

He was a local high school and middle school art teacher for nearly 10 years before leaving teaching to run Swayne Studios full-time. His goal is to get as much art into other people's homes as possible, because each piece of artwork or photo taken captures a little bit of the artist's soul.

Good to know

Questions, answered.

How much does a photo session cost?

Most sessions (portraits, seniors, children, families, engagements, maternity, newborns, boudoir and pets) have a $275 session fee. Weddings are $275 per hour with a 3 hour minimum. Sports photography prices vary, so contact us for a quote.

Do I get the digital images?

Yes. You receive all digital images at the end of the photoshoot. If you plan to order prints, we kindly ask that you use our printing service through Nations Photo Lab.

Is the shoot in the studio or outdoors?

Either. Photoshoots can happen indoors with our backdrops and props, or outdoors at a location of your choosing. Not sure where a good outdoor spot is? Don't worry, we know plenty in the Greater Wichita Area.

Do you photograph school portraits?

Yes, at no cost to the school. Parents purchase photo packages directly from us, either on the Nations Photo Lab website with a code for each student or with a paper form. Photos arrive in about 2 to 4 weeks, and 30% of every package goes back to the school as a check.
