Book a time

2

Talk it through

Directions
Do I need an appointment?

Yes. Deep Alchemy Massage works by appointment only. Call (970) 640-8709 or send a request with the days that suit you.

When is the studio open?

Tuesday to Saturday, 9 AM to 8 PM. Sunday and Monday closed. Holiday hours may differ.

Which techniques does Racheal use?

Deep tissue, trigger point, Swedish and myofascial work, often blended in one session. Energy work is available for clients who want it.

I have never had a massage. Is that okay?

Absolutely. Racheal asks about your experience and where you are having problems, then explains what she will be doing before she starts.

How can I pay?

Credit and debit cards are accepted.

Where do I park?

There is a paid parking lot and paid street parking near 125 N 8th St.

Is the studio accessible and inclusive?

The studio lists a wheelchair-accessible car park and restroom and a gender-neutral restroom, and it is LGBTQ+ friendly and a transgender safe space.
