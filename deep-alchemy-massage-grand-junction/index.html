The alchemy

Strong, intuitive hands. Unhurried attention.

Every session starts with a conversation: where it hurts, what you have tried, and whether you have had a massage before. Then Racheal explains what she plans to do and works the muscles that need it, from tight neck and shoulders to old injuries that have limited your movement for years.

Clients describe her work as deep, focused and tuned in, with pressure adjusted to the body in front of her. Sessions can end with hot towels at the back of the neck.

The work

Techniques, blended to what you need

One therapist, one table, several approaches combined in a single session. Tell Racheal what is going on and she will build the session around it.

01

Deep tissue

Slow, strong pressure into the deeper layers of muscle. The session clients mention most, especially for neck, shoulders and back.

02

Trigger point

Focused work on the knots that refer pain elsewhere, so tension you did not know you were carrying can let go.

03

Swedish

Long, soothing strokes for relaxation and circulation, on its own or woven between deeper work.

04

Myofascial

Restorative, sustained stretching of the connective tissue to help stiff areas move more freely.

05

Energy & body work

For clients who want it, Racheal pairs hands-on bodywork with energy work and a calm, guided pace that helps you settle and let go.
