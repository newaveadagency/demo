01 The oven

Logs, brick and real fire.

Inside the oven at 150 Wakelee Ave

The sign out front says it plainly: wood fired brick oven. The pies bake on the oven floor right next to the burning logs, as the photos from the kitchen show.

Beyond the pizza there is a full kitchen: grinders, baked pasta, parmigiana dinners served with salad, bread and butter, stuffed bread and calzones.

07 Questions

Good to know.

What are your hours?

Tuesday and Wednesday 11 AM to 9 PM, Thursday 11 AM to 9:30 PM, Friday and Saturday 11 AM to 10 PM, Sunday 3 PM to 9 PM. Closed Mondays.

Do you deliver?

Yes, delivery is offered, along with dine-in and take-out. Call 203-734-6815 or use the online ordering page.

Do you have gluten-free pizza?

Yes. A gluten-free pizza is on the menu under NY Style Traditional Pizza.

Can I reserve a table?

Roma Pizzeria accepts reservations. Call 203-734-6815.

Is there parking?

Yes, there is a free parking lot and free street parking. The entrance, seating, restroom and parking are wheelchair accessible.
