Before you book

A few studio policies

Missed appointments affect our therapists significantly, so every booking is secured with a card and deposit.

50% deposit

A credit or debit card is required to book. A non-refundable 50% deposit is applied to your bill and transfers if you reschedule.

24-hour notice

Call or email at least 24 hours ahead to cancel. No-call no-shows and late cancellations forfeit the deposit.

Weekends fill fast

Book weekend appointments at least 2–3 weeks ahead. New clients: arrive 5–10 minutes early for a quick intake form.

Questions

Good to know

How do I book an appointment?
Book online on the studio's Vagaro page, or call (203) 250-0703. A credit or debit card is required to book any service.
Is there a deposit?
Yes. A non-refundable security deposit of 50% of the booked service(s) is charged at booking. It is applied to your bill at the end of your treatment and transfers if you reschedule.
What if I'm running late?
Late arrivals can't be extended beyond the scheduled time. New clients should arrive 5–10 minutes early to fill out a quick intake form.
How do body contour packages work?
Packages are paid in full before the first session and expire 90 days after purchase. A no-call no-show or late cancellation means losing that day's treatment; packages are non-refundable.
Can I bring my children?
Please arrange childcare before your visit. Children are not allowed in treatment rooms or alone in the waiting area, so come in for your treatment alone.
Do you sell gift certificates?
Yes. Click “Reserve a time”, then “Gift cards”. Certificates can be printed or emailed. To buy one in person, call ahead so someone is at the front desk to help you.
Is body sculpting right for me?
Some health conditions rule out certain treatments, for example pregnancy, breastfeeding, pacemakers or recent surgery in the area. See the full contraindications list and ask your specialist in a consultation.
