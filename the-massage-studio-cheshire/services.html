Contra­indications

People with heart, kidney or liver problems, or with an active implanted device such as a pacemaker or defibrillator, should not book body sculpting unless their doctor has approved it. Your clinician will advise in a consultation.

Ultrasound cavitation, RF & laser lipo
Not for anyone with an acute or contagious disease, HIV, a history of cancer, kidney or liver failure, fatty liver, implanted devices such as pacemakers or electrical prostheses, epilepsy or MS, or during pregnancy and breastfeeding. Active contagious skin conditions cannot be treated. It is best to wait three to six months after surgery in the target area.
Absolute contraindications
  • Pregnancy or breastfeeding
  • HIV/AIDS; cancer (all forms); chemotherapy or immune therapy; immunodeficiency; lupus
  • Uncontrolled high blood pressure or diabetes
  • Keloid scarring, excessive pigmentation, vein inflammation
  • Prednisone and other steroid medications
  • Chemical peels and laser resurfacing; sunburn
Partial contraindications
You may or may not be suitable for treatment; your clinician will advise.
  • Recent surgery or scars: wait 6 months
  • Open lesions, skin disease, infection, inflammation, varicose veins: area avoided
  • Prosthesis/silicone implants, metal implants or screws, metal contraceptive coil: area avoided
  • Antibiotics: wait until the course is complete
  • Depilatory creams, IPL or laser: wait 2 weeks
  • Botox: wait 3 months; dermal fillers: wait 2 weeks
  • Suntan: avoid sun for the whole treatment regime
  • Chronic inflammation: discuss with your doctor first
