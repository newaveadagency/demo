Good to know

Questions we hear

Which areas do you serve?

We're based on Pine Grove Road in Hortonville and serve Greenville, Hortonville, New London, Fremont and the Fox Cities.

Can you upgrade my electrical service?

Yes. From a full rewire or replacing an old fuse box to upgrading 100A to 200A service, or even installing 480V 3-phase.

Do you install and service generators?

Yes. We're a Generac dealer: we sell, install, service and perform warranty repairs on whole-house standby generators. We also install inlets so you can connect a portable generator, and remote monitoring can be added to your phone.

Do you do farm and agricultural work?

Yes, from grain dryers to lift stations. We can help farmers with any of their electrical needs.

Can you run power to a shed, pool or outbuilding?

Yes. Our trencher buries underground wiring for outbuildings, pools and sheds, and we have a locator to trace existing lines.
