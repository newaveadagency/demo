Youth bowling

Saturday morning youth leagues

Our youth leagues teach the lessons that come with being part of a team, and have been a favorite of young bowlers in the Lake Geneva area for many years. All leagues are coached by USBC Certified and USBC Registered Volunteer coaches.

  • Pee-Wee League · ages 4 to 8 · 4-person teams
  • Junior Youth League · ages 6 to 11 · 4-person teams
  • Senior Youth League · ages 12 to 20 · 4-person teams

The Bowling Centers Association of Wisconsin offers a Grand Prix Scholarship Program for youth bowlers. Ask us for the details.

Spring & summer

Kids Bowl Free

Through spring and summer break, registered kids ages 5 to 15 get two free games of bowling every day.

  1. Register each child at kidsbowlfree.com/LakeGeneva.
  2. Your free bowling passes arrive by email every week.
  3. Come in and bowl free all spring and summer.

Questions? Call (262) 248-4805.
