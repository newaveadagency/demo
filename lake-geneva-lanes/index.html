Our story

Rolling on Main Street since 1958.

The current owners are local residents who have lived in Walworth County for over 40 years. They take pride in the building and in keeping it clean, friendly and up to date.

1958

Built with 12 hardwood lanes

Ernest Witzke and several investors build Lake Geneva Lanes with 12 hardwood lanes and a lounge/bar.

1976

New owners, four more lanes

On May 3, 1976 the center changes hands to its current owners, who add four lanes, a tele-score system, a game room and a disco downstairs.

1986

Enter “50 East”

The downstairs club is updated and renamed 50 East. It runs until 2002, when it closes to put the focus back on bowling.

2006

The big renovation

The concourse is gutted: 16 new synthetic lanes, Qubica AMF automatic scoring, bumpers on every lane, new restrooms, cosmic bowling, new signage and LED TVs in the bar.

Today

A new arcade

The newest addition is a new arcade, full of racing games, claw machines and prize pushers.

Good to know

Before you come in

Can I bowl when leagues are running?

Usually, yes. There is open play nearly every day, but leagues bowl on weeknights and weekends in fall and winter, so call (262) 248-4805 to make sure a lane is free.

Do you take reservations for the $5 special?

No. The Thursday and Sunday special is first come, first served, and there are no specials on holidays.

When is cosmic bowling?

Friday and Saturday from 7 pm to midnight. Some leagues bowl every other Friday and Saturday, so on league weeks call for the cosmic start time.

Are there bumpers for kids?

Yes. All 16 lanes have automatic bumpers.

Which cards do you accept?

Visa, MasterCard, Discover and American Express.

Can you host a car or motorcycle club rally?

Yes. Lake Geneva Lanes would like to host your next rally. Send your club's details by phone or email.
