FAQ

Good to know.

Where does Tarrant Landscaping work?

Abilene and the Dallas–Fort Worth area in Texas.

How do estimates work?

Estimates are done virtually or in person. Call 817-975-2681 or send the estimate request on the home page.

Is there a deposit?

Yes. After you approve the estimate, a 50% deposit is required to order materials and schedule your installation date. The remaining balance is due when the job is complete.

How can I pay?

Cash, check, credit card, Venmo or Zelle.

Do you only install sod?

Sod and drainage are the main work, but the list also includes outdoor landscaping and design, fence and gate installation, concrete, and tree trimming and removal.
