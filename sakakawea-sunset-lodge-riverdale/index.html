Good to know

Questions guests ask

Are you open in winter?

Yes. We are open year-round for all of your fishing, ice fishing and hunting lodging needs.

Can I bring my dog?

Yes. Sakakawea Sunset Lodge is a pet friendly place to stay.

What is in the rooms?

Each room has a microwave, a refrigerator and a television. And wait until you see the bathrooms.

Which boats can I rent?

Pontoons, fishing boats and canoes. We even launch the boat for you. Call 701-654-7600 to reserve.

How far is the Garrison Dam Tailrace?

The lodge is a few minutes from Lake Sakakawea, a few miles from the Garrison Dam and Tailrace, and a little further from Lake Sakakawea State Park.
