§ 3 Our story

One family, since 1901.

Over the years our location and faces may have changed, but our commitment of superior service from our family to yours is still our top priority.

  1. 1901

    John Kennedy founds the agency

    Kennedy Insurance Agency is founded in Washington, Iowa.

  2. 1930

    Charles Kennedy joins

    John's son Charles joins the agency and continues to serve until 1974.

  3. 1958

    Robert “Bob” Kennedy joins his father

    Bob becomes owner and agent, and serves clients until his retirement in 2022. From 1962 to 2002 Gerald Hahn, son-in-law of Charles, was an integral part of the agency's success.

  4. 2004

    Nicole Peterson joins

    Nicole, great-granddaughter of Charles, proudly joins Kennedy Insurance as an agent.

  5. 2022

    A new owner from the same family

    Shortly after Bob's retirement, Nicole becomes the new owner of the agency.

§ 4 People

Who you'll talk to.

Nicole A. Peterson

Principal Agent / Producer

Nicole joined the agency in 2004. She graduated from Iowa State University in 2000 with a Bachelor of Science in Business Administration, majoring in finance and marketing.

Her prior experience in banking and human resources adds to what she brings to clients. She is active in the local community and is a member of the Washington Area Youth Supporters.

Robert A. Kennedy

Former owner 1958 – 2022

Bob joined his father in the insurance business in 1958 and built a vast insurance knowledge over the years. He was closely involved with local veterans organizations, especially the American Legion, and served on several service-oriented fraternities.

Bob retired in 2022 and passed away on November 24, 2024. May he rest in peace.

§ 6 FAQ

Good to know.

Q.01Do I need an appointment?

Yes for new clients. Appointments are required for new client quotes and consultations. Call (319) 653-3883 or use the form above to set one up.

Q.02Do you charge for a quote?

No. Quotes and consultations are free, by appointment.

Q.03What are your office hours?

Monday to Friday, 8 AM to 4 PM. The office is closed daily from 12 to 1 PM, and may also close for short periods during the day when we are out conducting business.

Q.04How do I report a claim?

For home insurance, call us first at (319) 653-3883. Grinnell Mutual, Progressive, Hagerty and American Modern claims can also be reported directly to the company. The Claims page lists every number and link.

Q.05What does “independent agency” mean for me?

We are not tied to one insurance company. Being independent gives us more freedom to meet your needs with one of our carriers, and we can also place harder-to-place risks through our brokers.
