Our story

A Gallatin clinic built on Dr. McMillan's motto

McMillan Veterinary Clinic carries the name of Dr. Jerry McMillan, and his favorite saying still guides how we work. In 2018, Dr. Brad Mitchell and his wife Brittney purchased the clinic, honoring Dr. McMillan's legacy.

Our mission is transparent, high-quality and compassionate care. Every pet's situation comes with different options, so we walk you through each one and build a plan around your pet's needs and your budget. Most of what your pet needs is diagnosed and treated right here in house.

Meet the doctors

Meet the veterinarians caring for your pets

Veterinarian · Owner

Dr. Brad Mitchell, DVM

A Bowling Green, Kentucky native, Dr. Mitchell knew he wanted to be a vet at age five. He earned his undergraduate degree from the University of Kentucky in 2008 and his DVM from Auburn University in 2012. His medical interests are orthopedics, physical rehabilitation and surgery.

He and Brittney have three children, Grady, Macklyn and Molly Ann, and three dogs, Brodie, Chase and Millie. Off the clock you'll find him cheering on the Auburn Tigers, fishing, hiking or at a Nashville music event.

Veterinarian

Dr. Hillary Kaufman, DVM

Dr. Kaufman graduated from Ohio State's College of Veterinary Medicine in 1979. She practiced in four other states before settling in Tennessee and joining the McMillan team in 1995.

Her medical interests include small animal medicine, weight management, nutrition and parasite control. Outside the clinic she enjoys karate and her many pets.

Good to know

Questions we hear a lot

What are your office hours?
Monday to Friday, 7:00 am to 5:30 pm. We're closed Saturday and Sunday.
What do I do if my pet has an emergency?
Call (615) 451-0522 right away, during or outside business hours. Outside business hours our on-call provider is available until 10 pm. For 24-hour emergency care we recommend BluePearl in Franklin, TN.
How much is an exam?
A physical exam is $65.00 for dogs and cats on our 2026 price list. Vaccines and tests are listed on our pricing table. Prices are subject to change, so please call to confirm current rates.
What does a bath include?
Bath and blow dry, brush-out, nail trim, ear cleaning, a bandana and a light scented cologne. Medicated shampoo, anal gland expression and de-shedding are available on request. Baths scheduled on Fridays are $10 off.
What can I bring when my pet boards?
Your pet's favorite toys, bedding, food and treats are welcome. Please leave the dishes at home: we clean and sanitize our bowls daily.
Can I order medications and food online?
Yes. Our online store handles prescriptions and food orders with home delivery.
