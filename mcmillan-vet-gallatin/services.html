Exams, vaccines & prevention

Annual examinations and vaccinations are the foundation of your pet's care. We also offer heartworm prevention, microchipping and nutrition support.

  • Annual examinations & vaccinations
  • Heartworm prevention & testing
  • Microchipping services
  • Hill's prescription diets & treats
  • Nutritional counseling
  • Home delivery of medications & special diets

Nutrition matters for pets as much as for people. Counseling helps pets who are underweight, overweight or living with chronic medical problems, by comparing their weight with what they eat and finding room to improve.

Advanced & laser surgery

Our hospital is fully equipped for advanced small animal surgery, so most procedures happen right here in Gallatin.

  • Surgical oncology
  • Abdominal surgery
  • Soft tissue surgery
  • Laser surgery
  • TPLO for ACL repair
  • Wound management
  • Minimally invasive surgery

Why CO2 laser surgery? As it cuts, the laser seals small blood vessels, so there is far less bleeding and the surgical site stays clearly visible. It also seals nerve endings and lymphatics, which means less swelling and pain afterward. The laser tip never touches the tissue, which lowers the risk of infection, and shorter procedures mean less time under anesthesia. Many pets walk, eat and play sooner than after conventional scalpel surgery.

Laser therapy

Therapeutic laser light helps the body heal. Used with or in place of medication, it can manage pain, reduce inflammation and speed up wound healing.

  • Chronic arthritis
  • Surgical incisions
  • Tendon & ligament injuries
  • Traumatic injuries

Dentistry

Every month should be pet dental month. A clean mouth with healthy gums limits the spread of bacteria to the heart, kidneys and liver. At-home care (brushing, dental chews) combined with regular professional cleanings under anesthesia keeps the mouth healthy and pain-free.

Periodontal disease starts when plaque moves under the gumline and causes inflammation (gingivitis). Caught early, gingivitis is reversible. Left alone, it can lead to bone loss and, eventually, lost teeth.

Is a pet ever too old for a dental cleaning? Never. Healthy older pets handle anesthesia well, and we recommend pre-operative testing (complete blood panel and CBC) for every patient.

In-house lab & digital X-ray

When a physical exam isn't enough to find the cause, our in-house laboratory gives answers fast, with most results in about 10 minutes. For specialized tests we partner with IDEXX labs for next-day results.

  • Urinalysis
  • Complete blood count & chemistry
  • Thyroid & electrolyte testing
  • Cytology, ear cytology, skin scrapings
  • Heartworm / Lyme / Ehrlichia / Anaplasma
  • Intestinal parasites incl. Giardia
  • FeLV & FIV testing
  • Pancreatitis testing

Digital radiology. X-rays help us find fractures, bladder stones, tumors, heart, stomach and intestinal problems, and swallowed toys or rocks. Digital imaging lets us see results more quickly and more clearly. Some pets are lightly sedated so they can relax; your veterinarian will talk this through with you first.

Orthopedic & senior care

Knee injuries, joint pain, arthritis, hip and elbow dysplasia and cruciate ligament tears can be very painful. Our veterinarians offer a range of orthopedic treatments, and McMillan Veterinary Clinic also works with a traveling board-certified veterinary surgeon who comes to our hospital to perform orthopedic surgery.

Pets are living longer than ever before. Our veterinarian will work with you to find the best treatment plan for your pet.

Boarding

Leaving your best friend behind is hard. Our boarding facility was built with your pet's safety in mind, with dedicated boarding staff and full-time veterinarians on site. We accept special needs pets.

  • Exercise and play time every day
  • Hand-walks
  • A clean kennel daily
  • Your pet's own schedule, food & medications
At check-in we'll ask about your pet's routine, feeding, medications and bathing. Bring favorite toys, bedding, food and treats, but leave the dishes at home: we clean and sanitize our bowls daily.

Bathing

Treat your pet to a spa day. Regular bathing keeps skin and coat healthy, cuts down on shedding and leaves your pet looking and feeling great.

  • Bath & blow dry
  • Brush-out
  • Nail trim & ear cleaning
  • Bandana & light scented cologne

On request: medicated shampoo, anal gland expression and de-shedding treatment.
