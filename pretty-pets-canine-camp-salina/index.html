Two dog houses

Room for the little ones
and the big ones.

The signs on the doors say it best. Small dogs and big dogs each get a house of their own, with suites indoors and chain-link runs and a fenced yard outside.

Little Dog House

The Little Dog House

The cream building with the red fire hydrant and the rose bush by the front door, made for smaller guests.

  • Indoor suites with A/C, fans, food and water
  • Beds, blankets and an armchair to curl up in
  • Dog doors out to the runs
Big Dog House

The Big Dog House

The brown cabin with the white double doors, set between chain-link runs. Big dogs are welcome: reviewers have boarded German Shepherds and German Shorthaired Pointers.

  • Chain-link runs on both sides of the cabin
  • Turf-floored runs in the shade of big trees
  • A good-sized fenced yard to stretch out in
