FAQ

Good questions.

Is the estimate really free?
Yes. Free estimates include a friendly conversation to gather ideas, an on-site design drawn to your liking, and an on-site bid.
Do you handle landscaping too, or only the pool?
Both. Greecian Pools is a full-service outdoor environment contractor: pools, waterfalls and water features, landscape, irrigation, concrete, outdoor kitchens, patios and lighting. You don’t have to coordinate multiple contractors.
Can I see the design before construction?
Yes. We create 2D and 3D landscape designs, photo-based designs and full 3D walkthroughs, so you can preview your backyard with lighting, shadows and reflective water.
What is a spool?
A spool combines a spa and a pool. It is usually about 10 to 16 feet long and 6 to 8 feet wide, with strong jets to swim against. Use it as a pool during the day and heat it up as a spa in the evening. It is a good fit for smaller lots.
Do you offer financing?
Pool financing is available through Lyon Financial. You can apply online at lyonfinancial.net.
Are you licensed?
Yes, Greecian Pools holds California contractor license #871523.
