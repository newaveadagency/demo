01 · Residential

Traditional or contemporary

As a C.H.I. Four Star Distributor we carry C.H.I.’s full residential line. Here are the styles you can compare in our Butler showroom:

  • 01Shoreline

    The look and texture of a traditional wood carriage house door, without the maintenance. 24 overlay designs and 2 Accents Woodtones.

  • 02Overlay Carriage House

    Dimension and texture of a classic carriage house door in steel, fiberglass or natural wood.

  • 03Stamped Carriage House & Shaker

    Steel embossed with a woodgrain finish – rustic barn-door character or clean shaker lines, short or long panel.

  • 04Raised & Recessed Panel

    Classic panel doors in short and long panel designs; recessed panel in 4 design options and multiple standard colors.

  • 05Planks & Skyline Flush

    Horizontal grooves for texture, or crisp flush lines with a geometric look – Skyline Flush comes in 13 color options.

  • 06Sterling & Full-View Aluminum

    Glass-forward modern doors: Sterling pairs infinity glass and steel in 36 colors; Full-View lets in natural light.

  • 07Accents Woodtones & Fiberglass

    Non-repeating wood grain on steel with dual-layer UV protection, or high-definition fiberglass that won’t shrink, warp or crack like wood.

Want to see a style on your own house? Try C.H.I.’s DoorVisions door designer, then bring your pick to the showroom.

03 · Rolling steel

Coiling doors, shutters & fire doors

Industrial rolling steel doors, coiling fire doors, fire shutters, counter doors, grilles and side-folding grilles for commercial and industrial buildings.

  • 01Rolling service doors

    Industrial-duty insulated and non-insulated coiling doors (models 6182, 6202, 6222, 6242) and medium-duty model 6241, in a range of slat profiles, gauges and colors.

  • 02Counter shutters

    The 6500 series (6522, 6544, 6566) with reduced-size slats for smooth, secure counter openings.

  • 03Rolling fire doors

    Guardian™ fire doors with U.L. label ratings from 3/4-hour to 4-hour, built for simple drop testing and reset.

  • 04Fire counter shutters

    U.L.-labeled models 7522 and 7566 using the same Guardian™ drop-test system in a space-saving design.

  • 05Grilles & closures

    Side-folding grilles from 10 to 110 feet, and the 9200 Easy Closure that fits a six-inch stud wall.
