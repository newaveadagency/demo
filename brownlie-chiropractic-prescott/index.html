Our approach

Simply put: we adjust with our hands, and we do it well.

“Chiropractic” comes from the Greek cheir (hand) and praktos (done). We hold true to the traditional methods of adjusting the spine and keep everything simple and straightforward.

01

Specific, hands-on adjustments

We use specific spinal adjustments to correct dysfunction of the joints of the spine, which then allows the body to heal and repair itself.

02

Continued care is up to you

Our approach does not include expensive long-term treatment plans. We will never force continued care on our patients.

03

Honest referrals

If we feel we cannot effectively treat you, we will tell you and give you the appropriate recommendations and referrals.

Questions

Good to know before you come in

Do I have to sign up for a long-term treatment plan?

No. Our approach does not include expensive long-term treatment plans. We believe ongoing chiropractic care has real value, but it is not a requirement in our clinic. Continued care is completely up to you.

Do you accept insurance?

Yes. We accept Medicare and most insurance. Call 928.445.9781 if you want to check your plan before your visit.

I was hurt in an accident. Can you help with my claim?

We will happily wait for payment on personal injury and accident claims.

Do you see children and pregnant patients?

Yes. Our procedures are used with adults of all ages, infants and children, and during pregnancy.

What kind of techniques do you use?

Our preferred method is the hands-on spinal adjustment. We also use decompression and soft tissue techniques, among others.

What if chiropractic is not the right fit for my problem?

If we feel that we cannot effectively treat you, we will tell you and give you the appropriate recommendations and referrals.
