06 Questions

Good to know.

When are you open?

Wednesday to Saturday 11 AM to 7 PM and Sunday 11 AM to 5 PM. Closed Monday and Tuesday. Call 501-580-7246 to check before you drive over.

Do you cater?

Yes. 1 meat, 2 sides, buns, pickles, sauce and utensils for $12 a person. Delivery is free within 10 miles with a minimum $100 order, and 48 hours notice is appreciated. Call or text (501) 517-1690.

Can I eat there?

Yes. Dine-in, takeaway and kerbside pickup are offered at 119 Country Club Rd in Sherwood.

How can I pay?

Credit and debit cards are accepted.

Is there parking?

Yes, there is a free parking lot and free street parking. The parking, entrance and restroom are wheelchair accessible.
