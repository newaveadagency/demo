Matted dogs

Humanity before vanity.

Has it been a while and now your dog is matted? Many groomers refuse matted dogs. Furry Friends will groom your matted dog without judgment.

If mats are tight to the skin, for example on the ears or tail, we’ll recommend shaving those areas. If a significant portion of the body is matted, we shave the whole pet at the safest length possible, sometimes to the skin. Sores can be hidden under matted fur, and nicks can happen because of how tight mats are. Please review the matted dog release form; an additional charge may apply, since it’s time-consuming and causes extra wear on equipment.

For the health and comfort of all pets, a fee may be applied if fleas or ticks are found.

Salon policies

A few things to know.

Vaccines

As of July 2025, Colorado’s PACFA program requires groomers to keep vaccination records on file before your pet’s appointment. Dogs: Rabies, Distemper, Parvo. Cats: FVRCP, Rabies. Email them to dduffy@furryfriendsco.com or bring them along.

Arriving

All pets on a leash or in a carrier when entering and exiting, and only one pet family in the lobby at a time, for everyone’s safety.

Appointment times

Please arrive on time and let us know if you’re running late. A late fee applies after 10 minutes; after 20 minutes the groomer may cancel at their discretion.

No-shows

Missed appointments without notice carry an additional fee. Continued missed appointments may mean we can’t take future bookings for your pet.

Pick-up

We’ll call you as soon as your pet is finished. Kennel space is limited, so please pick up within an hour; additional fees may apply after that.

Photos

We may photograph your pet for client files, our website and social media. Your personal information is never shared or sold.
