Our story

From a sheet-metal shop apprentice to one of the area’s top HVAC companies.

Owner Robert Taylor started as a duct apprentice in a sheet metal shop in the 1970s. In 1982 he opened Taylor Heating & Air, and the business has served the Little Rock area ever since, built on a reputation for excellent service, hard work, honesty and integrity.

Today the team takes on everything from an exhaust fan or a grille to hotels, hospitals, schools and office buildings, even nuclear power plants. We hire and train top-notch technicians and stay current on the latest equipment and trends.

“We won’t push expensive services or repairs, but will give you alternatives when possible.”

Questions

Straight answers.

Anything else? Call the office at 501-847-5035.

Which brands do you service?

All brands. We install and service Trane, Carrier, Goodman and Lennox residential systems, and we repair, replace and maintain every make and model.

Do you give free estimates?

Yes, estimates are free. Call 501-847-5035 or send the quote form below.

What does a seasonal tune-up cost?

A spring air conditioner tune-up or a fall furnace tune-up is $125 for the first unit and $25 for each additional unit. A standard service call is $95.

Is there an annual service contract?

Yes. For $200 a year (first unit, $25 for each additional unit) you get both the spring and the fall tune-up plus a 5% discount on any repair parts.

How far do you travel?

We are based in Alexander, in the Little Rock area, and take jobs anywhere in Arkansas, northern Louisiana and surrounding areas.

Do you handle commercial and industrial work?

Yes. We are a design-build HVAC contractor: we meet with you, assess your needs and run room-by-room load calculations so every system is sized right. We also install control systems, design refrigeration and run planned maintenance programs.
