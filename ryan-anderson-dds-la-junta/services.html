Your first visit

What to expect.

  1. Call to book

    Appointments are booked by phone at (719) 384-9442, Tuesday to Friday.

  2. Fill out the Welcome Sheet

    Patient information, responsible party, dental insurance and your health history.

  3. Bring your insurance details

    As a courtesy we bill your dental insurance for you. Your portion is due at the time of service.

  4. Moving your records?

    Fill out the Record Release form so your current x-rays can be sent to Dr. Anderson.
