Questions

Good to know before you come in.

Can’t find your answer? Call (719) 384-9442

How do I book an appointment?

Call the office at (719) 384-9442 to book. You can also send an appointment request with the form on this page, and it will open a ready-to-send email to the office.

When is the office open?

Tuesday through Friday, 8:00 AM to 4:00 PM. The office is closed Saturday through Monday. Hours are subject to change, so please call to confirm.

Do you bill my dental insurance?

Yes. As a courtesy, the office will bill your dental insurance for you. Your portion is due at the time of service.

What should I do in a dental emergency?

Call (719) 384-9442 to schedule an appointment. Please note that same-day treatment is not guaranteed.

Which forms should I fill out before my first visit?

Download the Welcome Sheet (patient information, insurance and health history) and read the Notice of Privacy Practices. If your records are with another dentist, fill out the Dental Record Release Authorization too.

Can my records be transferred from my previous dentist?

Yes. Use the Dental Record Release Authorization form. The information supplied is restricted to current x-rays unless more is specifically requested.
