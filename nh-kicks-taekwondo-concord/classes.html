Ages 6 and up

Family Taekwondo

Our classes help develop lifelong skills in a fun and positive environment. At NH Kicks we believe children and teens receive many benefits from studying traditional Taekwondo that go beyond simple self-defense moves and physical conditioning:

  • Improved focus
  • Learning new skills
  • Empathy & respect
  • Goal setting
  • Sense of community
  • Self-control & patience
  • Increased awareness
  • Improved academics

These benefits are more pronounced when parents get involved beside them, so we encourage and offer programs for families to practice, work and grow together.

Adults

Strong body, strong mind.

With all the stress of work and obligations, it is important to keep yourself both physically and mentally fit. The curriculum features four core components:

01Technique
02Language
03Forms

At each step you set goals and reflect on your journey through Taekwondo, improving day by day. Everyone can join and benefit.

4WEEK INTRODUCTION

Introduction to Taekwondo

For individuals and families of all ages who are new to martial arts or Taekwondo. As you exercise to improve your physical and mental wellbeing, you begin learning the stances, kicks and strikes used in schools around the world.

Friday evenings. Wear comfortable, flexible clothing; uniforms are not required.
