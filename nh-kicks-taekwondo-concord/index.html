Master Jung asks all students to arrive at least 5 minutes before the start of class. Here is who will be on the mat when you do.

Grandmaster · Kwan-jang-nim

Daniel Jung7th Dan WTF · 8th Dan Universal Martial Arts Federation

Master Jung is from South Korea and has been teaching Taekwondo for the past 30 years. In Korea he was a Special Guerrilla Military Trainer to all officers below the rank of General in the Navy Academy, Army and Marine Corps.

In America he opened his school, Alpha Taekwondo, in Chelmsford, MA, and helped set up schools in Dracut and Medford and internationally in Iceland and Panama. He taught after-school Taekwondo in all of Chelmsford's elementary and middle schools and at Rundlett Middle School in Concord, coached the Massachusetts state Taekwondo team (state gold medalists 1993–1997), and taught at New Hampshire Technical Institute and through the Concord Recreation Department.

He has taught thousands of students on five continents, and estimates over a thousand of them have earned at least a 1st Dan black belt.

NH President, USA Taekwondo Martial Arts Commission NH President, Korean American Taekwondo Association Fundraisers for Muscular Dystrophy, Easter Seals & Lutheran Services

Instructor · 3rd Dan WTF

Kurt Goldstein

More than 20 years of martial arts: ITF Taekwondo from age 14 at National Karate Institute in Summerville, SC, then Suenaka-Ha Aikido in college. He joined NH Kicks in 2015, started again from white belt, and became an instructor in 2026.

He also spent 12 years as an Olympic recurve archer and 5 years as a USA Archery certified coach.

Instructor · Sa Bum Nim

Thomas Hartley

Tom took his first self-defense class in 1975, then trained in Taekwondo and Judo before and after his enlistment in the U.S. Marine Corps, later with the New England Taekwondo Karate Club.

He joined NH Kicks in 2020 starting over from white belt, earned his black belt here in 2021, and now holds a 3rd degree black belt.

FAQ

Before your
first class.

Who can train at NH Kicks?

Everyone. Family Taekwondo classes are for ages 6 and up, and there are teen, adult and family classes. Parents are encouraged to train beside their kids: the benefits are more pronounced when the whole family gets involved.

I've never done martial arts. Where do I start?

With the Introduction to Taekwondo program: a 4-week introduction for individuals and families who are new to martial arts or Taekwondo. Classes are held on Friday evenings.

Do I need a uniform?

Not for the introduction program. Wear comfortable, flexible clothing; uniforms are not required.

What style of Taekwondo do you teach?

Traditional Taekwondo affiliated with Kukkiwon / World Taekwondo Federation. The adult curriculum has four core components: Technique, Language, Forms and Self Defense.

Where do I park?

Coming from Loudon Rd, the building is on your right with the entrance on the left side. Parking is outside the studio entrance near the large picnic table.
