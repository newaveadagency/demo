Our story

Built on word of mouth.

Climate Control is a family-owned business. Owner Brantley Johnson started it in 2006 after working in the industry for six years, with his wife Leigh and their three sons behind him.

He believed he could succeed by being honest, prompt and, most of all, by giving the best customer service in town. It worked: the business has grown with very little advertising, because our customers do the marketing for us. Climate Control was founded on Biblical principles, and we hope our work in the field reflects the God we serve.

Questions

Good to know

Which areas do you serve?

We’re located in Starkville and work all over the Golden Triangle (Starkville, Columbus and West Point) and in Louisville, MS.

Do you work on commercial buildings?

Yes. We handle residential and light commercial heating and air conditioning, including commercial repair and preventive maintenance.

What does the maintenance subscription include?

With an annual checkup subscription, our technicians visit your home in the spring and in the fall to make sure your system is working at peak performance.

Which brands do you install?

Amana, Rheem and Goodman heating and air conditioning systems. We’re also a dealer of White Mountain Hearth gas log fireplaces.

Do you offer financing?

Yes. Financing is available through Synchrony. Apply online.

Are you licensed?

Yes. Climate Control is licensed, insured and bonded.
