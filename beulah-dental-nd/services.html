In the office

Less waiting, less mess.

Same-day crowns. Since summer 2024 we mill crowns on site instead of sending them out to a lab, so a crown can be started and finished the same day, with no temporary to go home with. Some cases still go to the lab.

Digital impressions. Our Trios 4 scanner (since December 2022) takes digital impressions for crowns, implants and some mouth guards, a great alternative to impression material for many procedures.

Digital records & handheld x-ray. Records and x-rays on a monitor in every operatory, and a Nomad handheld x-ray that does not release back-scatter radiation.

Infection control

Protecting patients, staff and community.

Our instruments are sterilized in a steam autoclave that kills bacteria and viruses as well as bacterial endospores. Anything that cannot go in the sterilizer is thoroughly cleaned with a hospital-grade disinfectant, which we also use in common areas and treatment rooms.

All of our staff wear personal protective equipment, including masks, eye protection and gloves, during patient care. We follow current recommendations of the ADA, CDC and OSHA. If you have any concerns, please contact our office.
