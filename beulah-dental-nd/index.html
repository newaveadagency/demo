A Beulah native, caring for Beulah.

What's new in our office

Updated, step by step.

Since 2021 we have been bringing new technology into the office and giving it a fresh look, without major, costly renovations.

Summer 2021

Nomad handheld x-ray

A handheld x-ray sealed so that it does not release back-scatter radiation, which lets it be safely held by the person taking the x-rays.

November 2021

Digital records in every operatory

Computers and monitors in every operatory, so patient records and x-rays are viewed chairside instead of digging for a paper chart.

Summer 2022

Office makeover

New interior paint by Heather Horning, new flooring from Home Connection, and local photos by Casey Helling on our walls.

December 2022

Trios 4 digital scanner

Digital impressions for crowns, implants and some mouth guards: an alternative to messy impression material. Some procedures still need traditional impressions.

Summer 2024

Same-day crowns

We started milling crowns on site with a Primemill, so many crowns can be started and finished the same day, with no temporary and no waiting on the lab.

A local effort

Local hands, local views.

Our makeover was a local effort. Special thanks to Heather Horning for the painting, Home Connection for the flooring, and Casey Helling, whose local photos hang on our walls.

Questions

Good to know.

What insurance do you take?

For your convenience we accept and file all types of insurance, and we are participating providers of Delta Dental and Blue Cross Blue Shield.

When is the office open?

Monday through Thursday, 8 am to 5 pm. Call (701) 873-2259 to request an appointment.

Can I fill out my paperwork before my first visit?

Yes. New patient paperwork and the medical history update for existing patients are online. See patient forms.

Can I get a crown in one day?

Since summer 2024 we mill many crowns on site, so a crown can often be started and finished the same day. Some procedures still need to be sent to a lab.

Do you do implants, root canals and extractions?

Yes, they are part of our services, but select procedures may be referred to a specialist on a case-by-case basis.
