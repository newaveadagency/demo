Good to know

Questions we hear a lot

Do you work on homes or businesses?

Both. Mankato Landshapes is a residential and commercial landscape business serving southern Minnesota, from backyard patios to commercial grounds.

Are estimates free?

Yes. Landscaping and tree service estimates are free. Call 507-381-2678 or send the estimate form and we’ll set up a time to look at your project.

Do you offer snow removal?

Yes. Snow removal is part of our landscape services, alongside walls, pavers, plantings, grading and drainage work.

What kind of retaining walls do you build?

Concrete block, natural stone and timber retaining walls, plus accent boulders and boulder walls, designed and installed by our own crew.

Can you handle tree emergencies?

Our tree crew offers 24 hour service, with certified arborists, a 60' bucket truck and expert climbing for trimming, removal and stump grinding.

What fencing do you install?

Wood, chain link and vinyl fence (Bufftech), plus ornamental steel and aluminum (Ameristar and Fortress) for homes and businesses.
