Indoor summer storage

Indoor summer boat storage starts at $450 for the season. Storing with us also lets our technicians complete maintenance and repairs, clean the interior and buff & wax the exterior during the off-season.

How it works

From reservation to spring launch.

  1. Reserve

    Complete the Winter Storage Reservation/Agreement, the Winterizing & Fall Services form and the Payment Policy form, and return them by email, fax or mail.

  2. Deposit

    A $150 storage deposit is charged to your Visa or Mastercard on file when we receive the paperwork. We no longer accept checks.

  3. Winter

    Fall services are charged when completed, the storage balance on January 10. You’ll get an email in January or February about additional services.

  4. Spring

    Boats leave storage by appointment beginning April 1, with 7 days’ notice. Summer storage charges begin June 10 ($20/day) unless arranged in writing.

Your boat is not accessible while in storage. All service and storage accounts must be paid in full before scheduling spring pick-up or launch. Lessee self-insures; see the agreement for full terms.
