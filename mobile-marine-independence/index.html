Good to know

Questions boaters ask.

Which boats do you service?

Our technicians are specialized and only service MerCruiser® powered boats: MerCruiser engines, sterndrives and inboards, in any used boat brand.

Can winterizing be done at my dock?

No. Winterizing of the engine & drive, toilet/head, water system, air conditioning and generator is performed exclusively in our shop. Dockside service is a summer service.

Where do I drop my boat off for winter storage?

Drive it to North Arm, Maxwell Bay, Chapman Place, Spring Park, Lake Independence, Lake Sarah or Long Lake landing. Other landings are available for an extra $80, dock pick-up is available for an extra charge, and you deduct 15% if you bring the boat to us on a trailer.

Does outdoor storage include shrink wrap?

Yes. Outdoor storage includes shrink wrapping, and the shrink wrap is recycled.

When can I get my boat back in spring?

Boats are removed from storage by appointment beginning April 1. A 7-day notice is required for spring pick-up or launch.

How do I pay?

A current Visa or Mastercard on file is required. Storage needs a $150 deposit and the balance is charged on January 10. Checks are no longer accepted.
