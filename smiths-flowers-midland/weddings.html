Make them last

Flower care tips from our designers, for wedding flowers and every bouquet after.

Keep the vase filled, or floral foam soaked, with water containing the flower food from your florist.

If the solution turns cloudy, replace it entirely, and recut stems by one to two inches with a sharp knife.

Recut stems underwater so they draw in water instead of air, and remove leaves that would sit below the waterline.

Keep flowers cool (65–72 °F), away from direct sun, vents, ceiling fans, televisions and radiators.

Can't get them into water right away? Keep them in a cool place until you can.

Woody stems like quince, forsythia or lilac: cut with sharp pruning shears and place in warm water with fresh flower food.
