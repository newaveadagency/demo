The store on Ashman Street

A flower shop by Alden B. Dow

Smith's works from a building that Midland architect Alden B. Dow designed for the shop. Step inside and the store opens up on four levels, with a semi-circular pool at the front, a curved brick wall behind it and globe lamps hanging over everything.

Three generations of the Smith family have run the shop. Come in, look around, and talk to the people who make your flowers.

  • Display areas on four levels around the pool
  • Fresh flowers, plants and gifts under one roof
  • Storefront open Monday to Saturday

Good questions

Do you deliver to Bay City and Saginaw?

Not directly. There are some very good florists in both cities, and because of the cost of delivery we can't offer it. Call us and we'll give you the number of a servicing florist. Just tell them "Smith's sent me".

Can you special order a flower or plant?

Yes. If there is a particular flower or plant you would like for a special event, contact us and we will do whatever we can to help you obtain it.

Can I change my online order?

No problem. Email sales@smithsflowers.com or call 989-631-0470 with your order confirmation number. Please request changes by noon on the delivery date.

What payment do you accept?

Visa, MasterCard, Discover and American Express, or an established Smith's house account. Personal checks are welcome in the store.

Do you recycle vases?

Yes, we do. Box up the vases you've collected, no need to wash them, and drop them by the store. We'll keep them out of the landfill. Sorry, no milk glass.

Discounts for civic and non-profit groups?

We work with civic organizations and non-profits to offer discounts on bulk purchases of fresh flowers and plants for fundraisers and events. Bulk means box lots that require no labor on our part.

Why are you closed on Sunday?

Sunday is a day of rest and time with family and friends. We're glad to serve you Monday through Saturday, 52 weeks a year.
