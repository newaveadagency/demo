Engine control module experts of the Gallatin Valley

It’s easy to forget, but inside the hunk of metal you drive every day is a complex computer system. It keeps everything working, monitors your engine and fluids, makes sure your lights work and even plays your music.

Many computer problems come and go and are hard to replicate. If you suspect an electrical problem, it’s best to bring the car in so our staff can start running tests and get it fixed quickly.

Automatic, manual & CVT transmissions

Your transmission works constantly, transferring engine power to the drive axle and wheels through different gears — and takes a lot of wear over the years.

“Transmission” may sound expensive, but if you suspect anything is wrong, come by. Our technicians will talk you through any issues with transparency, so you know you and your car are in the best hands.

Telltale signsHesitation after pressing the gasVibration at certain speedsO/D light on the dashBurning smellsLeaksNew noises

Differential repair & replacement

Your differentials manage the speed of each wheel as you drive and turn. A strange noise coming from the axle may be your first warning sign.

Left too long, a broken differential can freeze up while you drive — you could lose control, and your axle, driveshaft and transmission could be permanently damaged. Call us with any questions about differential repair.

Motor replacement

Replacing a motor may sound like open-heart surgery, but it can give your car a second lease on life — and is sometimes cheaper than buying a completely new car.

If you hear any of these from your engine, come talk to us right away and we’ll find the best solution.

Signs of a failing motorSmoking tailpipeFluid under the parked carKnocking or tickingBuzzing, squeaking or whining
