Our story

From a six-room stop to a brand-new motel.

  1. The beginningThe Largo started out as a gas station and a six-room motel on the highway through Quemado.
  2. Several owners laterThe current owners bought the property and made it their own.
  3. 2008They tore down the old motel and built a new one: 20 rooms, decorated with furnishings from the Marriott Corporation.
  4. TodayThe motel, the Largo Cafe and a trailer court behind the motel for long- and short-term rentals.

Come be our guests and share the love we have for our business and our community.

Good to know

Questions before you drive in

How do I book a room?
Call 575-773-4262 with your card information. You can also send a reservation request below with your dates and party details, and we will get back to you as soon as possible.
What does a room cost?
The posted rate is $118 for a room with 1 King or 2 Queen beds. The price includes tax and is subject to change without notice and based on availability.
Can I bring my dog?
Yes, for a $25 pet fee. Pets need to be on a leash outside, and please do not leave pets unaccompanied in the room.
What is your cancellation policy?
We offer a 24-hour cancellation policy.
Is there room for my trailer or RV?
Plenty. The lot has room for hunters, fishermen and women, campers, RVs, SUVs, recreational and sports vehicles, as well as trailers.
Do you have longer-term rentals?
Yes. We have long-term and short-term single-wide trailer court rentals behind the motel. Call or use the form to ask about availability.
When should I book for hunting season?
As early as you can. During hunting season we fill up very quickly.
