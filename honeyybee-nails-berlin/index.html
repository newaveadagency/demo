Before you book

A few house rules, so every set comes out right.

  1. Send your inspo early

    Any ideas or photos need to be sent in a day before your appointment.

  2. New clients: come bare

    Please come in with no nails. No foreign fills or foreign soak-offs.

  3. 48-hour notice

    A $30 cancellation or rescheduling fee applies if an appointment is cancelled within 48 hours of the set time.

  4. No call, no show

    Results in a charge of 100% of the services booked, and you’ll be blocked from making future appointments on the booking site.

  5. Broken nails

    Quick repairs start at $5. More than 4 broken nails will result in a new full set.

  6. Soak-offs

    $15 with a new set, $20 without – includes nail clean-up and top coat. No foreign soak-offs.

Policies as listed on the HoneyyBee Nails Square booking page.
