Good to know

Questions & policies

How do I book an appointment?

All appointments are booked online through HoneyyBee Nails’ Square booking page. Choose your service, pick a time and confirm.

When should I send my design ideas?

Any ideas or photos need to be sent in a day before your appointment. You can email them to honeyybeenails@gmail.com.

I’m a new client – what should I know?

Please come in with no nails. No foreign fills or foreign soak-offs. You can book the New Client Full Set (from $65) or the $75 New Client Special – a short full set acrylic with French tips.

What is the cancellation policy?

A cancellation or rescheduling fee of $30 will be charged if an appointment is cancelled within 48 hours of the set appointment time. A no call, no show results in a charge of 100% of the services booked and a block from making future appointments on the booking site.

What if a nail breaks?

Book a Quick Nail Repair – it starts at $5, depending on the design. More than 4 broken nails will result in a new full set.

Can I get a soak-off?

Yes: $15 with a new set or $20 without a set, including nail clean-up and top coat. Foreign soak-offs (sets done elsewhere) are not offered.

Why do prices say “from”?

Full sets, fills and structured gel manicures start at the listed price. The final price varies with design complexity and length – crystals and charms can be added.

See open times and book online.
