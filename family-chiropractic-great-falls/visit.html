Your visit to Family Chiropractic.

A small, easy-going office where the doctor listens first. Here is how a visit with Dr. Pam works, when the office is open and what to know before you call.

Step by step

From the phone call to feeling better.

1

Call to set a time

Appointments are recommended. Call 406-727-6331 on Monday, Tuesday, Thursday or Friday to set a time that works for you.

2

Tell Dr. Pam what’s going on

Back, neck, an old injury, a fusion, a new ache after a fall: she takes the time to listen before she starts.

3

A full-body adjustment

Pam adjusts the whole body in one visit, not one section at a time. Patients describe the office as comfortable and easy-going.

4

Come back as you need

Some patients come weekly while they recover, others monthly for maintenance, others whenever something flares up. You decide together.

Hours

Mon & Thu 8:30 AM – 6 PM
Tue & Fri 8:30 AM – 3 PM
Wednesday, Saturday & Sunday closed

Holiday hours can differ. Call to check.

Payment

Credit and debit cards are accepted. Patients describe the care as “super affordable”. For insurance questions, call the office.

Wheelchair-accessible parking, entrance and restroom.

Questions

Before you call.

406-727-6331
Do I need an appointment?

Appointments are recommended. Call 406-727-6331 during office hours (Mon & Thu 8:30 AM – 6 PM, Tue & Fri 8:30 AM – 3 PM) to set a time.

Which days is the office open?

Monday, Tuesday, Thursday and Friday. The office is closed on Wednesday, Saturday and Sunday.

What payment do you take?

Credit and debit cards. If you want to use insurance, call the office first and ask.

Is the office wheelchair accessible?

Yes. Parking, the entrance and the restroom are wheelchair accessible.

Who will I see?

Dr. Pamela A. Blanchard, DC. Patients simply call her Pam. Kim runs the front desk.
