Meet your chiropractor

Dr. Pam knows your back by name.

Patients call her Pam. Dr. Pamela A. Blanchard, DC runs Family Chiropractic Clinic as a one-doctor office, and many of the people she sees have been coming back for years, some for decades, often with their whole family.

Her approach is what one patient calls “old school”: a full-body adjustment instead of charging for every section of the spine separately. She takes the time to listen first, and at the front desk Kim makes you feel right at home.
