Under the house

Termites get the blame. Moisture is often the culprit.

In a damp crawl space, the beams that support your home become vulnerable to brown rot, white rot and other wood decay fungi. Sealing the space with a moisture barrier stops the dampness, closes off entrances for pests and keeps mold and mildew down.
