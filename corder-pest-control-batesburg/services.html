Moisture control

In South Carolina we talk about rain a lot during our long, hot summers — but too much dampness in your home or crawl space can lead to big problems. If you have a crawl space, the beams that support your home are exposed. With moisture intrusion they become vulnerable to brown rot, white rot and other wood decay fungi. Termites usually get the blame, but the culprit is often fungi.

The solution: crawl space encapsulation. A moisture barrier seals the area off, greatly reducing the chance of wood decay, closing off entrances for pests and reducing mold and mildew. A clean, dry crawl space means a more sanitary foundation and fresher air inside your home.
