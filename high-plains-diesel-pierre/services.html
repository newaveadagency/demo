Questions

Good to know

Do you work on diesels or only cars?

Both. High Plains specializes in automotive and light-duty diesel engine repair: pickups like the Ram 2500, Chevrolet and GMC 2500, Duramax and Ford 6.7L, as well as cars and SUVs.

What are your hours?

Monday to Friday, 8 AM to 6 PM. Closed Saturday and Sunday. Holiday hours may differ.

Do I need an appointment?

Appointments are recommended. Call (605) 204-9042 or email highplainsdiesel@outlook.com with your vehicle and the problem.

Where are you?

3341 Airport Rd, Pierre, SD 57501. The lot has wheelchair-accessible parking.

What payment do you take?

Credit and debit cards.

Do you sell Titan fuel tanks?

Yes, Titan XXL fuel tanks are sold here. Call the shop to ask about the tank for your truck.
