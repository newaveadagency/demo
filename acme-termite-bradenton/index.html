Good to know

Questions, answered

Don’t see yours? Call 941-756-0074. Advice and 2nd opinions are free.

Do you charge for an inspection or estimate?
No. ACME offers free inspections, estimates and 2nd opinions for homes and businesses.
Do I have to tent my house for termites?
Not always. Depending on the type of termite problem, we offer Whole Home/No-Tent treatment, spot treatments, Termidor® liquid applications, termite baiting, borate applications and, if necessary, tent fumigation.
How often do you service my home?
Whatever fits your home and schedule: one-time, monthly, every other month, quarterly, or our popular 1-Time-a-Year Service with a 1 year warranty.
Do you offer natural treatments?
Yes. Whether you’ve decided to go green or just want an alternative, a natural approach is available for pest, rodent and termite control. Call the office to learn more.
What areas do you serve?
Sarasota and Manatee counties, including Bradenton, Sarasota, Ellenton, Palmetto, Ruskin, Lakewood Ranch, University Park, Holmes Beach, Anna Maria Island, Longboat Key, Bradenton Beach and Siesta Key.
How can I pay?
Visa, MasterCard, Discover, American Express, cash and personal check. Existing customers can also pay online through the ACME payment portal.
