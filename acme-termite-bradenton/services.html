03 Rodent control

Noises in the walls or attic?

If rats or mice have moved in, our trained technicians will come to your home or business, inspect for rodent activity, harborage and entry, and put together a plan, not only to remove the offenders but to make sure your structure is fortified so they can’t get back in.

How it works

  • Inspection for activity, harborage & entry
  • Trapping of unwanted rodents
  • Rodent proofing & exclusion
  • Recommendations to prevent repeat invaders

Roof rats are the most abundant rodent pest in Florida. They live in attics, soffits, hollow walls and out-buildings, and hollowed-out citrus is often the first sign.
