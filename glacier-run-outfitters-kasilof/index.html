What Glacier Run does

River days, a cabin on the bank, and fish that make it home.

Guided Kasilof River trips

Fish for sockeye and king salmon from Zac’s boat with his gear. Half-day charters and multi-day trips for guests staying a week.

Cabins on the riverbank

Clean, well-furnished rental cabins right on the bank of the Kasilof, with views of the river and the quiet of the outdoors.

About the cabins

Complete trip packages

Zac can set up the whole trip: lodging on the river, river fishing and a saltwater trip out of Homer with a halibut guide he recommends.

Fish care

Professional processing equipment on site, a deep freeze for each cabin, and help packaging your fish for traveling home.

A day on the Kasilof

Your guide
