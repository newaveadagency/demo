FAQ

Questions anglers ask.

Where is Glacier Run Outfitters?

At 24705 Amber Dr, Kasilof, AK 99610, on the Kasilof River on Alaska’s Kenai Peninsula.

What do you fish for?

Guided trips on the Kasilof River target salmon, including sockeye and king salmon. Call Zac for what is running on your dates.

Can we stay with you?

Yes. Glacier Run has rental cabins right on the bank of the Kasilof River, each with its own deep freeze.

Can you set up a halibut trip too?

Zac can put together a package with lodging, river fishing and a saltwater trip out of Homer with a guide he recommends.

How do we take our fish home?

Fish can be processed on site with professional equipment, frozen in your cabin’s deep freeze and packaged for traveling.

How do we pay?

Credit and debit cards are accepted.
