About C&K

Your neighbors with the paint brushes.

For more than three decades, C&K Painting Services has been making the greater Fredericksburg area more beautiful through interior and exterior painting and refinishing.

We are locally owned and operated by Charles and Kim Elliott, who not only grew up here but raised a family here as well. Since 1988, Charlie, Kim and their staff have been providing personal service from the beginning of each project through its completion.

Unsure of what direction to take your home? Let our interior designer provide guidance when choosing your color palette.

Questions

Good to know

Don’t see your question? Call (540) 898-6448 and ask Kim or Charlie.

Do you offer free estimates?

Yes. Call (540) 898-6448 or send the estimate form on this page and we’ll get back to you to set up a visit.

Are you licensed and insured?

Yes. C&K Painting Services is fully licensed and insured with a Class B Virginia Contractor License #2705014167.

What areas do you serve?

We serve customers from Northern Virginia to the northern Richmond suburbs, including Spotsylvania, Fredericksburg, Stafford, King George, Caroline, Hanover, Orange, Culpeper and Tappahannock.

Can you help me choose colors?

Yes. If you’re unsure what direction to take your home, our interior designer can guide you when choosing your color palette.

What paint do you use?

We proudly use Benjamin Moore Paints & Products.

Do you take on commercial work?

Yes. We offer residential and commercial services. Past clients include churches, medical and dental offices, an animal hospital, a bank, a fire department and Stafford County Government Offices.
