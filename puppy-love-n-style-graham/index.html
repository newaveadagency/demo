Good to know

Before your first visit.

A few shop policies that keep every pup safe and the schedule running on time.

Which vaccinations does my dog need?

We need proof that your pet is up to date before the first appointment; a typed receipt or record from your veterinarian with their name and phone number is fine.

  • Puppies (12 weeks): full set of puppy shots
  • Dogs: Rabies, Distemper & Parvo. Bordetella is preferred for grooming but not necessary.

Shots must be given 24–48 hours before the appointment. No grooming without proof of a current Rabies shot. We’re glad to contact your vet for you if needed.

Is there a fee to book a groom?

New and returning clients pay $25.00 at the time of scheduling. It goes toward your service that day. If you must cancel for any reason, the $25.00 goes toward the missed appointment and is not refunded.

Can I come in for grooming on a Wednesday?

Grooming is open Monday, Tuesday, Thursday and Friday. On Wednesdays only the daycare and the store are open. Please register for Wednesday daycare by 3:00 pm on Tuesday.

What if I’m late or need to cancel?

Please cancel or reschedule by 7:00 am on the day of your appointment so we can call someone from our cancellation list. If you are more than 20 minutes late, you may be asked to reschedule. If you no-show more than once, we may not be able to see your pet on high-demand days.

What about late pick-ups?

We call you when your baby is ready. A late pick-up fee applies if your pup is still waiting 30 minutes after that call past closing time. If you arrange a later pick-up with us because of your work schedule, the fee doesn’t apply as long as you arrive within 30 minutes of the agreed time.

Do you take walk-ins?

Yes, for nail trims ($8) and anal gland expression ($10). Painted nails and ear cleaning & plucking need an appointment.

My dog has a skin issue. Can I still come?

For the safety of our other clients, please don’t bring a pet with any contagious skin issue, disease, fungus or worms. If we find a problem, we’ll ask you to pick up your pet right away and pay for the portion of the groom that was completed.
