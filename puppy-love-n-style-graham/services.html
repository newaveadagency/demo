Certified poodle groomers

Pick your poodle look.

Poodle fur changes as the dog matures and needs specialized attention to keep a healthy coat and style. If your favorite style isn’t listed, just tell us what you’re looking for!

Lamb cut

Body clipped short and tight; legs scissored slightly longer so they look a little fuller. Suggested grooming every 4 to 6 weeks.

Puppy cut

An even length all over, hand scissored longer than a blade can cut, an inch or more. High maintenance: brush at home and groom about every four weeks.

English saddle

Like the continental, except the hindquarters keep their coat apart from a small curved area on each flank, the feet and bands on the legs, leaving four pompons.

Scandinavian

Face, throat, belly, feet and base of the tail are shaved, with a topknot on the head. The most common show cut in Europe, also known as the “Second Puppy Clip”.

German trim

Perfect for owners who don’t care for a traditional poodle style and want “something different” or “something less formal”.

Continental

Face, throat, feet and part of the tail shaved, pompons around the ankles and optional rosettes over the hips. The most popular show clip today.

Shop policies

The fine print, in plain words.

$25 scheduling fee

New and returning grooming clients pay $25.00 when booking. It goes toward that day’s service. If you cancel for any reason, it goes toward the missed appointment and is not refunded.

Vaccination records

Puppies (12 weeks): full set of puppy shots. Dogs: Rabies, Distemper & Parvo; Bordetella preferred for grooming but not necessary. Shots 24–48 hrs before the appointment. No grooming without proof of a current Rabies shot.

Cancellations

Cancel or reschedule by 7:00 am on the day of your appointment. More than 20 minutes late? You may be asked to reschedule. More than one no-show and we may not be able to see your pet on high-demand days.

Pick-up & contagious issues

A late pick-up fee applies 30 minutes after we call to say your baby is ready past closing time, unless you arranged a later pick-up. Please don’t bring pets with contagious skin issues, disease, fungus or worms.
