The studio

One seamstress, two wardrobes, every fitting in person.

Easy Fitting is Rutika's alteration studio on Chittenden Road in Clifton. Customers bring her everything from work pants and winter coats to engagement lehengas, and she is equally at home with Indian and Western clothing.

She listens to how you want a piece to fit, explains what she'll do, what it costs and when it will be ready, and then does exactly that. That is what her card means by promise made, promise kept.

Blouses can be altered or stitched new. Hems, waists, lengths and zippers are done on everything from jeans to beaded gowns.

How it works

Three steps, no guesswork.

Easy Fitting is a home studio, so a quick call or text first makes sure Rutika is there when you arrive.

Call, text or WhatsApp

Reach Rutika at 551-232-4283. If she's busy with a fitting, leave a text or WhatsApp message and agree on a time to drop off.

Try it on, talk it through

Bring the garment to 238 Chittenden Rd. She checks the fit, and tells you the plan, the cost and the ready date before she starts.

Pick it up, fitted

Collect your piece on the agreed day, measured and ready to wear.

Questions

Good to know.

Do you alter Indian clothing?

Yes. Ladies suits, blouse stitching and alteration, lehenga choli, top-skirt, pajama suits and saree fall are all on Easy Fitting's list, next to Western clothes like jeans, shorts, shirts, dresses and gowns.

Do you work on men's clothes too?

Yes. Easy Fitting does all kind of fitting for men and women: trousers, jeans, shirts, jackets and coats as well as dresses and Indian wear.

What if Rutika doesn't pick up?

Send a text or WhatsApp message to 551-232-4283 and she will get back to you.

Where do I drop off my clothes?

At 238 Chittenden Rd, Clifton, NJ 07013. It is a home studio, so call or text before you come to agree on a time.

What are the hours?

Monday to Thursday 9 AM to 5 PM, Friday 11 AM to 7 PM, Saturday 9 AM to 5 PM, closed Sunday.
