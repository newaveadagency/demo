Good to know

Questions we hear at the counter.

Do you fill propane tanks and sell kerosene?

Yes. We offer propane filling and kerosene (K1) at the store on Main Street.

Can you order something you don't have in stock?

Yes. We have many products in stock and we can do special orders. Call (207) 925-9052 or email info@lovellhardware.com.

Do you deliver?

Yes, we deliver. Call the store to arrange it.

How much is a dumpster rental?

We rent a 15-yard dumpster. Contact us by phone or email for pricing, and see what can and can't go in it.

Are you open on holidays?

We're closed on New Year's Day, Easter, Thanksgiving Day and Christmas Day, and close early on Independence Day (1 PM), Labor Day (4 PM), Thanksgiving Eve (4 PM), Christmas Eve (3 PM) and New Year's Eve (3 PM).

Do you sell animal feed?

Yes, we carry animal and livestock feeds. Ask at the counter for what's in stock.
