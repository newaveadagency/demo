Garden & potting soil

Soils, compost & peat.

Miracle-Gro® Potting Mix

For indoor and outdoor container plants; feeds up to 6 months. 1 and 2 cubic foot bags.

PRO-MIX® All Purpose Mix

More peat humus for stable humidity. For containers or straight into the garden – bedding plants, perennials, trees. 1.5 cu ft.

Coast of Maine Lobster Compost

Quoddy lobster compost, approved for organic growers by MOFGA. Chitin-rich shells make it a great vegetable and flower soil.

Coast of Maine Compost & Peat

Penobscot blend complete planting mix, MOFGA approved. For annuals, perennials, shrubs and trees, or conditioning beds.

Earthgro Organic Manure & Humus

General-purpose soil amendment for vegetables and flowers. Loosens heavy soils and improves moisture.

Premier Sphagnum Peat Moss

Aged, seasoned peat moss from Quebec. Absorbs up to 20 times its weight in moisture; 98% organic. Handled pick-up bale.

Northeast Gardener Farmyard Manure

A blend of manure, compost and peat.
