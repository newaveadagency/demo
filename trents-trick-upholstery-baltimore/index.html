The story

Turned away once. Never looked back.

In 1989 Trent Van Arsdalen took his Chevy S-10 to an upholstery shop and was told they “only did street rods.” So he did the work himself – and the business grew from there.

Since then the shop has built interiors for SEMA Show vehicles and has designed and installed interiors for General Motors and other SEMA members. And it's still Trent you talk to when you bring in a seat on a Tuesday.

  1. 1989Refused at a shop that “only did street rods” – Trent starts doing it himself.
  2. 1996Joins SEMA, the Specialty Equipment Market Association.
  3. 2001Debuts the shop's first SEMA Show vehicle.
  4. 2006Buick Lucerne LUX SS show build – interior and a rear spoiler designed and fabricated by Trent.
  5. 2014Extreme Sport project truck with A.R.E. Accessories – a top-10 Mothers Choice Award at the SEMA Show.
  6. TodaySeats, interiors and repairs from the shop in Baltimore, Ohio.

Good to know

Questions, answered.

Where is the shop?

6886 Thoreau Ln NE, Baltimore, OH 43105. Call (740) 808-2727 before you bring a vehicle or seats in.

What kind of vehicles do you work on?

Hot rods and street rods, classic and muscle cars, trucks, Jeeps, race cars and motorcycles – full interiors as well as single seats, door panels and trunks.

Can you repair a seat instead of replacing it?

Often, yes. One customer's Subaru Forester seat frame had a broken weld; it was repaired in two places instead of replacing the whole seat.

Can I just bring the seats in?

Yes. Seats that are already out of the car can be brought to the shop on their own.
